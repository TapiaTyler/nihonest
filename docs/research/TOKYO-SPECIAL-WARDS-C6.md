# Tokyo special wards: C6 address-registration batch

Source inspection: September 23, 2026. This batch adds Kita (`jp-13117`),
Arakawa (`jp-13118`), and Itabashi (`jp-13119`) to the address-registration
article. Their IDs were already in the J-LIS administrative registry. No other
article gains ward-specific guidance. All three supplements remain
`needs-review` pending human editorial approval; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

Standard notice hours are weekdays 8:30–17:00. Domestic moves require notice within 14 days and a moving-out certificate or applicable card-transfer route.
