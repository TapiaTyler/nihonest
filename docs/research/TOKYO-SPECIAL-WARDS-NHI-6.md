# Tokyo special wards: National Health Insurance batch 6

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `joining-national-health-insurance` for Kita (`jp-13117`),
Arakawa (`jp-13118`), and Itabashi (`jp-13119`). Tokyo special-ward coverage
is now 19/23; the other four wards retain clearly labeled general lookup
advice. All three new supplements remain `needs-review`; no Japanese
translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Kita | The [joining page](https://www.city.kita.lg.jp/living/insurance-pension/1001703/1001712/1001713.html), updated April 1, 2026, asks for notice within 14 days and gives counter-specific rules: ordinary notices after the event can go to the Qualification Section or the Oji, Akabane, or Takinogawa citizen offices; birth joining and qualifying advance notices go only to the main Qualification Section. It directs move-ins to raise insurance during resident registration. The [remote-procedure page](https://www.city.kita.lg.jp/living/insurance-pension/1001703/1001712/1001721.html), also updated April 1, lists electronic joining after workplace or dependent coverage loss and postal joining after workplace coverage loss or the end of public assistance. | Neither remote route covers a move or birth. Advance notice is limited to documented expected coverage loss and does not grant coverage or issue a certificate before the loss date. Foreign-resident eligibility depends on residence status and period of stay. |
| Arakawa | The [joining page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kenkouhoken/kokuho/kanyu.html) and [document and counter table](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kenkouhoken/kokuho/todokede.html), both updated August 28, 2026, ask for notice generally within 14 days and distinguish moves, coverage loss, birth, and foreign-resident filings. A move can use the main National Health Insurance and Pension Division or a citizen office; birth and foreign-resident filings go to the main division. The [online joining page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kenkouhoken/kokuhokanyuu2025.html), updated the same day, limits that form to loss of workplace or dependent coverage and explicitly excludes move-ins and births. The [postal instructions](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kenkouhoken/kokuho/kokuho_koronataisaku.html) ask residents to phone the ward for forms. | Do not infer that every joining event may be filed by post. Online applicants must be the joining person or a member of the same registered household. Foreign-resident eligibility and documents are case-specific. |
| Itabashi | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/kokuho/kanyu/1003126.html), updated August 31, 2026, asks for notice within 14 days after the event, generally rejects advance filing, and lists the main Qualification Desk or residents' offices. A domestic move by someone already in municipal insurance is normally handled with resident registration. The [postal procedure](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/kokuho/1022019/index.html), whose page does not state an update date, limits postal joining to loss of other health coverage and specifies four supporting documents and same-household filing. The [electronic-procedure page](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/kokuho/1061347.html), updated April 22, 2026, links to a joining form. | Do not offer postal joining for a move or birth. The electronic page does not identify eligible joining events, so the supplement directs readers to its current instructions rather than promising that the form covers every case. Foreign-resident status and documents need an individual check. |

The source pages are Japanese. The English supplements summarize their local
procedure boundaries and link directly to them; they are not official
translations. The next NHI batch in code order is Nerima (`jp-13120`),
Adachi (`jp-13121`), and Katsushika (`jp-13122`).
