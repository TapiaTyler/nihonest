# Kasugai City local guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-25. Official Kasugai pages were fetched with `npm run local-guidance -- fetch` and read from `.local-guidance/aichi-batch/` and `.local-guidance/location-kasugai/`. The [J-LIS Aichi municipal table](https://www.j-lis.go.jp/spd/code-address/tyuubu/cms_14414182.html) gives Kasugai's identity and reading; the [city population page](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/shisei/toukei/index.html) reports **304,813 residents on September 1, 2026**, above the 300,000 threshold.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | [Moving and resident-registration procedures](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/kurashi/todoke/1001889/jumin/index.html) | Filing after moving, City Hall and branch counters, overseas-arrival and foreign-resident documents. Foreign residents are directed to City Hall because branches cannot complete every case. |
| National Health Insurance | [Joining procedure](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/kurashi/zei/kokuho/1036321.html) | Domestic move and workplace-coverage-loss documents and counters. Ask the city about a direct overseas arrival and current residence-status eligibility. |
| Household waste | [District collection calendar](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/kurashi/1032702/1032853/1033276/index.html), [bulky-waste collection](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/kurashi/1032702/1032853/1033052.html) | The town list selects one of 17 districts; bulky waste requires a separate telephone booking. Check the announced October 2026 acceptance and fee changes before applying. |
| Resident tax | [2026 city and prefectural tax declaration](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/kurashi/1027725/shinsei/shinsei/zeikin/1009649.html) | Form, common filing exceptions, City Hall/postal/electronic routes for the cited assessment year. Later years and personal departure arrangements need confirmation. |
| Disaster preparation | [Flood hazard maps](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/shisei/machi/haisui/hazardmap/index.html), [earthquake and flood map hub](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/shisei/chizujouhou/index.html) | Open the district map for the exact address and use live warnings during an event. The undated map hub needs periodic review. |
| Language and consultation | [Foreign resident consultation](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/kurashi/1033189/sodan/gaikokusodan.html), [Kasugai Fureai Japanese classes](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/shisei/sankaku/kaigaiyuukoutoshi/1009611.html) | City Hall desk and beginner class route; check language days, current cohort, capacity, and costs. |
| National Pension | [Enrollment after retirement FAQ](https://www.city.kasugai.lg.jp/faq/1000411/1000419/1000951.html) | Category I retirement filing counters, documents, and Mynaportal route. The page does not establish direct overseas-arrival or exemption procedures. |

All seven supplements remain `needs-review`. No shared sources were added.
