# Iwaki local guidance research

Inspected: 2026-09-25. Research is at Iwaki City level. Iwaki is not a designated city, and its own pages direct residents to City Hall, branch offices, and citizen service centers; where a venue or collection calendar depends on the address, use the city's branch or district directory and confirm locally.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Registering your address after arrival | [Address changes](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000003066/index.html) (updated 2026-03-06): filing locations, counter hours, overseas-arrival documents, identity/authorization requirements, and multilingual checklists. | Describes the city's listed counters and document categories; individual household records and the station service-center hours for My Number Card moves need checking against the live city page. |
| Joining National Health Insurance | [Enrollment and withdrawal](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1452663797980/index.html) (updated 2025-08-01): event-specific records, shared identity requirements, counters, and LoGo forms for some procedures. | Does not decide eligibility or premiums and does not establish online enrollment for every event; recheck the current forms and proof requirements. |
| Sorting household waste and arranging disposal | [Household waste handbook](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000001842/) (updated 2026-03-19): current handbook, district collection calendars, standard bags, collection-point instructions, and correction notice. | Directs residents to current district calendar and handbook; does not reproduce each district's collection schedule or full disposal rules. |
| Income and resident tax after moving to Japan | [FY2026 resident-tax declaration instructions](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1633932822988/) (registered 2025-12-26): filing channels, district venues, applicability guidance, and needed records for that filing year. | The filing period and venue schedule are year-specific. Verify the live page for the applicable year and confirm individual filing requirements with the City Tax Division. |
| Preparing for disasters and evacuation in Japan | [Iwaki disaster map, 2026 revision](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000003880/) (registered 2025-06-01): river flood, tsunami, and landslide layers, district map sheets, evacuation-facility distinctions, and multilingual maps. | The map supports advance planning; detailed hazard maps and current event instructions determine more specific risk and live conditions. |
| Finding Japanese-language and local support | [Multicultural coexistence services](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000003148/index.html) (updated 2025-04-21): city consultation staff, daily-life information, Japanese classes, and links to Iwaki International Association. | The city page does not state service languages, operating hours, appointment terms, or class schedules and fees; confirm these with the linked provider. |
| National Pension after moving to Japan | [National Pension enrollment](https://www.city.iwaki.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000003008/index.html) (registered 2024-04-01): Category I enrollment after leaving employee coverage, records, local counters, electronic application, and Category III workplace route. | The page is dated and does not cover every overseas-arrival or exemption situation; confirm current instructions with Iwaki or the Hirano Pension Office. |

The summaries are Nihonest guidance based on Japanese official pages, not official translations. All records remain `needs-review`.
