# Nara City local guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-26. The sources below are official Nara City pages. Each supplement is citywide; where service or collection details depend on address, the resident should use the ward's applicable office or address lookup.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | [Resident procedures and counters](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/soshiki/17/10165.html), updated 2026-03-02 | Lists city counters, move-in documents, foreign-resident identity records, and the general filing period. Overseas cases and counter assignment require confirmation with the relevant office. |
| National Health Insurance | [National Health Insurance procedures](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/site/kokuminkenkouhoken/6089.html), updated 2025-04-01 | Gives event-based document examples and distinguishes move-related from other filing counters. Eligibility remains governed by the national guide and personal circumstances. |
| Household waste | [Collection calendars](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/site/gomi-syusyu/214555.html), [sorting guidance](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/site/gomi-syusyu/6254.html), and [bulky waste](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/site/gomi-syusyu/4464.html), updated 2026-08-24, 2026-07-03, and 2026-05-14 | Describes the address-area calendar lookup, sorting resources, and separate bulky-waste route. Schedule details are not copied; moving-related waste requires a separate route. |
| Resident tax | [Individual resident tax](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/soshiki/13/9860.html), updated 2025-11-20 | Directs residents to current-year filing information and describes the city's overseas tax-representative procedure. No tax amounts or individual liability determinations are repeated. |
| Disaster preparation | [Hazard maps](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/site/bousai-saigai/89308.html), updated 2026-06-26 | Directs residents to address-specific maps and current emergency information; map editions and evacuation facilities can change. |
| Language and local support | [Multicultural coexistence directory](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/life/2/16/378/) (directory lists life-support and Japanese-learning pages updated 2026-06-01 and 2026-01-22) | Establishes the city directory and its linked resources only. Languages, schedules, eligibility, and appointment rules are left to providers to confirm. |
| National Pension | [National Pension procedures FAQ](https://www.city.nara.lg.jp/soshiki/37/9259.html), updated 2022-03-29 | Identifies municipal, employer, and Japan Pension Service routes in examples. Because the FAQ is old, current forms, eligibility, and documents must be confirmed. |

All seven records remain `needs-review`; detailed steps and current office requirements should be checked before relying on them.
