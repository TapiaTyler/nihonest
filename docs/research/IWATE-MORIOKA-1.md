# Morioka City local-guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-26. Sources were fetched from Morioka City's official site and the saved Markdown files were read before writing the seven English supplements. Every record remains `needs-review`.

| Guide | Evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | [Moving-in notification](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kurashi/todokede/jumintoroku/1000394.html), [resident procedure counters](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kurashi/todokede/madoguchi/1000446.html) | Covers domestic moving-in filing, listed identity and residence records, and city counter options. Does not state a complete checklist for all overseas-arrival cases. |
| National Health Insurance | [National Health Insurance procedures](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kenkou/hokennennkin/kokuho/1003559.html) | Covers city filing period, common evidence, counters, and the specified postal and online routes. Does not determine eligibility for every individual case. |
| Household waste | [Sorting and disposal rules](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kurashi/gomi_recycle/gomidashikata/1001153.html), [area-based collection calendar](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kurashi/gomi_recycle/gomi/1018503/) | Directs residents to town-specific schedules and regional rules, with special items checked on the current city page. It avoids reproducing changing fees or collection dates. |
| Resident tax | [Resident-tax filing](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kurashi/zeikin/kojinshiminkenminzei/1049660.html) | Describes the city filing distinction and available online, postal, and counter channels. Individual liability, filing need, documents, and annual dates depend on the person's case and current tax year. |
| Disaster preparation | [Disaster maps and guide](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kurashi/anzen_anshin/bousai/hazardmap/1024098.html) | Directs residents to the map sheet for their neighborhood and city corrections. Map pages are planning references; current alerts and operational evacuation instructions govern during an event. |
| Language and local support | [Foreign-resident consultation](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/jigyousha/koyo/1041052/1056988.html), [Japanese-class FAQ](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/faq/1036934/1036935/koryu/1014016.html) | Identifies city and prefectural consultation options and a community class listing. The class FAQ dates to 2016, so current activity and arrangements require direct confirmation. |
| National Pension | [Category 1 procedures](https://www.city.morioka.iwate.jp/kenkou/hokennennkin/nenkin/1003597.html) | Covers common category 1 changes, records to prepare, online applications, and municipal versus Japan Pension Service routing. The national guide remains authoritative for category and eligibility. |

Registry identity was transcribed from [J-LIS Iwate municipalities](https://www.j-lis.go.jp/spd/code-address/touhoku/cms_12414180.html): Morioka City, code 032018, kana もりおかし.
