# Takasaki local-guidance research (batch 1)

- Inspected: 2026-09-25
- City record: `jp-10202`, Takasaki City. Registry identity transcribed from the J-LIS Gunma municipalities table; current population was not checked for this batch.
- Official city pages were fetched and read as saved Markdown under `.local-guidance/location-takasaki/`.
- The seven supplements are city-level guidance. Where collection or evacuation details depend on the address, the reader is directed to Takasaki's address/area lookup and relevant office.

| Guide | Evidence and claim boundary |
|---|---|
| Address registration | [Moving to Takasaki from overseas](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/2248.html) gives the filing period, documents, and available counters, with an explicit restriction for foreign-resident address changes at service centers. The supplement does not make claims about unusual family records beyond telling residents to confirm them with Citizens Affairs. |
| National Health Insurance | [National Health Insurance procedure list](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/3604.html) states the reporting period, event-specific evidence, counters, identity/My Number requirements, and proxy route. It does not estimate premiums or decide which coverage route applies to an individual. |
| Household waste | [Household waste sorting and disposal](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/2171.html) links to current fiscal-year information and notes area/association variation. No specific household collection day is asserted; residents must check the exact address and building rules. |
| Disaster preparation | [Takasaki hazard maps](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/3242.html) divides maps into five areas and identifies the map edition as March 2023, directing residents to the online system for current data. [Designated evacuation shelters](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/3346.html) lists city shelters and current map links. The supplement does not assert any specific address's risk or shelter opening status. |
| Language and local support | [Consultation Center for Foreign Residents](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/60923.html) describes multilingual municipal/life consultation; [Takasaki International Association](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/3320.html) identifies Japanese-learning support. The association page gives no current class schedule, so residents are directed to confirm availability. |
| National Pension | [National Pension procedures](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/3612.html) lists evidence for selected status changes and the city counter. The page is dated 2023-12-18; confirm current procedure and documents with the Pension Section or Japan Pension Service. |
| Income and resident tax | [FY2026 resident-tax notices and payment](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/72237.html) describes fiscal-year-specific notice and payment routes; [resident-tax declarations](https://www.city.takasaki.gunma.jp/page/3809.html) explains filing and postal/electronic preparation. The supplement avoids deciding an individual's filing obligation and flags current-year and departure-representative details for recheck. |

All records remain `needs-review`; no translation artifacts or shared sources were changed.
