# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 6

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Toshima
(`jp-13116`), Kita (`jp-13117`), and Arakawa (`jp-13118`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Toshima | The [moving procedure](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/116/tetsuzuki/nenkin/nenkin/2402090956.html), updated March 11, 2025, sends eligible overseas arrivals to the ward's pension counter but requires no separate pension filing for a domestic move by an existing Category I member. The [coverage-loss page](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/116/tetsuzuki/nenkin/nenkin/1603081336.html), updated February 2, 2026, sets a 14-day period for retirement or dependent-coverage loss and lists documents. The [postal page](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/116/tetsuzuki/nenkin/nenkin/2003041553.html), updated June 19, 2025, covers joining after employee or dependent coverage ends and asks callers to check relief-mailing requirements. The [online page](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/116/tetsuzuki/nenkin/nenkin/2506041543.html), updated June 11, 2025, lists acquisition and relief forms. The [relief page](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/116/tetsuzuki/nenkin/nenkin/008389.html), updated August 18, 2026, gives counter and supporting-document details. | The 14-day period and postal joining package are for coverage loss, not established as overseas-arrival instructions. The overseas-move page gives no deadline and presumes a pension-number record; a first-time enrollee without one should confirm documents with the ward. |
| Kita | The [procedure page](https://www.city.kita.lg.jp/living/insurance-pension/1001688/1001692.html), updated November 4, 2025, directs overseas transferees to register the move and then visit the National Pension Section; its separate coverage-loss instructions name residents' offices and the pension office and list event evidence. The [postal page](https://www.city.kita.lg.jp/living/insurance-pension/1001688/1001701.html), updated March 10, 2026, lists enrollment/category change, exemption, and student forms and asks applicants to confirm attachments first. The [exemption](https://www.city.kita.lg.jp/living/insurance-pension/1001688/1001694/1001695.html) and [student](https://www.city.kita.lg.jp/living/insurance-pension/1001688/1001694/1001696.html) pages, updated March 9 and 10, 2026, specify relief counters and student documents. | No first-arrival document list or electronic Kita route was established. The postal page lists the forms generally but does not say an overseas arrival can use them without checking the case with the office. |
| Arakawa | The [membership page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kokuminnenkin/todokede/kanyu.html) and [notification page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kokuminnenkin/todokede/todokede.html), both updated August 28, 2026, identify the main pension section and list evidence for retirement and dependent-status changes. The [postal page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kokuminnenkin/todokede/nenkin_koronataisaku.html), updated August 15, 2025, gives joining and contribution-relief forms through Arakawa Pension Office. The [electronic page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kokuminnenkin/todokede/denshishinsei.html), updated April 17, 2024, lists Category I and relief applications through My Number Portal. The [relief page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a031/kokuminnenkin/todokede/konnannatoki.html), updated August 28, 2026, gives student documents and the ward counter. | These pages do not specify a first-arrival document package, local deadline, or arrival-specific postal/electronic form. Confirm those details with Arakawa before using a general joining form for an overseas arrival. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. These English supplements summarize
local routes and boundaries, not official translations. The next National
Pension batch in code order is Itabashi (`jp-13119`), Nerima (`jp-13120`),
and Adachi (`jp-13121`).
