# Yokkaichi local-guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-25. [Yokkaichi's official population page](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1494552413550/) reports 304,980 people as of 2026-09-01, so it meets the major-city plan threshold. Sources are Yokkaichi City pages unless the hazard map is served from its disaster-information subdomain. Yokkaichi is researched at city level; its procedures and resources apply citywide, while collection sites and hazard details depend on the address.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Registering an address after arrival | [Overseas move-in notification](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1779925147562/index.html) | Covers overseas move-ins, named counters, a 14-day filing period, and different documents for Japanese and foreign residents. Does not replace the separate list for moves from another municipality. |
| Joining National Health Insurance | [National Health Insurance procedures](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000001160/index.html) | Covers event-specific evidence, identity and My Number documents, counters, and postal/online withdrawal after joining another plan. It does not establish individual eligibility or premiums. |
| Sorting household waste and arranging disposal | [Household waste information](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000001550/) | Directs residents to current neighborhood calendars, the waste guide, Sanaru search/app, and separate bulky-waste and appliance routes. No collection calendar or item-by-item rules are reproduced. |
| Income and resident tax after moving to Japan | [Municipal and prefectural resident tax](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000000609/index.html), [municipal tax payment information](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000000766/index.html) | Describes Yokkaichi's combined administration and points residents to current-year payment information. The cited payment page is older and tax notices and personal filing obligations need current, individual confirmation. |
| Preparing for disasters and evacuation | [Integrated hazard maps](https://bousai2.city.yokkaichi.mie.jp/hazard-map/hm-02/), [shelter opening and crowding information](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1614236260364/index.html) | Directs residents to address-relevant hazard layers and live shelter status. The shelter-service description is dated; current emergency directions supersede planning maps and old explanatory text. |
| Finding Japanese-language and local support | [Foreign-resident consultation](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000000868/index.html), [Japanese-language classes](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1631176508823/index.html) | Names the International Exchange Center and lists class providers. Availability, schedules, language support, and costs must be confirmed directly with providers. |
| National Pension after moving to Japan | [National Pension enrollment](https://www.city.yokkaichi.lg.jp/www/contents/1001000001173/index.html) | Distinguishes city-side Category I procedures from employer procedures and states that Category I enrollment can use My Number Portal. Specific relief applications and records should be confirmed with the city or Japan Pension Service. |

J-LIS administrative identity: [Mie municipalities](https://www.j-lis.go.jp/spd/code-address/kinki/cms_14514183.html), listing Yokkaichi code 242021.
