# Tokyo special wards: National Health Insurance batch 4

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `joining-national-health-insurance` for Ota (`jp-13111`),
Setagaya (`jp-13112`), and Shibuya (`jp-13113`). Tokyo special-ward coverage
is now 13/23; the other 10 wards retain clearly labeled general lookup advice.
All three new supplements remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Ota | The [joining and withdrawal procedure](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/kokunen/kokuho/kanyuu.html), updated September 1, 2026, asks the household head to notify the ward within 14 days. It lists the Qualification Section and special branch offices other than Kamata East, identity and My Number evidence, and event-specific joining documents. Postal and LoGo-form electronic joining are described for loss of workplace coverage. | Do not extend remote joining to a move or birth. Retirement or separation documents replace the qualification-loss certificate only under the page's stated conditions. The general branch list does not decide every foreign resident's move-in registration or insurance eligibility. |
| Setagaya | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/online_tetsuzuki/288.html), updated September 1, 2026, asks for notice within 14 days after the event and tells people moving in or reporting a birth to raise National Health Insurance during that registration. It lists the Qualification and Levy Desk, general branch resident counters, branches, and event-specific evidence. Postal and MyNaPortal joining are limited to departure from workplace insurance or a National Health Insurance union, including loss of dependent status. | Do not present post or electronic filing as a route for a move, birth, or end of public assistance. The [eligibility page](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/286.html), updated April 1, 2023, says a qualifying foreign resident outside the resident register must use the main National Health Insurance and Pension Section rather than a branch; confirm current eligibility with that office. |
| Shibuya | The [event-by-event notification table](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/kokuho/kokuho/kokuho_tetsuzuki_todokede.html), updated April 1, 2026, asks the household head to file within 14 days and distinguishes domestic moves, workplace coverage loss, births, and foreign-resident procedures. For a foreign resident, a required address change precedes the insurance procedure at the ward's National Health Insurance Section. The [postal and online page](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/kokuho/kokuho/yusotetsuduki.html), updated May 7, 2026, confines remote joining to loss of workplace coverage and limits online applicants to the person or same registered household. | Do not offer postal or online joining for a move or birth. The residence-card, Designated Activities, and coverage-loss evidence depends on the person's case; the [eligibility page](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/kokuho/kokuho/kanyu_hokensyo.html) lists exclusions but cannot decide an individual case. |

The cited procedure pages are Japanese. The English supplements summarize
their local procedure boundaries and link directly to the official pages;
they are not official translations. The next NHI batch in code order is
Nakano (`jp-13114`), Suginami (`jp-13115`), and Toshima (`jp-13116`).
