# Tokyo: Hachioji local guidance, batch 1

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. Hachioji City (`jp-13201`) becomes a selectable location in Tokyo's
Tama area (phase G1 of [LOCAL-GUIDANCE-COVERAGE-PLAN.md](../LOCAL-GUIDANCE-COVERAGE-PLAN.md)). This batch adds
supplements for six guides; all remain `needs-review`, and no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.
National Pension follows once Codex's Tokyo pension work, which edits `national-pension.ts`, is committed. Researched
by Claude with `npm run local-guidance` (fetch and `verify`).

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | The [moving-in page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/kosekijumin/002/001/p004460.html) and the [moving-in from abroad page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/kosekijumin/002/001/p004456.html), both April 20, 2026: within 14 days after starting to live in the city, not in advance; the person, a household member, or a representative with authorization; City Hall Citizens Division window 7 weekdays 8:30–17:00; Hachioji Station South Exit office weekdays until 19:00 and Sundays 10:00–17:00 for domestic moves, weekdays only for moves from abroad; City Hall for people with a disability certificate. Residence cards or Special Permanent Resident Certificates of everyone moving (or a passport noting later card issue), identity documents, My Number Cards held, the moving-out certificate; a relationship document with a translator-named translation for a foreign resident joining a foreign head. | The late-filing fine, other Citizens Department offices' hours, and Japanese-national documents are not reproduced. |
| National Health Insurance | The [joining page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkin/001/001/001/p003454.html) and [foreign residents page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkin/001/001/001/p003452.html) (December 2, 2024): 14 days; City Hall window 13 or a Citizens Department office except the crematorium office; residence card or passport, My Number Card, designation document for Designated Activities; coverage-loss certificates; notice handed over when the person or household member shows photo ID, otherwise mailed. The [late filing page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkin/001/001/001/p003468.html): tax charged back to when prior coverage ended; costs may be fully self-paid. The [online page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkin/001/001/001/p034869.html) (March 16, 2026): Mynaportal joining and leaving with workplace coverage changes; acceptance timing and about two weeks to show. | Eligibility exceptions, certificate expiry rules, and tax amounts are left to the national guide and the city page. |
| Household waste | The [basic rules page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gomi/kateigomi/dashikata/p002334.html) (April 1, 2026): out by 8:30 on the collection day from the town-name calendar, given at registration; blue and yellow designated bags; roadside on your own land for houses, the designated point for apartments; bulky waste means items that do not fit a bag or weigh 5 kg or more. The [bulky waste page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gomi/kateigomi/sodaigomi/p026308.html) (July 1, 2026): online any time or call center weekdays 8:30–17:15; up to 10 items; online payment by the day before or tickets; label items and put out by 8:30. | Bag prices and bulky fees are linked rather than reproduced. |
| Disaster preparation | The [page for foreign residents](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/shimin/004/004/gaikokujinnbousaitaisaku/p027285.html) (March 17, 2025): guidebook in English and Chinese; Japanese-only disaster e-mail and social media; English and Chinese Facebook; two evacuation methods. The [hazard map page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/emergency/bousai/m12873/001/p005647.html) (December 5, 2024): landslide zones, flood areas, shelters, water stations; the city does not answer whether a property is in a zone. | Shelter lists for earthquakes and storms exist on separate pages and are not listed. |
| Language and local support | The [consultation page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/shimin/004/004/gaikokujinnosoudan/p021016.html) (April 1, 2025): Foreign Resident Support Desk run by the Hachioji International Association, free, confidential; Monday–Wednesday, Friday, Saturday 10:00–17:00 with listed closures; Gakuen Toshi Center, 11th floor of the Hachioji Square Building. The [Japanese classes page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/shimin/004/004/nihonngokyousitugakusyusien/p021068.html) (December 1, 2023): exchange groups with contacts. | Languages are not named; they likely depend on available staff. Group contact details are left to the city page. |
| Income and resident tax | The [payment page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/zeikin/001/003/001/p012346.html): installments due at the end of June, August, October, and January; salary deduction June to May. The [filing page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/zeikin/001/003/002/p020553.html) (July 1, 2026): online or postal filing preferred; Citizens Department offices accept completed returns without advice. The [leaving Japan page](https://www.city.hachioji.tokyo.jp/kurashi/zeikin/001/003/002/p012354.html) (July 1, 2026): the tax does not change when moving mid-year; pay in full or appoint a tax agent; the form can be posted. | Rates, non-taxable thresholds, and deductions are not reproduced. |
