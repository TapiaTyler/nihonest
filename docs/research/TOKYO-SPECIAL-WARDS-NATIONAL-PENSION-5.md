# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 5

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Shibuya
(`jp-13113`), Nakano (`jp-13114`), and Suginami (`jp-13115`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Shibuya | The [procedure and counter table](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkin/shikaku-menjo/nenkin_tetsuzuki_todokede.html), updated April 1, 2025, lists overseas arrival alongside retirement and dependent-coverage loss as Category I acquisition events and gives ward, pension-office, and My Number Portal channels. Its coverage-loss document examples apply to retirement or dependent-coverage changes. The [relief page](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkin/shikaku-menjo/menjo.html), updated the same day, links to the applicable table for exemption and student filings. | The table gives no first-arrival document package or filing deadline. Branch and service centers generally do not accept foreign nationals' filings and do not take relief applications. No postal route is established by these pages. |
| Nakano | The [overseas-return FAQ](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/faq/kokumin/kokumin10.html) and [foreign-resident FAQ](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/faq/kokumin/kokumin23.html), both updated May 7, 2024, direct people needing Category I enrollment to City Hall's pension desk. The [coverage-change procedure](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/tetsuduki/20sai.html), updated May 15, 2024, specifies event evidence and postal/electronic routes after employment or dependency changes. The [exemption](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/nenkinhokenryonokoto/menzyo.html) and [student](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/nenkinhokenryonokoto/gakutoku.html) pages, both updated November 18, 2024, allow main-counter, pension-office, postal, and My Number Portal relief filings. | The return FAQ gives no remote arrival route, deadline, or first-time foreign resident document list. Do not transfer the coverage-loss postal route to overseas arrival. Regional offices accept specified coverage changes, not relief applications. |
| Suginami | The [overseas-move page](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s035/2043.html), updated December 29, 2025, directs eligible residents to enroll after move-in registration and lists identity and number evidence. The [foreign-resident page](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s035/2044.html), updated April 7, 2026, distinguishes main-counter enrollment from retirement filings also accepted at residents' offices. The [retirement](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s035/2041.html) and [dependent-change](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s035/2040.html) pages, updated April 1, 2026 and December 29, 2025 respectively, list event evidence and My Number Portal joining. The [relief page](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s035/2047.html), updated April 15, 2026, lists the main pension section, pension office, and My Number Portal. | The overseas-move page lists residents' offices for returnees, while the foreign-resident page identifies the main pension section as the general Category I joining counter; a first-time foreign resident should confirm the appropriate counter. The cited pages do not establish an electronic overseas-arrival form, postal relief route, or local deadline. Residents' offices are not listed for relief. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. These English supplements summarize
local routes and boundaries, not official translations. The next National
Pension batch in code order is Toshima (`jp-13116`), Kita (`jp-13117`),
and Arakawa (`jp-13118`).
