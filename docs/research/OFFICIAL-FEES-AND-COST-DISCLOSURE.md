# Official fees and cost disclosure

Prepared September 18, 2026 and reconciled with the independent external-research pilot on September 19, 2026.

## Finding

The catalog discusses budgeting and financial eligibility, but it does not consistently tell readers what a government procedure costs. This is a material gap because Japan changed both consular and residence-procedure fees in 2025 and 2026 and has published another major residence-fee revision for October 1, 2026.

The external pass substantially corroborated the repository research. The reconciled record below corrects three defects in that return: it restores MOFA's explicit no-fee rule when a visa is not issued, records MOFA's worldwide yen reference amounts, and retains the separate JPY 220 online processing charges for ordinary re-entry permission and certificates of authorized employment.

Amounts must not become isolated numbers pasted into prose. Every disclosure needs an effective date, charged event, responsible authority, payment channel, transition rule, material exception, and current official source. Future and historical schedules must be visibly labeled.

## Current and future immigration fees

Unless noted otherwise, these fees are due when permission or a certificate is granted, not merely when the application is filed.

| Procedure | Applications accepted from 2025-04-01 through 2026-09-30 | Applications accepted on or after 2026-10-01 | Important boundary |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Change of status, counter | JPY 6,000 | JPY 10,000 / 18,000 / 25,000 / 33,000 / 48,000 / 64,000 / 75,000 | Seven tiers based on the period granted: up to 3 months; over 3 through 6 months; over 6 months but under 1 year; 1 year; over 1 but under 3 years; 3 to under 5 years; and 5 years or more |
| Extension of stay, counter | JPY 6,000 | The same seven counter tiers | The granted period, not the period requested, determines the tier |
| Change or extension, online | JPY 5,500 | JPY 10,000 / 15,000 / 21,000 / 27,000 / 42,000 / 56,000 / 65,000 | The same seven periods; the processing charge below is additional |
| Online change/extension processing | No separately stated processor charge under the earlier revenue-stamp process | JPY 330 for a grant under 3 years; JPY 550 for 3 years or more | The charge is the same for convenience-store and bank payment |
| Permanent residence | JPY 10,000 | JPY 200,000 | Published reduction or exemption measures may apply |
| Ordinary re-entry, counter | JPY 4,000 single / JPY 7,000 multiple | Base fees unchanged | Keep separate from special re-entry permission |
| Ordinary re-entry, online | JPY 3,500 single / JPY 6,500 multiple | Base fees unchanged plus JPY 220 processing, for totals of JPY 3,720 / JPY 6,720 | Online filing is available only in the applicable residence-procedure context |
| Certificate of authorized employment, counter | JPY 2,000 | Base fee unchanged | Charged when the certificate is issued |
| Certificate of authorized employment, online | JPY 1,600 | Base fee unchanged plus JPY 220 processing, for a total of JPY 1,820 | Do not confuse the certificate with permission to perform an activity |

The application acceptance date controls the October transition. An application accepted by September 30, 2026 keeps the earlier fee and payment process even if permission or issuance occurs later.

### Payment, relief, and fraud prevention from 2026-10-01

- Counter applications continue to use revenue stamps.
- Eligible online procedures use convenience-store or bank payment after examination. Supported internet banking may be used; credit cards and QR-code payments are not supported in the published process.
- Published relief can reduce a qualifying change or extension fee to JPY 10,000 and a qualifying permanent-residence fee to JPY 20,000.
- A person seeking reduction or exemption must use a counter application because the online channel does not accept those requests. Nihonest must explain the route without deciding eligibility.
- ISA states that legitimate payment instructions for the new online process come only from an address ending in `@service-dgft.com`. A change to that domain, processor, payment channel, or processing charge warrants high-priority source review.

## Confirmed no-fee procedures

| Procedure | Treatment | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Special or deemed re-entry | No separate advance application or application fee | Eligibility and departure-card handling still matter; ordinary re-entry is a different paid procedure |
| Certificate of Eligibility | No ISA application fee | School, employer, document, delivery, translation, agent, and professional charges are separate |
| Permission for activity outside the current status | No ISA application fee | This does not establish that a particular activity qualifies |
| Acquisition of status of residence | No ISA application fee | This is distinct from a paid change or extension |
| Residence-card validity renewal | No separate card-renewal fee | Do not confuse card validity with extension of the period of stay |
| Residence-card replacement after loss, theft, or destruction | No ISA fee | The correct reissuance route and reporting duties still apply |
| Residence-card replacement after qualifying damage or defacement | No ISA fee | An optional exchange requested for other reasons is different |

From June 14, 2026, an optional residence-card exchange requested without a qualifying loss or damage reason costs JPY 1,900 when the replacement card is issued. Integrated residence/My Number Card cases require their current official table rather than one compressed amount.

## Consular visa fees

For visa applications accepted by Japanese diplomatic missions on or after July 1, 2026, MOFA publishes reference amounts of approximately JPY 15,000 for a single-entry visa and approximately JPY 30,000 for a multiple-entry visa. Payment is generally collected in the local currency of the country or region where the issuing mission is located. Nationality and purpose can change or eliminate the fee.

MOFA explicitly states that no visa fee is charged if a visa is not issued. This does not establish the treatment of every separate agency service charge, withdrawal, courier charge, or other private cost. An embassy-approved agency or visa center may charge a separate handling fee.

Local mission pages remain necessary. For example, the Embassy of Japan in Washington, DC publishes USD 101 for single entry, USD 201 for double or multiple entry, and USD 6 for an Indian passport holder under its schedule effective July 1, 2026; U.S. citizens are exempt. These figures are a post-specific example, not a worldwide dollar schedule.

## Variable-cost classifications and handoffs

| Area | Correct classification | Safe editorial treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Residence certificates and other municipal records | Local-variable charge | Link the responsible municipality; certificate, counter, mail, and convenience-store charges can differ |
| My Number Card | Initial issuance is free; materially different reissuance and integrated-card cases vary | Use current J-LIS, ISA, and municipal guidance rather than generalizing from one municipality |
| Foreign-license conversion | Prefectural or local-variable charge | Link the responsible prefectural police for application, examination, issuance, and payment details |
| Vehicle ownership | Government charges, taxes, compulsory insurance, and private prices | Label registration, inspection, parking certification, compulsory insurance, and taxes separately |
| School application, admission, tuition, and refunds | Private or institutional price and policy | Use JASSO for orientation and the institution's applicable academic-year terms for exact amounts |
| Income and resident tax | Tax liability | Filing is not a generic fee-bearing application; tax and payment certificates may have separate charges |
| National Health Insurance | Insurance premium or contribution | Amounts depend on municipality, household, income, and assessment year; enrollment is not a one-time application fee |
| National Pension | Monthly social contribution | State an amount only with its fiscal year and current Japan Pension Service source |
| Housing | Deposit and private contractual charges | Keep potentially refundable deposits distinct from key money, brokerage, guarantor, insurance, and move-out charges |
| Utilities, internet, mobile, and banking | Provider or local-utility price | Hand off to the selected provider's current tariff and contract terms |
| Bulky waste | Municipal or licensed local-service charge | Link the municipality and distinguish its process from licensed private collection |
| Lawyers, administrative scriveners, movers, interpreters, and agents | Private professional price | Disclose separately from government fees; there is no single national service price |

Priority 3 sources establish classifications and authoritative handoffs, not universal prices. Broad portal pages must not be treated as evidence for a precise provider, professional, prefectural, or municipal amount.
