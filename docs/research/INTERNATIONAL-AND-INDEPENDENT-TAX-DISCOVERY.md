# International and Independent Tax Discovery

Prepared and scope-approved September 12, 2026. This record defines Phase 13 Iteration 9B-2. The guidance remains educational and does not determine an individual's treaty, income-category, expense, consumption-tax, or departure result.

## Four focused decisions

1. **Cross-border income:** determine tax residence, income source, treaty procedure, foreign tax, and remittance facts rather than relying on the payer or bank location.
2. **Independent activity:** classify the actual income and maintain records while separately checking immigration, employment, licensing, and local permission.
3. **Consumption tax:** examine base and specified periods, elections, and qualified-invoice registration independently from income tax.
4. **Departure:** decide whether to appoint national and local tax agents or complete filings and payment before leaving.

## Safety boundaries

The guides must not promise that 183 days resolves a treaty, that foreign tax is automatically credited, that a freelancer may choose any income category, that a small business is always consumption-tax exempt, or that moving out closes later tax obligations. Each guide should tell the reader which records and authority are needed for the next determination.

All new content remains `needs-review` pending explicit editorial approval.
