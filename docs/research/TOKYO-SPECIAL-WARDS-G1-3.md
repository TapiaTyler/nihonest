# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Shinagawa, Meguro, Ota)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Shinagawa (`jp-13109`), Meguro (`jp-13110`), and Ota
(`jp-13111`), completing all seven guides for each. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts
were created or changed. National Pension (Codex) was not touched. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance`.

Shinagawa's pages carry a long language menu and navigation before the content; the reduced text is correct but the
content starts after the page's own `##` heading.
