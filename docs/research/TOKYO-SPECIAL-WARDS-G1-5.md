# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Suginami, Toshima, Kita)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Suginami (`jp-13115`), Toshima (`jp-13116`), and Kita
(`jp-13117`), completing all seven guides for each. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts
were created or changed. National Pension (Codex) was not touched. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance`.

Toshima's four supplements rely on its official English guide, Daily Life Information for Foreign Residents. Its garbage
page is dated September 17, 2020; recheck it against the ward's Japanese waste pages in a later review.

**Date-sensitive:** Suginami's Ogikubo community center returns as a flood shelter on October 1, 2026, and Kita's online
bulky waste payment starts on October 1, 2026.
