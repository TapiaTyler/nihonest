# Tokyo special wards: C4 address-registration batch

Source inspection: September 23, 2026. This batch adds Ota (`jp-13111`),
Setagaya (`jp-13112`), and Shibuya (`jp-13113`) to the address-registration
article. Their IDs were already in the J-LIS administrative registry. No other
article gains ward-specific guidance. All three supplements remain
`needs-review` pending human editorial approval; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Current official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Ota | The ward's [foreign-resident moving-in page](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/koseki_j/gaikoku/jusho_tetsuduki/gaikokujintennnyu.html), [domestic page](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/koseki_j/jyuminhyo/todokede/tennyu.html), and [overseas page](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/koseki_j/jyuminhyo/todokede/kokugaitennyuutodoke.html) require notice within 14 days after residence begins. Foreign residents can use the main office or eligible special branches, except Kamata East. A passport stamped to say a residence card will be issued later requires the main office. Overseas notices are excluded from night and weekend intake. The foreign-resident page lists qualifying status documents, passports, and possible translated relationship evidence. | The general overseas page directs foreign residents to their own detailed page; its generic counter rules alone are insufficient. Do not imply every overseas visitor qualifies for resident registration or that every special branch accepts the card-later passport route. |
| Setagaya | The [moving-in procedure](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02233/88.html) covers domestic and overseas arrivals, with filing within 14 days, in person at listed counters weekdays 8:30–17:00. It lists passports for overseas movers, residence cards or special permanent resident certificates for foreign residents, and possible translated household-relationship documents. The [Saturday service page](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02233/86.html) excludes overseas moving-in notices. | Some domestic notifications are possible on Saturdays; this does not extend to overseas moving-in. Family evidence depends on household circumstances, including whether an unchanged relationship is already confirmed on a domestic transfer certificate. |
| Shibuya | The ward has separate current foreign-resident [domestic](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gaikokujin/jumin/f_t_tennyu.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.shibuya.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gaikokujin/jumin/f_t_tennyu_kaigai.html) instructions. Both require notice within 14 days. The overseas page lists passports and residence cards, possible relationship proof, weekday 8:30–17:00 ward office or branch counters, and service-center intake only weekdays 11:00–17:00. Foreign-language help is at the main office 3F. | The domestic page's later service-center hours and Saturday availability do not apply to overseas arrivals. Existing-household consent and evidence after very late filing are conditional, not standard requirements for every newcomer. |

The detailed current pages are Japanese. The English supplements summarize
their route distinctions and link to those originals; they are not official
translations. C5 is now recorded in `TOKYO-SPECIAL-WARDS-C5.md`. The next
code-order batch is C6: Kita (`jp-13117`), Arakawa (`jp-13118`), and Itabashi
(`jp-13119`).
