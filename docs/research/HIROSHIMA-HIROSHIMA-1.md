# Hiroshima: Hiroshima City local guidance, batch 1

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. Hiroshima City (`jp-34100`) is researched at city level; the city's eight wards are administrative counters, and address-dependent filing and collection details should be confirmed with the ward office or branch serving the resident. The seven supplements remain `needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | The [domestic moving-in page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/koseki/1003055/1025560/1003292.html) and [foreign-resident moving-in page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/koseki/1025555/1016851.html), both updated April 2, 2026: file within 14 days at the ward resident section or a branch office (not a liaison office); foreign residents bring a residence card or Special Permanent Resident Certificate and, when required, official proof of relationship with a Japanese translation. | The cited city pages cover moves from another Japanese municipality. People arriving from overseas should confirm the process and documents with the ward office serving their new address. |
| National Health Insurance | The [enrollment, withdrawal, and other procedure page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/hoken-nenkin/1021143/1025565/1003463.html), updated February 4, 2026: the household head files within 14 days at the ward insurance and pension section or a branch; My Number information and identification are required, and employer coverage loss requires a coverage-loss certificate. Withdrawal after joining another plan can be filed by post. | The claim is limited to city processing and examples of documents; coverage decisions, premiums, and household-specific requirements are left to the national guide and ward counter. |
| Household waste | The [household-waste page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/gomi-kankyo/1021277/1003072/1026095/1026096/1003182.html), updated April 2, 2026, links to the 2026 annual guide and an alphabetical sorting index, identifies multilingual guidance, says collection dates vary by neighborhood, and announces noncombustible-waste changes from April 2026. | No full category list or fee is reproduced. Users should check current directions for the item and collection area, including their building's collection point. |
| Resident tax | The [foreign-resident tax page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/zei/1006016/1025578/1019139.html), updated September 7, 2026, says liability can remain after departure and directs residents to file tax-representative paperwork with the municipal tax office or tax room for their ward. | Personal liability, any current thresholds, and payment arrangements depend on the tax year and individual circumstances; this note does not calculate tax. |
| Disaster preparation | The [Hiroshima City disaster portal page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/saigaiinfo/saigai/1025420/1013395.html), updated February 16, 2025, links to ward and school-district alerts, hazard maps, and open-site status. The [evacuation-site page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/saigaiinfo/saigai/1021037/1013372.html), updated May 29, 2026, explains that only needed sites open in response to disaster conditions and alerts. | Site lists and live openings change. The supplement does not treat every designated site as open or suitable for every hazard. |
| Language and local support | The [foreign-resident consultation page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/tourism-culture/global-inbound/1021462/1021264.html), updated August 7, 2026, describes the city and Aki District multilingual consultation counter and additional language services. The [Japanese-classes page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/tourism-culture/global-inbound/1021462/1021256.html), updated February 16, 2025, links to community classes and a directory whose attached PDF is dated March 2022. | Consultation arrangements and class listings can change; users should contact the counter or class provider to check current availability and participation details. |
| National Pension | The [age-20 procedure page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/hoken-nenkin/1021145/1006017/1025653/1022598.html), updated February 20, 2025, directs residents who do not receive the Japan Pension Service enrollment notice to the ward insurance and pension section or a branch. The [Mynaportal procedure page](https://www.city.hiroshima.lg.jp/living/hoken-nenkin/1021145/1025575/1022603.html), updated February 16, 2025, lists electronic enrollment/category-change, exemption, and student payment-postponement applications. | The cited city pages identify municipal and online routes; national rules determine eligibility and contribution relief. Employer pension enrollment is handled through the employer. |
