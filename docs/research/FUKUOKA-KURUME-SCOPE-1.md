# Kurume G3 scope check

Inspected 2026-09-25. The [Kurume City current population page](https://www.city.kurume.fukuoka.jp/1100keikaku/2010shoukai/3040toukei/toukei.html), updated September 7, 2026 and fetched into `.local-guidance/location-kurume-scope/`, reports **298,148 residents as of September 1, 2026** (291,032 Japanese and 7,116 foreign residents).

The [local-guidance coverage plan](../LOCAL-GUIDANCE-COVERAGE-PLAN.md) lists Kurume provisionally because it was near 300,000. It is below the plan's threshold and is not Fukuoka Prefecture's largest city. The user explicitly requested that Kurume be included anyway, so its G3 location batch proceeds as a coverage exception. The exception does not change the general population rule for other cities.
