# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 3

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Sumida
(`jp-13107`), Koto (`jp-13108`), and Shinagawa (`jp-13109`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Sumida | The [Category I notification page](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/kurashi/kokuminnenkin/kokuminnenkintoha/tetuduki1.html), updated April 1, 2024, lists arrival after overseas residence, retirement, and loss of dependent-spouse status, with ward or branch counters for eligible changes. The [exemption page](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/kurashi/kokuminnenkin/genmenyuuyo/hokenryou_menzyo.html) and [student page](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/kurashi/kokuminnenkin/genmenyuuyo/nouhu_tokurei.html), both updated August 6, 2026, put relief applications at the main ward counter or Sumida Pension Office rather than branches. The [current filing-channel table](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/kurashi/kenkouhoken/tetuduki/tetudukihayami.html), updated September 17, 2026, lists counter, postal, and electronic routes for National Pension enrollment generally and for these relief applications. | The ward does not list an arrival-specific document package or a local filing deadline; do not substitute retirement evidence for an overseas move. The channel table does not establish that its remote enrollment forms cover overseas arrival, so confirm the form for that event. Electronic filing uses Japan Pension Service, whose operation and status questions the ward does not handle. Branches do not accept exemption or student applications. |
| Koto | The [overseas-return procedure](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/060306/kurashi/nenkin/kokumin/todokede/5211.html), updated May 27, 2026, gives the return-event evidence and lists City Hall, branches, or Koto Pension Office for eligible filings, but excludes a foreign national's first pension enrollment from branch offices. The [retirement procedure](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/060306/kurashi/nenkin/kokumin/todokede/5208.html), updated May 25, 2026, separately permits counter, postal, and My Number Portal filing after employee coverage ends. The [exemption page](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/060306/kurashi/nenkin/kokumin/hoken/5217.html), updated August 1, 2026, and [student page](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/060306/kurashi/nenkin/kokumin/hoken/5218.html), updated July 31, 2026, specify their own documents and remote routes; branches take only current-year relief or student applications. | Do not apply the retirement page's postal or electronic route to an overseas-return enrollment: that page does not state either route or a filing deadline. First-time foreign-national enrollment must not be directed to a branch office. |
| Shinagawa | The [joining and notification page](https://www.city.shinagawa.tokyo.jp/PC/procedure/procedure-nenkin/20191225091839.html), updated July 1, 2025, identifies the main pension section and listed regional centers for retirement, overseas return, and dependent-status changes. It asks returning residents for a pension-number record and passport, and directs foreign residents' enrollment to the ward pension section. Exemption, postponement, and student applications go to the main section, not regional centers; the page links electronic applications. | A person entering Japan's pension system for the first time should not assume the returning-resident document row is their complete checklist. The page does not establish a postal filing route or a local deadline. Check the Japan Pension Service form for each electronic event. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. These English records summarize
local routes and boundaries, not official translations. The next National
Pension batch in code order is Meguro (`jp-13110`), Ota (`jp-13111`), and
Setagaya (`jp-13112`).
