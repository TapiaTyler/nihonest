# Tokyo special wards: National Health Insurance batch 2

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `joining-national-health-insurance` for Bunkyo (`jp-13105`),
Taito (`jp-13106`), and Sumida (`jp-13107`). Including the earlier Chiyoda,
Chuo, Minato, and Shinjuku records, Tokyo special-ward coverage is now 7/23.
The other 16 wards continue to receive clearly labeled general lookup advice.
All three new supplements remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Bunkyo | The [qualification and notification page](https://www.city.bunkyo.lg.jp/b021/p000478/index.html), updated August 5, 2025, asks for notice generally within 14 days and directs a move or birth through resident registration before National Health Insurance enrollment. It lists identity and My Number evidence, authorization for an out-of-household representative, and event-specific documents. The National Health Insurance Qualification Section is at Bunkyo Civic Center, 11th floor south side. | Do not assume a resident is eligible merely because they registered an address. A lost employer-plan qualification certificate is required for that event, subject to the page's narrower retirement alternatives; foreign-resident documents depend on status. The page does not establish a general online joining route. |
| Taito | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.taito.lg.jp/kurashi/zeikin/kokuminkenkohoken/kanyusoushitsu/kokuhokanyute.html), updated December 24, 2025, asks for notice within 14 days after the event. It lists the ward office and eligible resident-service offices, but routes a person's first Taito resident registration after arriving from abroad to the Family Registry and Resident Services Section at the ward office. The page gives event-specific documents and postal joining conditions. | Do not route a first arrival from abroad to a general resident-service office or a district center. Postal joining requires requesting paperwork and including a health-insurance qualification-loss certificate; it is not a blanket option for every joining event. |
| Sumida | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/kurashi/kenkouhoken/kokuminkenkouhoken/kanyu/kokuhokanyu.html), updated July 28, 2026, asks for action within 14 days, lists joining-event and foreign-resident documents, and gives the ward-office and branch routes. It says postal and electronic joining are available for loss of workplace coverage or dependent status; neither remote route accepts an out-of-household representative. | Do not present remote filing as available for a move or birth. A foreign resident joining after moving from abroad generally uses the ward office, with the stated permanent-resident exceptions for branch filing. Check the current page for exact documents and eligibility. |

The source pages are Japanese. The English supplements summarize their local
procedure boundaries and link directly to them; they are not official
translations. The next NHI batch in code order is Koto (`jp-13108`),
Shinagawa (`jp-13109`), and Meguro (`jp-13110`).
