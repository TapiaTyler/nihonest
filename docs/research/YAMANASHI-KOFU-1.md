Direct readers to current address-specific schedules and separate appliance routes. |
| Resident tax | [Resident-tax filing requirements](https://www.city.kofu.yamanashi.jp/shiminze/faq/kurashi/zekin/noze/00202.html) | Summarizes who generally files and the listed supporting records. Tax-year channels, notices, and departure procedures require current individual confirmation. |

| Language and consultation | [Foreign-resident consultation](https://www.city.kofu.yamanashi.jp/shimin/tabunka/gaikokujinsoudan2.html) | Covers listed consultation languages, translation app, hours, location, and a linked Japanese conversation salon announcement. Service availability can vary; verify before attending. |
| National Pension | [Citizens Division and National Pension Section](https://www.city.kofu.yamanashi.jp/shimin/) | Identifies the responsible municipal section and counter location only. The city's captured page did not provide current enrollment/exemption checklists, so readers are directed to confirm details with the section or Japan Pension Service. |
