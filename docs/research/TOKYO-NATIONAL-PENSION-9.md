# Tokyo National Pension batch 9

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Tachikawa
(`jp-13202`), Fuchu (`jp-13206`), and Machida (`jp-13209`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Location | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Tachikawa | The [joining page](https://www.city.tachikawa.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/1002479/1002581/1002593.html) and [overseas-move FAQ](https://www.city.tachikawa.lg.jp/faq/1020019/1002625/1002878/1002905.html), both updated March 3, 2026, distinguish employer and dependent changes from enrollment after overseas transfer and describe local counters and documents. The [postal page](https://www.city.tachikawa.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/1002479/1002581/1002594.html), updated September 26, 2025, lists some forms mailed to Tachikawa Pension Office. The [relief page](https://www.city.tachikawa.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/1002479/1002579/1002587.html), updated August 4, 2025, lists contribution-relief documents. The [electronic page](https://www.city.tachikawa.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/1002479/1002581/1028128.html), updated August 20, 2026, lists My Number Portal application types. | The arrival FAQ gives an in-person counter route and number/identity examples but does not explain a first-time enrollee without a pension-number record. The postal and electronic lists do not identify an overseas-arrival form. Confirm service-center handling hours for the intended procedure. |
| Fuchu | The [notification table](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkinnotodokede.html), updated May 27, 2025, lists documents and local counters after workplace or dependent coverage ends. The [postal and electronic guide](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/kokuminnenkin/yuusou-densisinsei.html), updated July 1, 2026, lists the coverage-loss mail packages and My Number Portal acquisition and relief applications. | Neither page specifies first enrollment from overseas, its documents, or an arrival-specific remote route. The coverage-loss postal package should not be reused for a first arrival without confirmation from the Pension Desk. |
| Machida | The [overseas page](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/0914kokunen.html), updated October 22, 2020, directs a returner who has registered a Japanese address and is not otherwise pension-covered to join Category I at the city section or a citizen center. The [foreign-resident page](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/0308kokunen.html), updated April 7, 2026, says foreign residents handle Category I enrollment and municipality changes themselves. The [employment page](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/0304kokunen.html), updated July 23, 2026, lists coverage-loss evidence. The [counter page](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/toiawasesaki/0801toiawase.html), updated November 15, 2024, assigns joining and relief to the city section and citizen centers. The [exemption](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/nenkin_menjoseido/1001menzyosinnsei.html) and [student](https://www.city.machida.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/nenkin_menjoseido/1001menzyo.html) pages, updated July 23 and April 20, 2026, specify relief documents and channels. | The older overseas page describes returning residents rather than a first-time foreign-national document package. Confirm first-enrollment documents at the city section. The cited pages do not establish a postal or electronic overseas-arrival joining route. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. These English supplements summarize
local routes and boundaries, not official translations. This completes the
current 30-location National Pension coverage plan. These Tokyo records sit
before Nagoya in `national-pension.ts` to preserve administrative code order.
