# Saitama: Saitama City (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for Saitama City
(`jp-11100`) in [`locations/saitama/saitama.ts`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/saitama/saitama.ts). All sources
are Japanese pages; Saitama City's pages carry no update date, so dates below are not given. Resident tax returns go to
the Northern or Southern City Tax Office rather than ward offices. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese
translation artifacts were created or changed.
