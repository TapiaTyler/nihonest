# Tokyo special wards: C2 address-registration batch

Source inspection: September 23, 2026. This batch adds Bunkyo (`jp-13105`),
Taito (`jp-13106`), and Sumida (`jp-13107`) to the address-registration article.
The stable IDs were already in the J-LIS administrative registry. Only the
address-registration article gains local supplements for these wards; other
guides do not acquire ward-specific coverage. All three records remain
`needs-review` pending human editorial approval. No Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Current official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Bunkyo | The ward's [domestic](https://www.city.bunkyo.lg.jp/b013/p000263.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.bunkyo.lg.jp/b013/p000264.html) moving-in pages both require filing within 14 days after residence begins, in person at the Bunkyo Civic Center 2F. Community service corners do not take the notification. The overseas page lists passports, residence cards for foreign movers, and possible translated family records. | Do not apply Japanese nationals' koseki requirements to foreign residents. The ward's English form is not an English version of the detailed procedure. Office hours and exceptional cases must be checked on the live ward page. |
| Taito | The [resident-notification table](https://www.city.taito.lg.jp/kurashi/todokede/jyuminhyo/hikkoshi/jumintoroku.html) separates domestic and overseas arrivals. Foreign residents arriving from abroad file at the main Resident Services Division, while domestic arrivals may also use ward residents' offices or branches. It lists a 14-day period, passports and residence cards, and possible translated relationship documents. | Do not imply that a branch accepts foreign residents' overseas moving-in notices or that every foreign national qualifies for resident registration. |
| Sumida | The [current consolidated procedure](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/kurashi/todokede_syoumei/zyuumintouroku/zyuumin_touroku.html) requires filing within 14 days after moving in. Foreign nationals moving in from abroad use the main Ward Office only, weekdays 9:00–16:30; domestic moves can use the ward office or an eligible branch. The ward asks residents to confirm documents by phone before visiting. | A [February 2 FAQ](https://www.city.sumida.lg.jp/faq/kurashi/todokede_syoumei/zyuumintouroku/416.html) generically mentions branch offices for overseas moves. The more specific consolidated procedure, updated February 26, explicitly excludes branches for foreign nationals arriving from abroad; the supplement follows that rule. This restriction is not stated for every overseas mover. |
