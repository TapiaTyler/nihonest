# Aichi: Nagoya rewrite of the pilot records

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch rewrites Nagoya's (`nagoya`) five pilot supplements (address
registration, household waste, disaster preparation, language and local support, and income and resident tax) to the
current standard. Nagoya's National Health Insurance record was already current ([AICHI-NAGOYA-NHI-1.md](AICHI-NAGOYA-NHI-1.md));
National Pension is still missing. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts were created or
changed. Nagoya's 16 wards show these city supplements. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance`.

`verify` warns that the Nagoya International Center sources are not on the city's domain. The Center is the city's
international exchange body and was already cited by the pilot records.

Osaka's, so later Tokyo pension records go above Nagoya's (the order test enforces this).
