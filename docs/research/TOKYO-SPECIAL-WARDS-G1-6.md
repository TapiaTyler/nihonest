# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Arakawa, Itabashi, Nerima)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Arakawa (`jp-13118`), Itabashi (`jp-13119`), and Nerima
(`jp-13120`). Arakawa has all seven guides; Itabashi and Nerima still need National Pension (Codex). All records remain
`needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed. Date-sensitive items and old sources are
listed in [LOCAL-GUIDANCE-RECHECK.md](../LOCAL-GUIDANCE-RECHECK.md).

## Arakawa

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Household waste | The [sorting page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a025/moyasugomi-moyasanaigomi-dasikata.html) (July 29, 2026): guide in nine other languages; semi-transparent bags; branches to 30 cm; spray cans separately. The [bulky waste page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a025/recycle/sodaigomi/sodaigomi-2.html) (July 24, 2026): over 30 cm; online (10 items) or phone Monday–Saturday 8:00–19:00; Arakawa tickets only; out by 8:00 in front of the home. | Burnable waste times are in the guide PDF and not given. |

The [evacuation page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a013/bousai/suigainisonaete/hinannhouhou.html) (May 25, 2026): new alert-level warnings from May 29, 2026. | Earthquake shelters are not covered by a source in this batch. |
| Language and local support | The [page for foreign residents](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a001/seikatsu/for_foreigner_video.html) (September 24, 2026): living-guide videos, Japanese for now. | No ward-run multilingual desk was found; the supplement says so. |
| Income and resident tax | The [filing page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a012/zeikin/juuminzei/jyuuminzeinoshinkoku.html) (February 2, 2026) and the [tax agent page](https://www.city.arakawa.tokyo.jp/a012/zeikin/juuminzei/nouzeikanrinin.html) (August 20, 2026): foreign public pension recipients must file. | |
