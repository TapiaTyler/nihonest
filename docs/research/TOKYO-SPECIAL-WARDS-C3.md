# Tokyo special wards: C3 address-registration batch

Source inspection: September 23, 2026. This batch adds Koto (`jp-13108`),
Shinagawa (`jp-13109`), and Meguro (`jp-13110`) to the address-registration
article. Their IDs were already in the J-LIS administrative registry. No other
article gains ward-specific guidance. All three supplements remain
`needs-review` pending human editorial approval; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Current official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Koto | The ward's [domestic](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/060301/kurashi/jumin/idotodoke/5014.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/060301/kurashi/jumin/idotodoke/5021.html) instructions require filing within 14 days after beginning residence. Foreign residents arriving from abroad must use the City Office main building's 2F resident-record counter; Toyosu and other branches handle overseas arrivals for Japanese nationals only. Overseas notices are received weekdays 8:30–17:00, not during Wednesday extended or Sunday opening. | Do not transfer the domestic branch-office options to foreign residents arriving from abroad. The overseas source excludes foreign arrivals without an eligible residence-card status. |
| Shinagawa | The ward's [domestic](https://www.city.shinagawa.tokyo.jp/PC/procedure/procedure-zyuumin_inkan/procedure-zyuumin_inkan-zyuumin/procedure-zyuumin_inkan-zyuumin-todoke/tennyu20241020.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.shinagawa.tokyo.jp/PC/procedure/procedure-zyuumin_inkan/procedure-zyuumin_inkan-zyuumin/procedure-zyuumin_inkan-zyuumin-todoke/20241025kokugaitennyu.html) pages require filing within 14 days after beginning residence. The overseas page says foreign residents registering after new entry or re-entry are received weekdays until 17:00, excluding Tuesday extended and Sunday counters. It lists passports, residence cards, and possible translated family-relationship documents. | The general counter list includes some regional centers and scheduled Sunday operations, but their availability varies by case. The supplement directs readers to confirm the actual counter rather than promising every center accepts foreign overseas arrivals. |
| Meguro | The ward's [domestic](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/koseki/kurashi/hikkoshi/tennyu_mado.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/koseki/kurashi/hikkoshi/tennyuukokugai.html) pages require filing within 14 days after beginning residence. The overseas page lists the Ward Office and four district service offices on weekdays 8:30–17:00. Foreign residents bring residence cards; a foreign-only household that wants relationships recorded needs official relationship documents and translations. The page says registration can still proceed without those records, with relationships shown as cohabitants. | Do not present family-relationship proof as a prerequisite to registration itself, or apply Japanese nationals' koseki requirements to foreign residents. Exact supporting documents depend on the household. |
