# Tokyo special wards: C5 address-registration batch

Source inspection: September 23, 2026. This batch adds Nakano (`jp-13114`),
Suginami (`jp-13115`), and Toshima (`jp-13116`) to the address-registration
article. Their IDs were already in the J-LIS administrative registry. No other
article gains ward-specific guidance. All three supplements remain
`needs-review` pending human editorial approval; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Current official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Nakano | The ward's [domestic](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/kurashi/koseki/jyuminhyo/tennyu-kugaikara.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.tokyo-nakano.lg.jp/kurashi/koseki/jyuminhyo/tennyu-kokugaikara.html) moving-in pages require notice within 14 days after starting residence. Foreign residents arriving from abroad bring residence cards or special permanent resident certificates; a passport with a card-later notation is required, and passports can be checked in other cases. The Ward Office or regional offices accept notices. A group of three or more foreign residents moving together who are not one family or household needs a Ward Office booking three business days ahead. Sunday notices are held for next-business-day registration. | A family or one household of three or more is exempt from the group booking and may use a regional office. Sunday intake does not mean same-day registration or linked certificate and card processing. Do not require passports for every foreign arrival when the ward lists them conditionally. |
| Suginami | The [domestic](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s018/996.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.suginami.tokyo.jp/s018/10715.html) procedures require notice within 14 days after residence begins. The overseas checklist lists passports for all movers, foreign residents' residence cards or special permanent resident certificates, and original official relationship evidence with translator-identified Japanese translation for foreign residents. The Ward Office and residents' offices offer alternating Saturday schedules; residents' offices have Wednesday evening hours. The overseas page says first-time My Number assignment after 17:00 or on Saturday leaves resident-record processing incomplete that day. | Saturday and evening intake does not guarantee immediate completion when a My Number is being assigned for the first time. Japanese nationals' koseki requirements do not apply to foreign residents. |
| Toshima | The ward's [domestic](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/096/tetsuzuki/todokede/kiroku/000320.html) and [overseas](https://www.city.toshima.lg.jp/096/tetsuzuki/todokede/027375/027295.html) pages require notice within 14 days after moving in. Overseas arrivals bring all movers' passports and foreign residents' residence cards or special permanent resident certificates. Foreign-only households changing recorded relationships may need original evidence with translator-identified Japanese translation. The Ward Hall and East or West residents' offices accept overseas notices weekdays 8:30–16:00. | The domestic page lists later weekday hours and Saturday opening at the Ward Hall; do not carry those options over to the overseas route. Japanese nationals' koseki requirements are expressly waived for foreign residents. |

The detailed current pages are Japanese. The English supplements summarize
their route distinctions and link to those originals; they are not official
