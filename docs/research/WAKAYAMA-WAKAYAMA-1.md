# Wakayama City local guidance — first pass

Inspected: 2026-09-25. Official Wakayama City pages were fetched with `npm run local-guidance -- fetch`, and the saved Markdown was read. All seven supplements remain `needs-review`.

| Guide | Official evidence and claim boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Address registration | [Move-in notification](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/1007484/1009289/1010258.html): the city gives the post-move filing period, Citizens Division and service-center counters, and records for overseas arrivals. It excludes branch and liaison offices. |
| National Health Insurance | [Enrollment, withdrawal, and changes](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/kurashi/kokuho_nenkin/1001090/1001516.html): the city gives the reporting period, counters, representative rules, and event-specific document lists. The supplement does not decide eligibility. |
| Household waste | [Household-waste page](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/kurashi/gomi_kankyo/1001113/1001251/1018293/index.html) and [foreign-resident information](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/site/languages/1006056.html): these link schedules, sorting search, multilingual guides, and bulky-waste procedures. Collection points and days require the exact address. The waste page has no displayed update date. |
| Resident tax | [FY2026 declaration page](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/kurashi/zeikin/1001476/1029092.html): the city lists city and branch sessions, postal submission, document guidance, and late-filing effects. Dates and channels must be checked for each year. |
| Disaster preparation | [Hazard-map index](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/kurashi/bousai_bouhan_koutsu/1000032/1036426/index.html) and [foreign-resident information](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/site/languages/1006056.html): maps cover several hazards, while multilingual information includes alerts. Site-specific risks and actual shelter opening require current checks. The map index has no displayed update date. |
| Language and consultation | [City Japanese class](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/kurashi/bunka_sports/1045064/1045081.html) and [consultation information](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/site/languages/1006056.html): eligibility, application route, textbooks, and city consultation languages are stated. Class schedules and staffed languages are time-sensitive. |
| National Pension | [Pension procedure](https://www.city.wakayama.wakayama.jp/kurashi/kokuho_nenkin/1001091/1001538.html): the city lists arrival and category-change documents and relief routes. The supplement sends residents to the desk to decide the right category and documents. |

No shared-source addition was needed; each cited page is specific to Wakayama City.
