# Asahikawa City local guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-25. Official Asahikawa pages were fetched with `npm run local-guidance -- fetch` and read from `.local-guidance/location-asahikawa/`. The [J-LIS Hokkaido municipal table](https://www.j-lis.go.jp/spd/code-address/hokkaido/cms_12214179.html) identifies `012041`, 旭川市 / あさひかわし. The [city's resident-register population page](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/700/701/705/d055301.html) reports **309,393 residents on September 1, 2026**, above the 300,000 threshold.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | [Moving-in notice](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/800/840/841/p006573.html) | City and branch counters, filing period, domestic and overseas documents, residence cards, and proxy route. Confirm documents for unusual overseas or family cases with the Citizens Division. |
| National Health Insurance | [NHI notifications](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/kurashi/112/128/003/p004511.html) | Event-specific proof, identity and My Number documents, and postal route. The page does not separately specify direct overseas enrollment documents. |
| Household waste | [District calendar](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/kurashi/271/272/273/p005710.html), [new-resident waste guide](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/800/840/841/d055526.html) | Choose the exact district calendar and authorized station; bulky and moving waste use different routes. Do not carry forward the fiscal-year calendar without rechecking. |
| Resident tax | [2026 declaration instructions](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/kurashi/112/113/1141/d070999.html) | Assessment-year filing cases, documents, and electronic/postal routes. Personal tax liability and later-year rules require current-year confirmation. |
| Disaster preparation | [Consolidated hazard map](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/kurashi/320/kouzui/d065806.html) | Flood, inland water, snow, earthquake, outage, shelter, and digital-map links for planning. Check exact addresses and live alerts during an event. |
| Language and consultation | [Foreign resident consultation](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/1000/1001/sonota/sonota/p003603.html), [Japanese-study inquiry](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/1000/1001/sonota/sonota/p003602.html) | International Center and study inquiry route. Both city FAQs are from 2021, so confirm current languages, classes, and schedules. |
| National Pension | [Category change and enrollment](https://www.city.asahikawa.hokkaido.jp/kurashi/112/127/129/p006588.html) | City/branch route and documents for retirement or dependent-category changes. Direct overseas arrivals need case-specific confirmation. |
