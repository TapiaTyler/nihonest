# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Setagaya, Shibuya, Nakano)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Setagaya (`jp-13112`), Shibuya (`jp-13113`), and Nakano
(`jp-13114`), completing all seven guides for each. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts
were created or changed. National Pension (Codex) was not touched. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance`.

Shibuya's official English guide, Living in Shibuya, is cited as English sources. It carries no page date; the bulky
waste hours it gives were not used, because the ward's current Japanese page does not repeat them.
