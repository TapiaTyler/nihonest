# Chiba: Funabashi, Matsudo, Ichikawa, Kashiwa (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for four Chiba
major cities, each in its own location file under [`locations/chiba/`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/chiba/).
The registry gains `jp-12203`, `jp-12204`, `jp-12207`, and `jp-12217` (J-LIS). All sources are official city pages;
Matsudo's Japanese class page is the English page on its international portal. All records remain `needs-review`;
no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| City | Location file | Notes and claim boundaries |
|---|---|---|
| Funabashi (`jp-12204`) | [funabashi.ts](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/chiba/funabashi.ts) | Moving in from abroad is not accepted after 5 p.m. or on holiday openings; NHI coverage starts from the qualifying date; bulky waste online with PayPay or card; Multilingual Information Center in 12 languages; pension ends automatically on moving abroad. The tax page gives both FY2025 and FY2026 as the start of online filing, so no year is stated (gap). Old sources: the moving FAQ (November 18, 2023) and the Japanese class page (July 9, 2021). |
| Matsudo (`jp-12207`) | [matsudo.ts](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/chiba/matsudo.ts) | Moving-in counter hours, Sunday service center openings from July 2026, 1,000-yen bulky waste ticket, staged shelter opening in heavy rain, consultation in English, Chinese, and Filipino. The Japanese class page's English text says Monday to Friday and Sunday, but its Japanese text says Tuesday to Friday and Sunday; the Japanese is followed. Gaps: filing deadline, pension when moving abroad. |
| Ichikawa (`jp-12203`) | [ichikawa.ts](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/chiba/ichikawa.ts) | Arrivals file at the main Citizens Section or Gyotoku branch; NHI counters and same-day certificate; calendar in nine languages; large-item waste by phone, online, or LINE; 2026 filing period (tax-year recheck) and tax agent FAQ; consultation desks in 23 languages. The consultation page dates from April 10, 2024 (old-source recheck). |
| Kashiwa (`jp-12217`) | [kashiwa.ts](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/chiba/kashiwa.ts) | Arrivals file only at the Citizens Section; NHI documents; Kashiwa-area waste rules (the Shonan area differs); foreign-resident tax page; flood map (no tsunami or storm surge map); adviser consultation in four languages; overseas voluntary pension. The bulky waste fee is omitted because the waste page dates from January 20, 2022 and fees were revised (old-source recheck and gap). The Japanese class source is the city FAQ site, which redirects; the checker now keys pages by requested URL. |
