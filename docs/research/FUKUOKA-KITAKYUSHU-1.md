# Fukuoka: Kitakyushu local guidance

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. All seven Kitakyushu City supplements were added and remain `needs-review`. The city is researched at city level; ward offices handle address-dependent counters, waste collection schedules, and map details. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | [Overseas move-in procedure](https://ssl.city.kitakyushu.lg.jp/contents/14900055.html) (March 13, 2026): notice within 14 days at the ward office or branch for the new address; filer ID, all passports, and domicile-dependent family register documents; residence card or special permanent resident certificate if held; other-household proxy requires authorization. | Covers overseas move-in only; the destination ward or branch confirms household-specific documents. |
| National Health Insurance | [National Health Insurance overview](https://www.city.kitakyushu.lg.jp/contents/924_10011.html) (January 23, 2026): includes foreign residents with residence over three months and city registration subject to exceptions; event procedures are handled at ward counters. | No premium figures or assumption of eligibility; ask the ward counter about event-specific documents. |

| Resident tax | [Resident tax for foreign residents](https://www.city.kitakyushu.lg.jp/contents/08801108.html) (September 24, 2026): January 1 residency rule and departure-related tax representative information. | Thresholds and handling depend on tax year and individual facts; verify the current notice with the responsible tax office. |
