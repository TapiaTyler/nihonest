# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 1

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Chiyoda
(`jp-13101`), Chuo (`jp-13102`), and Minato (`jp-13103`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Chiyoda | The [National Pension procedure page](https://www.city.chiyoda.lg.jp/koho/kurashi/hoken/nenkin.html), updated April 1, 2026, lists the ward office National Pension Section and branch offices for retirement and dependent-status changes. It gives event-specific documents; contribution exemption or payment-postponement applications go to the main section only, while student applications can use the main section or branches. It also lists categories of electronic applications. | The page does not specify an initial-arrival filing package, a local filing deadline, or a postal joining route. The electronic application is linked to Japan Pension Service; do not present it as a ward-operated portal or assume every procedure is available at a branch. |
| Chuo | The [notification page](https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/a0024/kurashi/hokennenkin/nenkin/kokuminnenkin/todokede.html), published August 7, 2025, explicitly lists arrival from abroad and directs the resident to the Pension Section for required documents. It identifies City Hall and Nihonbashi, Tsukishima, and Harumi branches for eligible notifications, with a postal route. The [relief page](https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/a0024/kurashi/hokennenkin/nenkin/kokuminnenkin/mennjyo.html), published August 14, 2025, puts exemption, payment-postponement, and student applications at the main Pension Section or Chuo Pension Office, with postal and My Number Portal routes. The [electronic overview](https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/a0024/nenkin_dennsisinnsei.html), published August 5, 2025, points to Japan Pension Service for individual forms. | Do not invent documents for an arrival from abroad or send relief applications to a satellite branch. The electronic overview does not establish that every notification can be submitted online. Employer-handled category changes should go through the employer. |
| Minato | The [postal procedure](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/kokuminnenkin/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/yuusou.html), updated July 1, 2026, identifies the form and evidence for Category I joining after leaving employee coverage or spousal dependent status, plus separate exemption, postponement, and student forms. The [electronic procedure](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/kokuminnenkin/kurashi/hoken/nenkin/denshishinsei.html), updated June 26, 2026, lists My Number Portal applications for Category I joining after those coverage changes and for contribution relief and student postponement. | These pages do not establish that the same retirement documents apply to a first enrollment after arrival from abroad. Check the proper route with the ward. Applying for contribution relief does not guarantee approval. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. The English supplements summarize
local filing routes, documents, and boundaries; they are not official
translations. The next batch in code order is Shinjuku (`shinjuku`), Bunkyo
(`jp-13105`), and Taito (`jp-13106`).
