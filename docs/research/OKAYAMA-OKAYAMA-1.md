# Okayama: Okayama City local guidance, batch 1

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. Okayama City (`jp-33100`) is researched at city level; the city's four wards are administrative counters, and address-dependent details should be confirmed with the ward office serving the resident. The seven supplements remain `needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | The [moving-in notification page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000008759.html), dated January 26, 2012: residents moving into Okayama from another municipality file within 14 days; bring the moving-out certificate unless moving out through a My Number Card process, identification, and a proxy authorization when applicable. Ward offices, branch offices, and regional centers accept the notification. | The page is old and its checklist is for ordinary domestic moves. For an overseas arrival or special household documents, confirm the current list with the ward office. |
| National Health Insurance | The [enrollment and withdrawal page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000011305.html), dated November 12, 2018: joining and leaving are not automatic; procedures are accepted at ward offices, branches, regional centers, and citizens service centers. Enrollment after employer coverage ends requires identification and proof of loss; withdrawal after joining another plan can be filed by post to the ward office for the address. | The page is older; no premium figures or household-specific eligibility conclusions are included. |
| Household waste | The [household waste and recycling guide](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000054825.html), updated May 13, 2026, links to Japanese and multilingual sorting guidance. The [waste app page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000005082.html), dated August 31, 2016, describes address collection calendars, a sorting dictionary, notices, and facility maps, with English, Chinese, and Vietnamese display options. | No collection days or item-level fee amounts are copied; the app page is old, so users should check current local instructions. |
| Resident tax | The [foreign-resident tax page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000080393.html), dated March 25, 2026: municipality depends on January 1 residence, tax is based on prior-year income, direct-payment notices are generally sent around June, and someone unable to pay before departure should appoint a tax representative in Japan and file the declaration before leaving. | Amounts and individual liability are not determined; procedures and dates must be checked for the relevant tax year and with the municipal tax office for the address. |
| Disaster preparation | The [web hazard map page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/0000047403.html), updated August 20, 2026, covers flood/landslide, storm surge, earthquake, and tsunami hazards and can show nearby hazards, evacuation-site status, and routes. The [evacuation information page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000011520.html), updated June 18, 2026, explains that sites open when the city issues evacuation information and directs residents to current status. | Specific sites depend on address and hazard, and change during events; none are listed as universally available. |
| Language and local support | The [foreign-resident consultation page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000012926.html), dated June 3, 2019, describes a free city consultation counter and staff language support. The [Friendship Exchange Salon page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/shisei/0000012991.html), updated August 5, 2026, lists Japanese classes at the salon and two community centers. | The consultation page is old and schedules or service availability can change; verify languages, class times, and venue details with the provider. |
| National Pension | The [National Pension procedures page](https://www.city.okayama.jp/kurashi/0000011539.html), dated April 1, 2024: ward offices handle municipal filings for relevant category changes; My Number-linked address changes generally need no filing, otherwise report within 14 days. | Eligibility, employee pension enrollment, benefits, and contribution relief remain governed by national rules; confirm category and required documents with the ward counter. |
