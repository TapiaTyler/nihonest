# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 2

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Shinjuku
(`shinjuku`), Bunkyo (`jp-13105`), and Taito (`jp-13106`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Shinjuku | The [foreign-resident pension page](https://www.foreign.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/jp/kurashi/kokuminnenkin/) (no page date stated) directs residents first registered at age 20 or later to file through their municipality. The [joining and category-change page](https://www.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/hoken/file03_02_00001.html), updated July 14, 2025, identifies the main Pension Section and special branch offices for eligible coverage changes, with retirement/dependent-loss documents and postal or electronic routes. The [relief page](https://www.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/hoken/file03_03_00001.html), updated July 1, 2026, handles exemption, postponement, and student applications at the main section or remotely. The [identity guidance](https://www.city.shinjuku.lg.jp/kenkou/hoken04_002009.html), updated October 1, 2025, distinguishes counter, mail, and proxy evidence. | Do not invent an arrival-specific document list or a filing deadline. Special branch offices are not the relief counter. Employer-handled category changes must not be presented as municipal filings, and electronic forms use Japan Pension Service's My Number Portal route. |
| Bunkyo | The [current procedure page](https://www.city.bunkyo.lg.jp/b021/p005987.html), updated June 15, 2026, names the National Pension Section at Civic Center 11F south and Bunkyo Pension Office for most filings. It lists counter evidence for Category I after employee-pension loss and names the eligible electronic applications. Postal filing is a fallback when electronic or counter filing is difficult; the page asks residents to consult Bunkyo Pension Office before mailing exemption or student applications. The [exemption page](https://www.city.bunkyo.lg.jp/b021/p000661.html), updated August 7, 2026, lists identity, pension-number, and unemployment evidence. The [student page](https://www.city.bunkyo.lg.jp/b021/p000662.html), updated January 23, 2023, lists student status evidence. | No arrival-from-abroad document list or local deadline is stated. Do not imply every event has an online form or that relief applications can simply be mailed to the ward without the pension-office check. Some counter procedures require appointments. |
| Taito | The [current pension procedure](https://www.city.taito.lg.jp/kurashi/zeikin/kokuminnenkin/tetsuzuki.html), updated September 1, 2026, explicitly lists overseas moves, coverage changes, their evidence, municipal and pension-office counters, and My Number Portal forms. Its postal instructions require advance contact with a pension office and direct mail to a Japan Pension Service processing center; the ward office does not accept postal pension applications. The [relief page](https://www.city.taito.lg.jp/kurashi/zeikin/kokuminnenkin/menjyo.html), updated April 1, 2026, gives separate exemption, postponement, and student evidence and identifies the ward pension section or Ueno Pension Office as application counters. | Do not route postal forms to Taito City Office or infer that every listed electronic procedure covers an overseas arrival. Avoid copying contribution amounts or income thresholds from the relief page. Check the appropriate counter for an individual's first enrollment or overseas pension history. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. The English supplements summarize
the local routes and boundaries; they are not official translations. The next
batch in code order is Sumida (`jp-13107`), Koto (`jp-13108`), and Shinagawa
(`jp-13109`).
