# Osaka: Sakai local guidance, batch 2

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds Sakai (`jp-27140`) supplements for six guides; all
remain `needs-review`, and no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed. Sakai runs these procedures
city-wide, so its 7 wards show the city's supplements. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance` (fetch and
`verify`). National Pension for Osaka City and Sakai follows once the Tokyo pension batch is committed.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | The [moving notifications page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kurashi/todokede/tenkyo/tennyushutsushosai.html), March 30, 2026: the person or household head files within 14 days at the ward Citizens Section; no postal filing; moving-out certificate or My Number Card after a special moving-out notice; authorization for a non-household representative; an official relationship document when the household head is a foreign resident. From abroad: passports of everyone moving with the entry stamp, plus a boarding-pass stub or printed e-ticket after an automated gate; temporary returns of less than a year are excluded. | School-transfer documents for children and the Mynaportal reservation details are not reproduced. |
| National Health Insurance | The [14-day notifications page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kurashi/honen/kokuho/kokumin_kenkouhoken/14niti.html), June 29, 2026: the household head files within 14 days at the ward Insurance and Pension Section with identity documents and a seal; authorization for a non-household visitor; cash card and bank seal for a move-in from another municipality's NHI; coverage-loss certificate; premiums back to the month of eligibility and medical costs fully self-paid for a late filing unless unavoidable. The [post and electronic joining page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kurashi/honen/kokuho/kokumin_kenkouhoken/enroll.html), July 14, 2026: eligible cases, household head only, not before the event, electronic signature, health-insurance loss date required, 2–3 weeks for documents. | Premium amounts and the special same-household certificates are not reproduced. |
| Household waste | The [household waste page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kurashi/gomi/gomi_recy/bunbetsu/shigen/index.html): application required before a detached-house resident's first collection; clear or white translucent bags of 45 L or less; no cardboard boxes; daily-life waste twice weekly, plastic containers weekly, cans and bottles and PET bottles twice monthly; sorting app with calendar. The [bulky waste page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kurashi/gomi/gomi_recy/bunbetsu/shigen/sodaigomi/index.html): bulky over about 30 cm with a fee, small non-combustibles free, online any time with cashless payment, specified appliances not collected. | Fees and item categories beyond those listed are linked rather than reproduced. |
| Disaster preparation | The [designated shelters page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/kurashi/bosai/shiru/hinanbasyo/hinan/index.html): shelters designated by disaster type and listed for each of the 7 wards. The [foreign-resident disaster handbook](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/shisei/kokusai/kyosei/hasshin_teikyou/bousai-handbooku.html), April 6, 2026, with the International Division and POME Sakai as contacts. | Specific shelters and hazard areas are left to the ward lists. |
| Language and local support | The [living consultation page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/shisei/kokusai/kyosei/seikatsu/seikatsu_sodan.html), September 17, 2026: POME Sakai counselors for residents, workers, and students; 11 languages in person and 8 by phone, weekdays 10:00–12:00 and 13:00–16:00; OFIX as a wider option. The [Japanese classes page](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/shisei/kokusai/kyosei/seikatsu/nihongo/index.html): classes including complete-beginner classes and free materials. | Class schedules and OFIX hours are left to the linked pages. |
| Income and resident tax | The English [Sakai City Tax Facts](https://www.city.sakai.lg.jp/foreign-language/english/visitors/living/dailylife/tax.html), updated November 27, 2025: tax paid to the municipality of January 1 registration on the previous year's income; declaration by March 15 unless the employer reports salary; before leaving Japan, pay in full or appoint a tax agent and notify the ward office; payment methods. | Rates and deductions are not covered; the guide is Sakai's own English. |
