# Chiba: Chiba City (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for Chiba City
(`jp-12100`) in [`locations/chiba/chiba.ts`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/chiba/chiba.ts). All sources are
Japanese pages (the disaster guide portal also has English text). Chiba City uses designated paid bags for burnable and
non-burnable waste. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.
