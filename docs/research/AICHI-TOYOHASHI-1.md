# Aichi G3 local guidance research: Toyohashi City

Inspected 2026-09-25. Toyohashi's official resident population was confirmed above the 300,000 major-city threshold from current city statistics. Research is at city level; counters, waste calendars, hazard information, and evacuation places must be checked for the resident's address.

| Guide | Official evidence inspected | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Address registration | [Resident registration and moving-in notifications](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/8814.htm) | 14-day move-in filing, required records, and exception that foreign-resident arrivals cannot use counter centers. Follow the page for the address and case-specific route. |
| National Health Insurance | [Enrollment and withdrawal notifications](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/6660.htm) | 14-day notification, counters, household My Number and event-specific evidence; confirm records matching the event. |
| National Pension | [National Pension information](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/2709.htm) | City page identifies the municipal pension group and links enrollment/exemption/e-filing topics; event-specific detail is on linked procedures. |
| Waste | [Waste sorting](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/2804.htm) | The index links sorting lookup, household guide, and area calendars. Confirm current calendar and pickup point locally. |
| Resident tax | [FY 2026 resident-tax return](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/2606.htm) | Current year page includes filing criteria, postal and electronic channels, required records, and year-specific dates. Check the next tax-year notice; foreign nationals needing income-tax returns are directed to the tax office. |
| Disaster preparation | [Disaster information](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/23788.htm) | Index links emergency information, evacuation facilities, and foreign-language disaster maps; consult the current address-specific map and alerts. |
| Language and local support | [INFOPIA consultation](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/40707.htm); [Japanese classes](https://www.city.toyohashi.lg.jp/26453.htm) | City page lists multilingual in-person/phone support and class materials. Verify current language coverage, access arrangements, and schedules with the provider. |

All supplements are marked `needs-review`. The pension and disaster pages are landing/index pages, and the waste calendar varies by address.
