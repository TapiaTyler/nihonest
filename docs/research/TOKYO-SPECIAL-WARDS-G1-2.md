# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Taito, Sumida, Koto)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Taito (`jp-13106`), Sumida (`jp-13107`), and Koto
(`jp-13108`), completing all seven guides for each. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts
were created or changed. National Pension (Codex) was not touched. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance`.

**Date-sensitive:** Taito's new foreign-resident desk opens on November 15, 2026, and its ward-office Chinese, English,
and Korean consultation runs until the end of March 2027; Taito's online bulky waste payment starts October 1, 2026.
Recheck Taito's language supplement after April 2027.
