# Kanagawa: Kawasaki (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for Kawasaki City
(`jp-14130`) in [`locations/kanagawa/kawasaki.ts`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/kanagawa/kawasaki.ts).
Kawasaki closed its foreign-language website, so every source is a Japanese page (the consultation page is in plain
Japanese). Kawasaki is researched at city level; the panel's ward note covers ward-office differences. Unlike Yokohama,
resident tax is handled by city tax offices (市税事務所), not ward offices. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese
translation artifacts were created or changed.
