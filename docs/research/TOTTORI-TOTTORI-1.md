# Tottori City local guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-26 using saved pages from Tottori City's official website. All supplements are citywide; regional counter, collection-day, and evacuation details depend on the address and current conditions.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | [Address change procedures](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/3967.html), updated 2025-10-20 | Identifies city counters, online move-out/arrival reservation, filing periods, and foreign-resident documents. Cases moving from overseas and correct branch selection should be checked with the city. |
| National Health Insurance | [Insurance procedures](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/4628.html) and [enrollment after moving in](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/4718.html), updated 2025-10-20 | Gives resident counter routes and examples of filing records for moving in. It does not determine individual eligibility or premiums. |
| Household waste | [Sorting and disposal](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/4083.html) and [collection-day index](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/life/1/11/60/index.html), calendars updated January 2026 | Directs residents to district calendars and current sorting instructions, with separate handling for excluded items. No detailed schedules or fees are copied. |
| Resident tax | [Resident tax filing](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/33516.html), updated 2026-03-17, and [overseas departure](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/1959.html), updated 2025-10-20 | Points residents to current-year filing requirements and the city's tax representative/payment steps for overseas moves. Individual tax liability is not determined here. |
| Disaster preparation | [Comprehensive disaster map](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/2319.html), updated 2022-04-01, and [designated evacuation places and shelters](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/2282.html), updated 2026-06-01 | Directs residents to address-specific hazard layers and current shelter information. The hazard map page references a 2021 edition; site suitability and opening depend on hazard and current conditions. |
| Language and local support | [Japanese-learning resources](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/site/gaikokunokatahe/3155.html), updated 2025-10-20, and [easy Japanese life information](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/site/gaikokunokatahe/), updated 2026-02-05 | Identifies city and partner language-learning resources and the foreign-resident information directory. Service schedules, languages, and eligibility must be confirmed with providers. |
| National Pension | [National Pension enrollment](https://www.city.tottori.lg.jp/page/4672.html), updated 2025-10-20 | Describes resident-side enrollment routes, sample event-specific records, branch counters, and online filing. Counter hours are subject to a trial change announced for 2026-11-30, so confirm before visiting. |
