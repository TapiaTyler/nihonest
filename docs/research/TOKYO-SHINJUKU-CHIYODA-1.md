# Tokyo: Shinjuku rewrite and Chiyoda's remaining guides

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch rewrites all seven Shinjuku (`shinjuku`) supplements to the
current standard, replacing the one-action pilot records (address, National Health Insurance, waste, disaster, language,
and tax) and revising the National Pension record, and adds Chiyoda's (`jp-13101`) waste, disaster, language, and tax
supplements. Both wards now have all seven guides. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts
were created or changed. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance` (fetch and `verify`).

Shinjuku's English portal (foreign.city.shinjuku.lg.jp) loads its details with scripts, so the evidence comes from the
ward's Japanese procedure pages. The English pages stay cited as reader-friendly overviews.
