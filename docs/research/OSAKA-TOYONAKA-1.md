# Toyonaka City local-guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-26. Osaka Prefecture's resident-register totals dated January 1, 2026 list Toyonaka at 405,958 residents ([official prefectural table](https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/documents/136365/r8sichousonnbetsujinnkou.pdf)). The city runs services at the main Citizens Division and two branch offices; neighborhood calendars, collection points, and hazard information are address-dependent.

| Guide | Official evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| Address registration | [Domestic move-in](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/shimin/jusho/tennyuu.html) and [overseas arrival](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/shimin/jusho/kokugaitennyuu.html): both require filing within 14 days; overseas and foreign-household documents differ. Domestic page lists Citizens Division and two branch offices. |
| National Health Insurance | [Enrollment and withdrawal](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kenko/kokuho/kanyuu/kanyuudattai.html): most move-ins are routed through resident registration with eligibility information sent later; listed special cases require contact with Insurance Consultation Division. |
| Household waste | [Sorting and neighborhood schedules](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/gomi_risaikuru_bika/bunbetsu_dashikata/gominowakekata/): current annual calendar is selected by neighborhood; bulky and temporary waste follow separate arrangements. |
| Resident tax | [Tax liability](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/sizei/kojin/gimusyatokeisan/nouzeigimusya.html) and [tax representative notification](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/moushikomi/soshiki/moushikomi6/nouzeikanrinin.html): January 1 status determines liability municipality; overseas departure forms and annual payment details need current confirmation. |
| Disaster preparation | [Flood and digital hazard maps](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/bosai/bousai_info/hazardmap/sinsuiHM.html): map layers include flood/inland-water, storm-surge/tsunami, landslide, earthquake, and evacuation information. Check exact address and current event instructions. |
| Language and consultation | [Foreign-resident consultation](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/yokuaru/soudan/gaikokujin.html), [multilingual support](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/jinken_gakushu/gaikokujin_joho/soudan.html), and [Japanese courses](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/jinken_gakushu/gaikokujin_joho/nihongo.html): current languages, schedules, and booking requirements vary by provider. |
| National Pension | [Electronic applications](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/kokuhonenkin/nenkin/nenkin/densi_sinsei.html) and [employment-change procedures](https://www.city.toyonaka.osaka.jp/kurashi/kokuhonenkin/nenkin/nenkin/syuusyoku.html): lists Mynaportal-supported filings and local section/document guidance for employment changes. |
