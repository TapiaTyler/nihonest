# Aichi G3 local guidance research: Okazaki City

Inspected 2026-09-25. Okazaki City's current resident population table reports 379,811 residents on 2026-09-01, above the 300,000 major-city threshold. Research is at city level; use the office, collection calendar, map, or provider for the resident's address and case.

| Guide | Official evidence inspected | Claim boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Address registration | [Moving-in notification](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/hikkoshi/1012264/1012266/1001536.html) | 14-day filing and separate domestic, overseas, and card-based procedures; check the route and records for the resident's case. |
| National Health Insurance | [Enrollment procedure](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kenko/kokuho/1012378/1002055/1002056.html) | 14-day filing, counters, and event-specific documents, including foreign-resident move cases; verify current proof for the event. |
| National Pension | [Pension procedure index](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/nenkin/1002102/index.html) | Links to separate join/retirement/dependent procedures but does not enumerate their documents on the index itself. |
| Waste | [Household waste rules](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/gomi/1012430/1002188/1002200.html); [foreign-resident waste guide](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/gomi/1012430/1002188/1002218/1002225.html) | Designated bags and current neighborhood calendar/station, with sorting information in seven languages; follow the date-specific calendar and local station directions. |
| Resident tax | [FY 2026 resident-tax return guide](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/zei/1001651/1001659/1001663/1001664.html) | Current-year filing channels, dates, and procedure; check the next fiscal guide and individual requirements. |
| Disaster preparation | [Disaster information hub](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/bosai/index.html) | Links preparedness, response, recovery, and the city disaster portal; map and evacuation information are address-specific and current-event dependent. |
| Language and local support | [Information for foreign residents](https://www.city.okazaki.lg.jp/kurashi/foreigners/index.html) | Index links consultation, interpreting, disaster resources, and Japanese classes; contact the linked provider for current schedule and availability. |

All supplements are marked `needs-review`. Tax filing and waste calendars are time-sensitive; the pension and disaster pages are landing pages rather than complete individual procedures/maps.
