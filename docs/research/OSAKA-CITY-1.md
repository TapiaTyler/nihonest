# Osaka: Osaka City local guidance, batch 1

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. Osaka City (`jp-27100`) and Sakai (`jp-27140`) become selectable
locations under Osaka (`jp-27000`). This batch adds Osaka City supplements for six guides; all remain `needs-review`,
and no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed. Osaka City runs these procedures city-wide, so its 24
wards show the city's supplements (see the designated-city rule in `docs/LOCAL-GUIDANCE-WORKFLOW.md`). Researched by
Claude with `npm run local-guidance` (fetch and `verify`). National Pension follows once the Tokyo pension batch is
committed; Sakai is batch 2.

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | The [moving-in page](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/shimin/page/0000369822.html), dated May 15, 2026: file within 14 days at the ward office resident services section or a branch; not before the move, not by post, not at service counters. Moving-out certificate (not needed within the city or after a Mynaportal moving-out notice), identity documents, My Number Cards; residence card or Special Permanent Resident Certificate, which also completes the immigration address notification; authorization for a representative, even a relative in another household. From abroad: passport, and entry-date evidence without a stamp; a relationship document with a translator-named translation for a foreign non-head joining a foreign head. | Japanese-national family-register documents for arrivals from abroad and the Mynaportal special moving-out rules are not reproduced. |
| National Health Insurance | The [moving](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/fukushi/page/0000369734.html) (August 24, 2026) and [employment](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/fukushi/page/0000369739.html) (September 1, 2026) pages: the household head files within 14 days at the ward insurance and pension section; others need authorization; My Number and identity verification; cash card or passbook and seal; coverage-loss certificate or the Mynaportal screen; contact the office if documents are not ready. Premiums by account transfer set up at the counter; late joining back-charges up to two years and benefits may start from the filing date. Leaving NHI for an employer plan can be filed online. | Premium amounts, reductions, and birth or marriage procedures are not covered. |
| Household waste | The [collection days page](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/kankyo/page/0000370521.html), May 31, 2026: address-based days and approximate two-hour windows on the city map; separate collection of ordinary waste, recyclables, plastic resources, and paper and clothing; mixed waste left with a sticker; community collection of paper and clothing in some areas. The [bulky waste page](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/kankyo/page/0000369355.html), June 2, 2026: over 30 cm on a side or rods over 1 m; online or phone Monday to Saturday 9:00–17:00 (closed December 29 to January 3); pay the fee; business waste excluded; reuse partners. | Fees, item-by-item categories, and the sorting app are linked rather than reproduced. |
| Disaster preparation | The [evacuation page](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/kikikanrishitsu/page/0000012054.html), March 23, 2026: evacuation places (wide-area, temporary, tsunami and flood buildings from the third floor) versus shelters; go to a place designated for the disaster; staying on the third floor or above can be a safe evacuation; loudspeaker, mobile alert, television, and radio warnings. The [ward maps page](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/kikikanrishitsu/page/0000139402.html) links each of the 24 wards' maps. | Place counts and specific locations change and are not listed. |
| Language and local support | The [foreign-resident consultation page](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/shimin/page/0000370516.html), April 24, 2026: free consultation in English, Chinese, Korean, Vietnamese, and Filipino, by phone or in person, no appointment; the main desk is temporarily relocated during the Osaka International House renovation with changed hours; City Hall Citizen Consultation Room weekdays 9:00–17:30; ward-office consultation lines. | The temporary address and hours are left to the city page because they change; Japanese classes are not covered by this source. |
| Income and resident tax | The [individual inhabitant tax overview](https://www.city.osaka.lg.jp/zaisei/page/0000370518.html), May 29, 2026: liability by the January 1 situation; previous calendar year's income; per-person and income-based parts; forest environment tax collected alongside; return by March 15 unless an exception applies; payment slips, salary, or pension deduction; reduction or exemption by application, including under tax treaties. | Exceptions to the filing requirement, rates, and deduction details are not reproduced. |
