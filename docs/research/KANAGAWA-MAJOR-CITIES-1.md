# Kanagawa: Fujisawa, Yokosuka (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for two Kanagawa
major cities in [`locations/kanagawa/`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/kanagawa/). The registry gains `jp-14201`
and `jp-14205` (J-LIS). All sources are official city pages. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

|---|---|---|
| Fujisawa (`jp-14205`) | [fujisawa.ts](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/kanagawa/fujisawa.ts) | Foreign residents arriving from abroad have their own page (not the Fujisawa or Muraoka citizen centers); NHI exclusions by status; 500-yen large-waste ticket; March 15 filing deadline and tax agent form; tsunami map with arrival as early as 6 minutes; Spanish and Portuguese consultation; Japanese class map in seven versions; pension notifications. Gaps: burnable waste details and flood or shelter information. |
| Yokosuka (`jp-14201`) | [yokosuka.ts](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/kanagawa/yokosuka.ts) | Moving in from abroad at the Counter Services Section or nine administrative centers; same-day NHI certificate with photo ID; pamphlet in seven languages; bulky waste fees rise on October 1 (dated recheck); who must file; tsunami map revised in fiscal 2022; Yokosuka International Association counseling. The City Hall outreach counseling schedule covers only April 2025 to March 2026 and is omitted. Gaps: filing deadline and tax agent, pension when moving abroad. |
