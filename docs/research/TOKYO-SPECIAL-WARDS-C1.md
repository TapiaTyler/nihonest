# Tokyo special wards: C1 address-registration batch

Source inspection: September 23, 2026. This batch adds Chiyoda (`jp-13101`),
Chuo (`jp-13102`), and Minato (`jp-13103`). The IDs follow the existing J-LIS
administrative registry. Shinjuku (`jp-13104`) remains the earlier pilot. Only
the address-registration article gains local supplements for the three new
wards. A ward appearing in the selector does not imply that every article has
guidance for it. All new supplements remain `needs-review` pending human
editorial approval. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Current official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Chiyoda | [Moving into Chiyoda](https://www.city.chiyoda.lg.jp/koho/kurashi/koseki/jumintoroku/tennyu.html) distinguishes domestic and overseas moves, requires filing within 14 days after moving, identifies the City Office and branches, and lists residence cards, passports, and family documents for overseas arrivals. | The supplement points to the correct route. It does not promise that every case can be handled at a branch or outside regular hours. |
| Chuo | [Domestic moving-in procedure](https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/a0012/kurashi/touroku/juuminidou/tennyuu/mynocd-nashi/tennyu.html), [overseas moving-in procedure](https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/a0012/kurashi/touroku/juuminidou/tennyuu/mynocd-nashi/kokugai.html), and the older [English overview](https://www.city.chuo.lg.jp/en/live/moving.html) establish the 14-day period and distinct routes. The overseas page excludes Wednesday extended hours and Sunday opening. | The supplement links the current Japanese procedures alongside the English overview and does not apply domestic document rules to overseas arrivals. |
| Minato | [Current moving-in procedure](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/shibamadosa/kurashi/todokede/hikkoshi/tennyu.html) and [English overview](https://www.city.minato.tokyo.jp/easyjp/en/moving/residentregistration/moving.html) distinguish domestic and overseas documents, the 14-day period, and regional office counters. The current procedure advises arriving around 4 p.m. for overseas cases requiring outside confirmation. | The supplement directs readers to the current procedure for exact documents and hours. The older English overview is supporting context only. |
