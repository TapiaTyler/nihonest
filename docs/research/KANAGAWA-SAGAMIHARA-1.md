# Kanagawa: Sagamihara (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for Sagamihara City
(`jp-14150`) in [`locations/kanagawa/sagamihara.ts`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/kanagawa/sagamihara.ts),
completing Kanagawa's three designated cities. All sources are Japanese pages. Sagamihara spreads counters across ward
office Resident Sections, town development centers (まちづくりセンター), and branch offices, and some procedures are limited
by counter (Chuo Ward Office, City Hall's National Health Insurance and Pension Division). All records remain
`needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.
