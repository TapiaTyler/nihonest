# Niigata: Niigata City (all seven guides)

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds all seven local guidance supplements for Niigata City
(`jp-15100`) in [`locations/niigata/niigata.ts`](../../src/data/local-guidance/locations/niigata/niigata.ts). All sources
are Japanese pages. Several procedures (tax filing, the pension counter) are only linked from the city's pages to its
online system, so those supplements are thinner and carry gap items. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese
translation artifacts were created or changed.
