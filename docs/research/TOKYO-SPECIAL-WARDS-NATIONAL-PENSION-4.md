# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 4

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Meguro
(`jp-13110`), Ota (`jp-13111`), and Setagaya (`jp-13112`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Meguro | The [notification page](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kurashi/nenkin/todokede.html), updated February 2, 2026, lists ward, district-service-office, and Meguro Pension Office counters for retirement and dependent-status changes, with identity and event-date evidence and a My Number Portal route. The [relief page](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kurashi/nenkin/nofutokurei.html), updated the same day, lists electronic exemption, postponement, and student applications. The [postal instructions](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kurashi/kokuho/kokuhoyuusou.html), updated April 1, 2026, cover a switch from employee pension coverage to National Pension with specified supporting copies. | None of these pages gives a first-arrival-from-abroad document package or counter. Do not extend the coverage-loss postal route to overseas arrival or imply that relief and student applications have a ward postal route. No local filing deadline was established. |
| Ota | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/kokunen/nenkin/kanyusoushitsu/kokuminnenkinkanyuusousitu.html) and [procedure FAQ](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/faq/bunya/nenkin/kokuminnenkin_tetuduki.html), both updated September 1, 2026, separate employee/dependent-coverage loss from overseas move-in and identify Ota's main counter, eligible branches other than Kamata East, and Ota Pension Office. The [foreign-resident guide](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/kokunen/nenkin/kokuminnenkingaikokujin/kokuminnenkin_foreignes.html), updated April 1, 2025, directs first-time arrivals to City Hall and advertises a separate smartphone form. The [LoGo joining page](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/kokunen/nenkin/online/kanyu_logoform.html), updated September 2, 2025, limits that form to loss of employee or dependent coverage. The [exemption page](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/kokunen/nenkin/hokenryoumenjo/kokuminnenkin_menzyo.html), updated July 1, 2026, and [student page](https://www.city.ota.tokyo.jp/seikatsu/kokunen/nenkin/hokenryoumenjo/kokuminnenkingakuseinouhutokurei.html), updated May 2, 2025, describe separate relief applications. | The cited pages do not give a general overseas-arrival deadline or complete document package. The first-arrival smartphone form's fields could not be extracted by the fetch tool, so confirm its current instructions before relying on it. Do not apply the retirement postal or LoGo form to a first arrival. Special branch offices do not take relief applications. |
| Setagaya | The [Category I joining page](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/361.html), updated May 11, 2026, asks for filing within 14 days after the event and gives counter, postal, and electronic instructions for coverage-loss cases. The [overseas-arrival page](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/359.html), updated December 13, 2022, permits pension enrollment during resident move-in registration at a district counter or branch and lists identity and number evidence. The [exemption page](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/366.html) and [student page](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/367.html), both updated April 1, 2026, exclude district and branch counters but permit main-counter, pension-office, postal, and electronic applications. The [electronic list](https://www.city.setagaya.lg.jp/02060/355.html), updated September 10, 2025, lists eligible My Number Portal application types. | Do not assume the retirement postal or electronic form also covers an overseas arrival. The older dedicated arrival page is still linked from the current joining page; check with the ward when a first-time enrollee lacks a Basic Pension Number record. Relief applications are not accepted at the district or branch counters. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. These English supplements summarize
local routes and boundaries, not official translations. The next National
Pension batch in code order is Shibuya (`jp-13113`), Nakano (`jp-13114`),
and Suginami (`jp-13115`).
