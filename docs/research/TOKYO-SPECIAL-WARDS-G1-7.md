# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Adachi, Katsushika, Edogawa)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Adachi (`jp-13121`), Katsushika (`jp-13122`), and Edogawa
(`jp-13123`). With National Pension complete (Codex), all 23 wards now have all seven guides, completing phase G1. All
records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed. Date-sensitive items and old
sources are listed in [LOCAL-GUIDANCE-RECHECK.md](../LOCAL-GUIDANCE-RECHECK.md).
