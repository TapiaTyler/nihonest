# Koriyama City local guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-25. Research was performed at city level; Koriyama's local offices and administrative centers serve residents citywide. Ward-level guidance is not applicable.

No premium amounts copied. |

| National Pension | [National Pension procedures](https://www.city.koriyama.lg.jp/soshiki/35/4611.html) | Lists common municipal procedures, document categories, online filing for some procedures, and locations.
