# Shimonoseki City local-guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-25. Location `jp-35201` is included as Yamaguchi Prefecture's largest city under the coverage plan's largest-city exception, although it is below 300,000 residents. Shimonoseki reports 237,308 registered residents at August 31, 2026, based on the Basic Resident Register. The city identifies itself as having Yamaguchi Prefecture's largest population in its official business-location material; the 2020 census table in the city's population page also places Shimonoseki (255,051) ahead of Yamaguchi City (about 193,966). The latest city population figure and the largest-city qualification are therefore documented independently of the 300,000 threshold. J-LIS lists the city as 下関市, reading しものせきし, code 352012; the local five-digit registry ID is `jp-35201`.
