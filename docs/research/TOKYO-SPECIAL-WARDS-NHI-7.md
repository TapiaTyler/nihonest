# Tokyo special wards: National Health Insurance batch 7

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `joining-national-health-insurance` for Nerima (`jp-13120`),
Adachi (`jp-13121`), Katsushika (`jp-13122`), and Edogawa (`jp-13123`). This
completes the 23 Tokyo special wards and all 24 currently selectable
municipalities on this article. All four supplements remain `needs-review`;
no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Nerima | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminkenkohoken/hoken_kanyu/tetsuzuki.html), updated April 22, 2025, asks for notice within 14 days of the event and lists event-specific evidence, the main and Shakujii insurance desks, four citizen offices, and postal joining with four classes of documents. A move-in is directed to transfer registration at a citizen office. | Do not imply the move-in requires a separate visit to a particular insurance counter. Postal filing is limited to the household head, joining person, or same-household member. The page links electronic withdrawal, not electronic joining. Registered foreign residents can be eligible, subject to the listed exclusions. |
| Adachi | The [joining and other procedures](https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kurashi/hoken/kenpo-tetsuzuki.html), dated May 1, 2019, generally ask for notice within 14 days and distinguish domestic move-in counters from insurance coverage-loss, public-assistance, and birth counters. Postal joining starts by contacting the insurance section for its form. The [foreign-resident page](https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kanyu-gaikoku.html), updated August 5, 2024, explains eligibility exclusions and Designated Activities evidence. | The general procedure page's displayed date is old and merits editorial rechecking before publication. Do not assume the same counter or documents apply to every event, or that every registered foreign resident is eligible. No online joining route was established by these pages. |
| Katsushika | The [NHI notification procedure](https://www.city.katsushika.lg.jp/kurashi/1000049/1001690/1001699.html), updated June 5, 2026, asks the household head or a same-household member to report within 14 days at the main Qualification Desk or a residents' office. It provides separate evidence for moves, workplace or dependent coverage loss, public-assistance end, birth, and foreign-resident registration. Its [eligibility page](https://www.city.katsushika.lg.jp/kurashi/1000049/1001690/1001697.html), updated October 1, 2025, describes foreign-resident conditions and exclusions. | The postal instructions on the notification page apply to **withdrawal** after workplace coverage begins, not joining. The page does not establish a postal or online joining route. Foreign-resident eligibility needs an individual status check; counter issuance of qualification documents is conditional. |
| Edogawa | The [joining procedure](https://www.city.edogawa.tokyo.jp/e053/kurashi/iryohoken/kokuho/shikaku/kanyu.html), updated August 17, 2026, asks for notice within 14 days. It lists counter filing for move-ins, births, public-assistance end, and qualifying foreign-resident registration, while workplace or dependent coverage loss has Pitatto Service electronic and postal routes with specified evidence. | Do not offer the remote routes for move-in, birth, public-assistance end, or foreign-resident registration. Foreign residents must check their status and stay; the page excludes certain Designated Activities cases. The electronic application has My Number card, PIN, device, and loss-proof requirements. |

These official pages are in Japanese. The English supplements summarize the
local procedure boundaries and link directly to the sources; they are not
official translations. There is no remaining Tokyo special-ward NHI batch.
