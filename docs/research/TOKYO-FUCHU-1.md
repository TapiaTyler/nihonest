# Tokyo: Fuchu local guidance, batch 1

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. Fuchu City (`jp-13206`) becomes a selectable location in Tokyo's Tama
area (phase G1 of [LOCAL-GUIDANCE-COVERAGE-PLAN.md](../LOCAL-GUIDANCE-COVERAGE-PLAN.md)). This batch adds supplements
for six guides; all remain `needs-review`, and no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed. National
Pension follows with the other Tama cities once Codex's Tokyo pension work is committed. Researched by Claude with
`npm run local-guidance` (fetch and `verify`).

| Guide | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Address registration | The [moving-in page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/tetuduki/tenkyo/tennyu.html), May 1, 2026: within 14 days, no future dates, no online filing; the person, the new household, or a representative with authorization; foreign residents only at the General Service Division, not the Shiraitodai or Saifu branches; moves from abroad on weekdays only, not at the Saturday counter. Identity documents, residence card or Special Permanent Resident Certificate, relationship document where needed; moving-out certificate and My Number Card; from abroad, the entry passport, asking airport staff for a stamp after automated gates, or a boarding-pass stub. | The late-filing fine, the special moving-in with a My Number Card, and Saturday counter hours are not reproduced. |
| National Health Insurance | The [counter page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/kokuminkenko/kanyu/kokuhokanyu/madoguchikanyu.html) (February 6, 2026): coverage-loss certificate and identity; four counters; the person or household, or a representative with authorization; same-day issue; pension separately. The [online page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/kokuminkenko/kanyu/kokuhokanyu/kokuhokanyuuonline.html) (June 10, 2024): certificate required (not a separation notice), My Number Card and both passwords, MynaSign app. The [postal page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/hoken/kokuminkenko/kanyu/kokuhokanyu/kanyuyuusou.html) (September 17, 2026): form and copies; mailed to the household head about five days later. | These pages cover joining after leaving workplace coverage; the arrival-from-abroad route and the filing deadline are left to the national guide. |
| Household waste | The [household waste page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gomirisaikuru/dashikata/kateigomi/gominodasikata.html) (April 17, 2026): by 8:00; green, orange, and pink paid bags; burnable twice a week, plastic packaging once a week; paper tied with string. The [bulky waste page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gomirisaikuru/dashikata/sodaigomi/sodaigomidasikata.html) (May 23, 2025): 40 cm (30 cm electrical, 80 cm rods); call center Monday–Saturday 8:00–19:00 or online; stickers by the day before or online payment; out by 8:00; 15 items a day; household waste only. The [LINE menu page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/gomirisaikuru/linegomi3r/index.html): English, Chinese, Korean. | Bag and sticker prices and application deadlines are not reproduced. |
| Disaster preparation | The [hazard maps page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/bosaibohan/saigai/hazard-map.html) (June 8, 2021): earthquake map, flood hazard map with a foreign-language edition, landslide map. The [evacuation areas page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/bosaibohan/saigai/hinan/ittokisyugo_koikihinan_basyo.html) (September 5, 2013): local, designated (school grounds), and wide-area evacuation areas; agree on a meeting place. | Both pages are older; the evacuation-area page predates the current evacuation-level terms, so the supplement makes no claim about alert levels. |
| Language and local support | The [DIVE page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/bunka/bunka/tabunnkakyousei/muliticultural_center_DIVE.html) (April 3, 2025): information, consultation, and cultural exchange; Plats in Le Signe 5F; Monday–Saturday 10:00–17:00, consultation 11:00–15:00; information in plain Japanese, English, and others. The [Japanese study page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/bunka/bunka/tabunnkakyousei/salon_nihongo.html) (May 15, 2025): free; high-school age and older who live, work, or study in Fuchu; sessions on several days; apply at Fuchur. | Session times and reception hours are left to the city page. |
| Income and resident tax | The [overview](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/zekin/shiminze/aboutshiminze.html) (August 6, 2026): January 1 residence and previous-year income; who must file; income-tax filers need not. The [filing page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/zekin/shiminze/shinkoku.html) (August 6, 2026): by March 15, later filing may miss June; documents; post preferred; online filing from the 2026 tax year. The [leaving Japan page](https://www.city.fuchu.tokyo.jp/kurashi/zekin/shiminze/kokugaitennshyutu.html) (February 13, 2025): mid-year moves do not change the tax; a tax agent before leaving, online, at the counter, or by post; salary-deduction cases; public posting and late charges without one. | Rates and thresholds are not reproduced. |
