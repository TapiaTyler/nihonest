# Individual Tax Fundamentals Discovery

Prepared and scope-approved September 12, 2026. This record defines the focused Phase 13 Iteration 9B-1 tax implementation. Public guidance remains subject to article-level editorial review.

## Recommended content structure

The current two tax drafts combine four decisions that users need at different times. Iteration 9B-1 should create a four-guide sequence:

1. determine the tax-residence classification and taxable scope that require investigation;
2. understand the different national income-tax and local resident-tax calendars;
3. manage salary withholding, employee declarations, year-end adjustment, and the withholding slip; and
4. decide whether a final return is required or useful.

This avoids presenting payroll withholding as the final tax result or the immigration status as a tax-residence answer.

## Important boundaries

- Resident, non-permanent resident, and non-resident are income-tax classifications. They are not interchangeable with immigration residence statuses.
- Individual resident tax commonly follows prior-year income and the January 1 municipality, creating a delayed first bill that must be planned for.
- Year-end adjustment resolves covered salary tax for qualifying employees, not every payer, income category, deduction, or international issue.
- The often-repeated 200,000-yen figure is part of limited national filing exceptions for defined salary earners. It is not a general tax-free amount and does not erase separate municipal questions.
- Foreign-income and treaty conclusions, independent business tax, consumption tax, invoice registration, and detailed departure procedures belong to 9B-2.

## Implementation sequence

The two new guides should be `tax-residence-and-taxable-income-in-japan` and `payroll-withholding-and-year-end-adjustment`. The existing income/resident-tax overview and final-return guide should be rewritten around them. Explore, the ongoing-responsibilities journey, FAQs, glossary terms, sources, and reviewed search aliases should expose the sequence.

All four guides and materially changed support records remain `needs-review` until explicitly approved under the repository editorial workflow.
