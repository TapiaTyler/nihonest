# Tokyo special wards: National Health Insurance batch 3

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `joining-national-health-insurance` for Koto (`jp-13108`),
Shinagawa (`jp-13109`), and Meguro (`jp-13110`). Tokyo special-ward coverage
is now 10/23; the other 13 wards retain clearly labeled general lookup advice.
All three new supplements remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation
artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Koto | The [National Health Insurance move-in page](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/250102/fukushi/kokumin/todokede/5144.html), dated December 2, 2025, says a resident continuing municipal insurance or losing workplace coverage at move-in should tell the resident-registration counter; no separate Medical Insurance Division procedure is needed for those cases. It lists the move-in filing period, evidence, and resident-service counters. The [online-procedure page](https://www.city.koto.lg.jp/250102/kokuho/pittari.html), dated September 18, 2026, lists specific qualifying coverage changes and explicitly excludes joining because of a move or birth. | The move-in page does not resolve every overseas-arrival or insurance-eligibility case. The insurance division is the contact for questions, whereas the listed resident counter handles these move-in transactions. Do not offer online joining for a move or birth, or infer a postal route. |
| Shinagawa | The [joining page](https://www.city.shinagawa.tokyo.jp/PC/procedure/procedure-kenkouhoken/procedure-kenkouhoken-todokede/hpg000001509.html), updated December 2, 2024, asks for notice within 14 days, gives documents by event and My Number requirements, and lists the ward insurance desk plus specific regional centers. Its [postal page](https://www.city.shinagawa.tokyo.jp/PC/procedure/procedure-kenkouhoken/procedure-kenkouhoken-todokede/20200325095605.html), updated the same day, limits postal joining to loss of workplace insurance with qualification-loss or retirement proof and restricts who may file. | Postal joining is not stated for move-ins. Residence status and period of stay affect foreign-resident eligibility; Designated Activities requires a designation document. Do not infer a universal overseas-arrival route from the general joining table. |
| Meguro | The [joining page](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kurashi/kokuho/kanyu_todoke.html), updated December 2, 2025, asks for notice within 14 days, accepts joining from 14 days before the loss date when voluntary-continuation coverage ends (added after review, 2026-09-24), and distinguishes counters and documents by event. A foreign resident moving in uses the main qualification desk, while a district service office other than the eastern one may handle that move only if the resident had municipal insurance at the prior municipality. It lists postal and electronic options for certain coverage-loss cases and a prior resident-registration step for birth. The [online page](https://www.city.meguro.tokyo.jp/kokuho/kurashi/kokuho/dattaidenshi.html), updated April 1, 2026, gives the eligible joining events and limits online filing to the person or same registered household. | Do not present district service offices or online filing as universal routes. A representative from another registered household needs authorization and cannot use the online route. General eligibility remains with the national guide and the ward's current criteria. |

The source pages are Japanese. The English supplements summarize local
procedure boundaries and link directly to those pages; they are not official
translations. The next NHI batch in code order is Ota (`jp-13111`),
Setagaya (`jp-13112`), and Shibuya (`jp-13113`).
