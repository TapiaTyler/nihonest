# Tokyo special wards: waste, disaster, language, and tax (Chuo, Minato, Bunkyo)

Official source inspection: September 24, 2026. This batch adds household waste, disaster preparation, language and
local support, and income and resident tax supplements for Chuo (`jp-13102`), Minato (`jp-13103`), and Bunkyo
(`jp-13105`). With their existing address, National Health Insurance, and National Pension supplements, all three wards
now have all seven guides. All records remain `needs-review`; no Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.
National Pension work (Codex) was not touched. Researched by Claude with `npm run local-guidance`.

The evidence check now also recognizes English times written as "8 a.m." on English source pages (Minato's living guide).
