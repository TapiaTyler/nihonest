# Tokyo special wards: National Pension batch 7

Official source inspection: September 25, 2026. This batch adds local
supplements to `national-pension-after-moving-to-japan` for Itabashi
(`jp-13119`), Nerima (`jp-13120`), and Adachi (`jp-13121`). They remain
`needs-review`. No Japanese translation artifacts were created or changed.

| Ward | Official evidence | Claim boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Itabashi | The [overseas-move page](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/nenkin/kanyu/1003178.html), updated May 25, 2026, identifies Category I enrollment after overseas arrival and accepts the main National Pension Section or residents' offices. The [retirement](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/nenkin/kanyu/1003180.html) and [dependent-loss](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/nenkin/kanyu/1003184.html) pages, updated July 17 and 22, 2026, give separate coverage-end evidence. The [counter table](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/nenkin/1059606.html), updated May 25, 2026, lists joining and relief counters plus limited postal and electronic routes; the [exemption](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/nenkin/hokenryo/1016757.html) and [student](https://www.city.itabashi.tokyo.jp/kenko/kokuho/nenkin/hokenryo/1017766.html) pages, both updated July 22, 2026, give relief documents. | The arrival page gives no local deadline or first-enrollment document package without a pension number. Residents' offices do not accept relief applications. The postal route covers only some procedures; call the section before relying on it for a particular event. |
| Nerima | The [foreign-resident page](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/nenkin_kanyuchu/gaikokujin.html) and [overseas-move page](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/nenkin_kanyuchu/gaikoku.html), both updated April 1, 2026, give first-arrival steps and documents after resident move-in registration. The [retirement](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/nenkin_kanyuchu/taishoku.html) and [dependent-loss](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/nenkin_kanyuchu/fuyohazureta.html) pages, both updated May 8, 2023, list separate event evidence and postal attachments. The [student page](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/nenkin_hokenryo/taino.html), updated April 1, 2024, excludes residents' offices; the [postal instructions](https://www.city.nerima.tokyo.jp/kurashi/nenkinhoken/kokuminnenkin/nenkin_tetsuzuki/yuusou.html), updated October 5, 2021, describe mailing to the ward section or pension office. | The foreign-resident page describes first enrollment from the first Japanese-address date; do not apply that statement to every category change. The cited ward pages do not establish a local online route. Ask the section about case-specific arrival evidence before using post. |
| Adachi | The [joining page](https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/koreiryo/kurashi/hoken/nenkin-kanyu.html), updated March 2, 2018, requires a pension step after overseas return in addition to resident registration and lists possible records, separately from retirement and dependent-loss documents. The [filing-channel page](https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/koreiryo/kurashi/hoken/nenkin-tetsuduki.html), updated November 30, 2022, lists My Number Portal procedures and limits residents' offices to simplified receipt of completed relief applications. The [postal page](https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/koreiryo/kurashi/hoken/nen0028.html), updated August 8, 2022, identifies its request form for mailed instructions and documents for coverage-loss joining and relief. The [relief page](https://www.city.adachi.tokyo.jp/koreiryo/kurashi/hoken/nenkin-shinsemenjo.html), updated August 1, 2019, describes the relief types. | The pages ask residents to confirm arrival-specific documents and do not establish the coverage-loss postal request form for overseas arrival. The postal request form sends forms to the registered address; submitting that request is not the completed pension filing. |

The cited municipal pages are in Japanese. These English supplements summarize
local routes and boundaries, not official translations. The next National
Pension batch in code order is Katsushika (`jp-13122`), Edogawa (`jp-13123`),
and Hachioji (`jp-13201`). These Tokyo records must be inserted before Nagoya
in `national-pension.ts` to preserve administrative code order.
