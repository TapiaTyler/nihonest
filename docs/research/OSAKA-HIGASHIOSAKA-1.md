# Higashiosaka City local-guidance research

Inspected 2026-09-26. Osaka Prefecture's resident-register totals dated January 1, 2026 list Higashiosaka at 478,689 residents ([official prefectural table](https://www.pref.osaka.lg.jp/documents/136365/r8sichousonnbetsujinnkou.pdf)). City-level guidance applies across the municipality; service centers and address-dependent waste and hazard details should be confirmed for the resident's neighborhood.

| Guide | Official evidence and boundary |
|---|---|
| Address registration | [Moving-in notification](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000007213.html): file within 14 days after moving; ordinary required records and counters are listed. Overseas arrivals should ask the city because required records differ. |
| National Health Insurance | [Enrollment and withdrawal](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000003168.html): lists event-specific documents, Qualification Benefits Section and administrative service-center routes, and some postal/electronic options. Eligibility remains with the national guide. |
| Household waste | [Neighborhood collection schedule](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000000082.html): dates depend on neighborhood and waste type; large-apartment bucket collection can differ. Use the current calendar and local environmental office. |
| Resident tax | [Overseas departure tax-representative notice](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000043332.html): explains January 1 municipal responsibility and the representative or final salary-withholding route. Tax-year details must be checked against current instructions. |
| Disaster preparation | [Hazard maps](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/kikikanri/0000030406.html): address lookup covers flood, landslide, and reservoir risks. The map is planning information, not an active alert. |
| Language and consultation | [2025 living consultation guide](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000040732.html) and [Japanese classes](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000032874.html): service and class schedules/languages are provider-specific and should be confirmed. |
| National Pension | [Enrollment and withdrawal](https://www.city.higashiosaka.lg.jp/0000029567.html): describes local filing cases, documents, departure filing, and some postal options. Confirm application channel for your case with the National Pension Division. |
