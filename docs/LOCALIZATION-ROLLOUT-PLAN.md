# Localization Rollout Plan

Phase 14 expands the Phase 11 Japanese pilot without weakening canonical-English ownership, revision invalidation, fallback, protected terminology, or editorial review state. Work remains repository-first and offline; it does not add a runtime translation provider, deployment dependency, or separate localization database.

## Japanese language direction

- Use polite, conversational standard Japanese in `です・ます` form.
- Apply Easy-Japanese principles to sentence structure and explanations rather than maintaining a second Easy-Japanese locale.
- Simplify the explanation, not the legal or administrative distinction.
- Preserve official Japanese terms as protected anchors and explain them in plain language when needed.
- Prefer stable wording for high-risk concepts; natural UI wording may vary only where meaning remains unambiguous.
- Add readings selectively rather than placing furigana on routine Japanese.
- Keep useful acronyms such as COE alongside the established Japanese name.
- Machine-translated, canonical-English, and non-official-translation status must remain distinct.

These are project language rules, not human approval of any generated translation. High-risk translations remain machine translated until an independent qualified reviewer approves the exact artifact revision.

## Measurable coverage contract

`npm run localization:audit` compares repository Japanese article artifacts with:

- every canonical article ID;
- the current full canonical MDX revision;
- the protected-terminology revision; and
- the translation-prompt revision.

The audit reports current machine-translated, current human-reviewed, stale, missing, and incorrectly active outdated artifacts. Missing and explicitly stale translations are valid during incremental rollout because the application falls back to English. An outdated artifact marked active is a blocking error because it could render or enter localized search as current.

Initial Phase 14 baseline:

- 95 canonical articles;
- 0 current Japanese article translations;
- 2 explicitly stale pilot artifacts;
- 93 articles without Japanese artifacts; and
- 0 human-reviewed Japanese artifacts.

The Japanese option must continue to identify itself as a pilot while interface and catalog coverage remain incomplete.
