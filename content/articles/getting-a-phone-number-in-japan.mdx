## Decide what service you need

A travel eSIM or data SIM can provide immediate internet, but it may not include a Japanese voice number. A voice number is often useful for school, housing, banking, deliveries, and emergency contact. Compare network coverage, data allowance, voice and SMS, eSIM support, minimum term, activation fee, device compatibility, cancellation cost, and multilingual support.

## Prepare for identity verification

Mobile operators must verify identity for contracts. Official guidance lists documents such as a residence card, driver’s license, My Number Card, or a passport that shows the current address; the operator determines which it accepts for the particular plan. Payment methods and additional requirements also vary. Complete address registration first when possible and check the provider’s current checklist before visiting.

If you are underage, expect the provider to require guardian consent and additional documentation. Do not borrow another person’s identity, phone contract, or payment account.

> **Practical advice:** Ask your school which providers nearby regularly serve new international students, but compare the actual written terms yourself.

Before confirming, check whether the advertised price excludes calls, activation, device installments, optional services, or later price changes. Save the contract and cancellation instructions. Update the operator when your address or residence information changes, and cancel correctly before leaving Japan.
