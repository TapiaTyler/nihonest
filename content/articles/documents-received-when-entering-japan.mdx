> **Route check:** Student-status entrants staying as mid- to long-term residents receive the residence documentation described below. A Temporary Visitor ([短期滞在](/glossary/tanki-taizai), **tanki taizai**) receives evidence of landing permission in the passport but does not receive a residence card.

## Landing permission

When entry is approved, your passport receives evidence of Landing Permission ([上陸許可](/glossary/joriku-kyoka), **jōriku kyoka**). It records the Status of Residence ([在留資格](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku), **zairyū shikaku**) and Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), **zairyū kikan**) granted. These—not the label you casually use for your visa—govern your permitted stay after entry.

## Residence card

Mid- to long-term residents receive a residence card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), **zairyū kādo**). ISA currently lists New Chitose, Sendai, Narita, Haneda, Chubu, Kansai, Kobe, Hiroshima, Fukuoka, and Naha airports as issuing it with landing permission. At other ports, the passport is endorsed to show later issuance; after you report your address, the card is generally mailed to that residence. Check the current ISA list because airport operations can change.

A Temporary Visitor should skip the resident-registration, My Number, public-insurance, and pension steps that depend on mid- to long-term residence.

Check the spelling of your name, status of residence, period of stay, expiration date, and any work-permission notation. Ask immigration or your school promptly if something appears wrong. Do not alter the card.

> **Official requirement:** A residence card is an important government identity and immigration document. Follow current rules on carrying it, presenting it when lawfully required, and reporting changes. Never lend it or share an image casually.

## Later card procedures have different fee rules

Do not assume every replacement or renewal costs the same:

- ordinary residence-card validity renewal has no separate card-issuance fee;
- replacement after loss, theft, destruction, or qualifying severe damage has no ISA fee; and
- from June 14, 2026, an optional exchange requested without one of those qualifying reasons costs **JPY 1,900** when the replacement is issued.

An extension of the period of stay is a separate immigration procedure and can carry its own fee even when the resulting card is issued as part of that permission. The newer integrated residence/My Number Card system also has case-specific issuance and reissuance rules; check ISA and your municipality's current table instead of applying the JPY 1,900 figure to every card case.

## Do not confuse arrival documents with earlier or later records

The COE is **not** a document issued when you enter Japan. It was issued before travel to support the visa and landing process. The visa was also obtained before travel. At entry, landing permission records the authorized stay and a qualifying resident receives a residence card immediately or through the later issuance route described above. The [My Number](/glossary/my-number) notice and optional My Number Card belong to a separate process after resident registration.
