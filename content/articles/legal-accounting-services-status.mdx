> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Legal/Accounting Services status is for work that may legally be performed by a person holding the relevant Japanese professional qualification. A foreign credential or overseas license alone does not necessarily authorize regulated practice in Japan.

## Who should examine it

- Attorneys, judicial scriveners, accountants, tax accountants, and other covered professionals authorized in Japan
- Professionals joining a qualifying Japanese practice

## Evidence to plan for

- Japanese registration, license, or qualification records
- Engagement or employment terms and practice details
- Professional history and the precise regulated services

## Important boundaries

Identify the Japanese law governing the profession and confirm both professional authorization and immigration status. Adjacent consulting work may fit a different status.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
