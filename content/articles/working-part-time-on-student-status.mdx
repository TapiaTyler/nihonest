> **Student status only:** This limited-work permission route does not apply to Temporary Visitors. A person studying during a Temporary Visitor stay cannot take paid work under that status.

## Student status does not itself permit work

The purpose of Student status is study. Before starting paid work or income-producing activity outside that purpose, obtain the applicable Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted (資格外活動許可, **shikakugai katsudō kyoka**). Eligible first-time entrants expecting to stay more than three months may be able to apply during landing procedures; otherwise use the responsible immigration office. Confirm that permission has actually been granted and read its scope.

Under the common comprehensive permission, work is limited to 28 hours in a week. During long school vacations defined by the institution’s rules, the limit is up to eight hours per day. The permission does not allow work in adult-entertainment businesses, and more specialized activities may require individual permission.

## Protect your studies and status

Count hours across all jobs, including training or preparation time when it legally counts as work. Keep contracts, schedules, payslips, and tax records. Check the employer, wage, duties, safety, commute, and payment method before accepting.

> **Official requirement:** Work must not displace the studies for which your status was granted. Poor attendance or activity outside permission can affect immigration outcomes.

Do not rely on promised earnings to fund the move. Ask your school or immigration authority before internships, freelancing, online work, self-employment, or any activity whose treatment is unclear.
