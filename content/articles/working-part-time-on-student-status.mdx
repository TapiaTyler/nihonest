> **Student status only:** This limited-work permission route does not apply to Temporary Visitors. A person studying during a Temporary Visitor stay cannot take paid work under that status.

## Student status does not itself permit work

The purpose of Student status is study. Before starting paid work or income-producing activity outside that purpose, obtain the applicable Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted ([資格外活動許可](/glossary/shikakugai-katsudo-kyoka), **shikakugai katsudō kyoka**). Eligible first-time entrants expecting to stay more than three months may be able to apply during landing procedures; otherwise use the responsible immigration office. Confirm that permission has actually been granted and read its scope.

Under the common comprehensive permission, work is limited to 28 hours in a week across all jobs. During long school vacations defined by the institution's rules, the limit is up to eight hours per day. A weekend or personal break is not automatically a long vacation. The permission does not allow work in adult-entertainment businesses, including otherwise ordinary duties performed at such a business.

Read the notation on your residence card and keep the permission record. An employer seeing “Student” should not assume work is allowed without checking the separate permission and its scope.

## Protect your studies and status

Count hours across all jobs, including paid training, preparation, or other time that counts as work. Keep contracts, schedules, payslips, and tax records. Check the employer, wage, duties, safety, commute, and payment method before accepting. Leaving one job does not reset hours already worked that week.

> **Official requirement:** Work must not displace the studies for which your status was granted. Poor attendance or activity outside permission can affect immigration outcomes.

Do not rely on promised earnings to fund the move. Ask ISA or an appropriately qualified professional before an internship, freelance project, online work, self-employment, performance, research payment, or another activity that may not fit the comprehensive permission. An individual permission may be relevant; a school or employer cannot grant it.

Tell the school promptly when work is damaging attendance or study. Staying below an hourly ceiling does not cure poor attendance, activity outside permission, or loss of the studies supporting Student status. After graduation, withdrawal, dismissal, or another end to enrollment, do not assume the former student permission remains usable.
