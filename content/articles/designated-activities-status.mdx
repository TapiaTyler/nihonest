> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Designated Activities is an umbrella status: the Minister of Justice designates the activity permitted for the individual. It includes many unrelated routes such as working holiday, internships, EPA candidates, domestic workers, continued job hunting, long-stay sightseeing, J-Find, start-up preparation, and digital nomad arrangements.

## Who should examine it

- People using a named Designated Activities program
- Residents checking the scope written in their individual designation

## Evidence to plan for

- The applicable designation or program requirements
- The designated-activities document and residence-card records
- Host, contract, funds, insurance, relationship, or education evidence required by that route

## Important boundaries

Never infer work permission or family eligibility from the words Designated Activities alone. Read the individual designation and the program-specific conditions.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
