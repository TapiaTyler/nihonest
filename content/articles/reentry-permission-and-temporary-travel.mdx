> **Editorial review needed:** Re-entry and residence deadlines can cause loss of the current route. Confirm dates and eligibility with ISA before departure, especially during a pending application or long absence.

## Start with three questions

1. Are you leaving temporarily and returning under the same Status of Residence ([在留資格](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku), *zairyū shikaku*)?
2. What is the exact expiry date of your current Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), *zairyū kikan*)?
3. Will you return within one year, before that status expires, and before any earlier pending-application deadline?

If the departure ends Japanese residence, use the permanent-departure guide. Re-entry arrangements preserve a route for a temporary absence; they do not close municipal, tax, employment, or housing obligations automatically.

## Special re-entry permission for an eligible short trip

Special Re-entry Permission ([みなし再入国許可](/glossary/minashi-sainyukoku-kyoka), *minashi sainyūkoku kyoka*) generally lets an eligible status holder with a valid passport return without obtaining an advance ordinary permit when the return will occur within the applicable short deadline. A mid- to long-term resident ordinarily also carries the Residence Card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), *zairyū kādo*).

At departure, mark the re-entry departure record to show that the trip is temporary and that you intend to return, and present the required records to the immigration inspector. Merely possessing the documents is not a substitute for the departure declaration.

### The deadline is whichever comes first

- one year after departure; or
- the expiry of the current period of stay.

Special re-entry cannot be extended abroad. Temporary Visitor, a person granted three months or less, and several listed enforcement or public-interest cases are excluded. If eligibility is uncertain, check before leaving.

## Ordinary re-entry permission for a longer absence

Re-entry Permission ([再入国許可](/glossary/sainyukoku-kyoka), *sainyūkoku kyoka*) is applied for before departure. It can be issued for one use or multiple uses and ordinarily for up to five years—but never beyond the existing period of stay. Special Permanent Residents have different rules.

Ordinary re-entry permission does not renew the status. If the status will expire while you are abroad, plan around that earlier expiry. ISA states that a period-of-stay extension cannot be filed at a Japanese embassy abroad. A limited consular extension of an ordinary re-entry permit, where available, is a separate procedure and still cannot extend the underlying status.

> **Travel while an extension or change is pending:** Valid ordinary or special re-entry arrangements may permit travel, but do not rely on the standard one-year headline. The Special Period ([特例期間](/glossary/tokurei-kikan), *tokurei kikan*) after the old status expires ends at the decision or two months after the old expiry, whichever comes first. Remain reachable, return by the earliest applicable deadline, and receive the decision before the special period ends.

## Build a date table before booking

Write down:

- departure date;
- planned return date;
- passport expiry;
- residence-card expiry where relevant;
- period-of-stay expiry;
- ordinary re-entry expiry, if applicable;
- one year after departure for special re-entry; and
- old expiry plus the special-period limit when an application is pending.

Use the earliest controlling date. Leave time for canceled flights, illness, document replacement, and appointment or decision collection.

## If a document is lost abroad

Report a lost passport to the local authorities and contact your nationality country's embassy or consulate for replacement or emergency travel documentation. Confirm airline requirements before traveling.

ISA states that losing a residence card abroad does not itself prevent return under special re-entry. However, a carrier may request proof of Japanese residence or the re-entry deadline. A representative in Japan can ask the responsible regional immigration office for certification of that deadline. After returning, apply promptly for a replacement residence card; when the loss was learned abroad, the ordinary 14-day replacement clock runs from re-entry.

## If the plan changes and you will not return

Do not let a temporary departure drift into an unexplained permanent departure. Determine when the re-entry permission or status became unusable, notify relevant organizations, complete outstanding municipal and tax steps, and return the residence card as instructed. ISA states that a mid- to long-term resident who leaves with re-entry permission but does not return within its validity generally must return the card within 14 days after that circumstance arises.

## Pre-travel checklist

- [ ] The trip is temporary and the current status will continue to support the return.
- [ ] Passport, residence card, and every applicable deadline were checked.
- [ ] Special re-entry eligibility and exclusions—or the ordinary permit—were confirmed.
- [ ] The departure declaration will be completed correctly.
- [ ] A pending application, decision notice, or request for evidence will remain reachable.
- [ ] Employer, school, family, municipal, insurance, and tax consequences were checked separately.
- [ ] Copies and emergency contacts are stored securely.
