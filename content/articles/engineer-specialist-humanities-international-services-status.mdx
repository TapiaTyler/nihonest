## Three kinds of professional activity

Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services ([技術・人文知識・国際業務](/glossary/gijutsu-jinbun-kokusai), **gijutsu, jinbun chishiki, kokusai gyōmu**) covers technical or natural-science work, humanities work, and specified international-services work under a contract with an organization in Japan. Activities expressly assigned to Professor, Researcher, Instructor, Intra-company Transferee, regulated professions, and other statuses remain separate.

## Common roles to examine

Official cases specifically include systems engineer, programmer, and software engineer. Related duty-dependent titles include software, web, frontend, backend, or application developer; IT, network, cloud, infrastructure, security, database, mechanical, electrical, or civil engineer; data analyst; systems architect; technical consultant; and product or technical designer.

Humanities and international-services examples include interpreter, translator, marketing, public relations, advertising, planning, product development, overseas sales, international trade, design, accounting, financial analysis, business consulting, qualifying HR work, and private-company language instruction.

For creative and digital work, useful titles to examine include graphic designer, visual or brand designer, UI or UX designer, web designer, digital-product designer, multimedia or motion-graphics designer, game designer or game artist, VFX artist, art director, creative director, content strategist, and advertising creative. ISA expressly lists “designer” and publishes design-related employment examples, but these modern titles are search aids rather than an eligibility list. Show what the worker actually researches, plans, designs, produces, and decides; how those duties use specialized knowledge or foreign-cultural sensitivity; and how the education or experience relates to that work.

Photography, film, performance, journalism, and independent artistic work require special caution. Artist, Entertainer, and Journalist have their own activity definitions, and commercial production or an independent artistic practice should not be placed here merely because a company uses the word designer or creator.

Modern titles remain broad. “Support engineer” might mean advanced systems work or routine installation; “data analyst” might mean specialized analysis or basic reporting. Describe the knowledge, decisions, outputs, and time spent on each duty.

## English teachers at private companies

ISA lists private-company language teachers as an example. This can include some English-conversation ([英会話](/glossary/eikaiwa), **eikaiwa**) employment. University teaching points first to Professor, while covered-school teaching points first to Instructor. ## Qualification pathways

- **Relevant education:** university or equivalent education, or a qualifying Japanese professional-training credential, connected to the technical or humanities work.
- **Relevant experience:** generally at least ten years of related education and practical experience.
- **International services:** generally at least three years of related experience for translation, interpretation, language instruction, public relations, advertising, overseas transactions, specified design, product development, and similar work. University graduates performing translation, interpretation, or language instruction have a stated exception to that experience requirement.
- **Recognized IT route:** an information-processing examination or qualification appearing on the current ministerial list can replace the ordinary education or experience evidence, but the work must still fit the status.
- **Remuneration and contract:** pay must be equivalent to a Japanese worker's for comparable work, under a qualifying contract with an organization in Japan.

## Mixed duties

Separate qualifying professional work from onboarding, factory-line or warehouse work, cleaning, driving, food service, retail, routine installation or data entry, general administration, business management, and licensed services. Limited connected practical training is not the same as an indefinite non-qualifying assignment. Provide approximate duty percentages and explain the permanent role.

## Evidence, period, and renewal

Use the checklist for the actual procedure and organization category. Evidence commonly covers duties, contracts, degree or transcript, experience, recognized IT qualification, relationship between background and work, remuneration, and organization records.

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. The granted period is not guaranteed. Extension can examine continuing activity, duties, remuneration, organization stability, notifications, taxes, and residence history. Changing employers or duties can require a new status-fit analysis; notification alone does not authorize the new work.

Side work—including freelance web development, consulting, or private lessons—must be checked against the current authorized activity, employment agreement, outside-activity rules, tax, and licensing before it starts.
