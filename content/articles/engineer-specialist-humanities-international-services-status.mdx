> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services covers qualifying professional duties that rely on technical or humanities knowledge, or on sensitivity and experience connected with foreign culture. Common examples include engineering, IT, interpretation, design, and some language instruction, but each application turns on the actual duties and the applicant's relevant background.

## Who should examine it

- Technical and IT professionals
- Interpreters, designers, marketers, and other qualifying humanities or international-services professionals

## Evidence to plan for

- Detailed duties and the proportion of each activity
- Degree, major, work experience, or other qualifying background
- Employer category, contract, remuneration, and business need

## Important boundaries

Routine or primarily manual work does not become professional work because of an English job title. Compare each substantial duty with the current criteria.
