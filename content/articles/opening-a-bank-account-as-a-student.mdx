## Pick an account that fits your actual needs

Ask your school whether it recommends a bank for tuition, scholarships, payroll, or campus access. Compare branch and ATM access, fees, online banking languages, international transfers, cash-card delivery, and whether the bank can support your name as shown on official identity records.

Banks perform identity and purpose checks and set their own account-opening procedures. Commonly requested records can include a residence card with current address, contact details, student or enrollment evidence, and information about the expected use of the account. A Japanese phone number or particular payment/address history may be required by some institutions, not all. Check the chosen bank’s current official list rather than treating any checklist as universal.

## Protect the account

Use the account only for legitimate personal transactions. Never sell, rent, lend, or give another person the account, cash card, PIN, passbook, login, or one-time codes. Be cautious of messages asking you to receive or forward money for someone else.

> **Official guidance:** Tell the financial institution when your address, residence period, status, school, or other requested customer information changes. Respond to legitimate review requests so transactions are not restricted.

Keep opening documents and fee information. Before permanently leaving Japan, settle payments and follow the bank’s closure procedure instead of abandoning the account.
