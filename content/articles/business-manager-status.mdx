> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Business Manager status covers substantive operation or management of a qualifying business in Japan. Criteria for new applications changed on October 16, 2025. The current framework requires careful evidence of the Japanese office, business scale and investment, qualifying staffing, management background, Japanese-language capacity, and a professionally confirmed business plan, together with proof that the operation is real and compliant.

## Who should examine it

- Founders ready to operate an established Japanese business
- Executives or managers assigned to a qualifying operation

## Evidence to plan for

- Business plan, ownership, capitalization or investment trail, and financial projections
- Office lease and evidence the premises genuinely support the operation
- Qualifying employee, experience or degree, language-capacity, licensing, tax, labor, and social-insurance records

## Important boundaries

Treat the former ¥5 million shorthand as outdated for a new application. The revised criteria include a substantially higher scale threshold and additional cumulative requirements; use ISA's current page and qualified advice for the exact business form and any transitional rule.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
