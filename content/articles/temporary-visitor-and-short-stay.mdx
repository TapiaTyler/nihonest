## Five different questions are often called “a tourist visa”

Keep these decisions separate:

1. Does the traveler need a Visa ([査証](/glossary/sasho), **sashō**) before travel?
2. If so, can they use a paper application or the JAPAN eVISA channel?
3. Is the issued visa still valid for presentation at the border?
4. Will immigration grant Landing Permission ([上陸許可](/glossary/joriku-kyoka), **jōriku kyoka**)?
5. What status and Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), **zairyū kikan**) are actually granted?

A visa supports travel to seek entry; it does not guarantee landing. Its validity window is not the same as the number of days granted after landing.

## Activities Temporary Visitor can cover

Temporary Visitor ([短期滞在](/glossary/tanki-taizai), **tanki taizai**) covers qualifying short activities such as:

- tourism, recreation, and amateur sport;
- visiting relatives or friends;
- observation, inspection, meetings, conferences, lectures, or business contact;
- short non-remunerative study fitting within the admitted stay; and
- other temporary activity consistent with the status.

The status does not authorize remunerative work in Japan. A business meeting or contract negotiation is not permission to enter Japanese employment or provide ordinary paid labor while present.

## Visa exemption is passport- and condition-specific

MOFA maintains the current visa-exemption list and its passport conditions. The period granted under current exemption arrangements is commonly fifteen, thirty, or ninety days depending on the country or region, with special bilateral handling for some nationalities.

Do not infer exemption from residence in an eligible country, a different family member's passport, a residence permit, or an earlier trip. Some exemptions require a qualifying ePassport, prior registration, a passport with a personal identification number, or another specific condition.

## eVISA is only an application channel

JAPAN eVISA currently provides an online route for eligible applicants residing in specified countries or regions to seek a single-entry short-stay visa for tourism, generally up to ninety days. It is not the channel for every nationality, residence, visiting-family, business, study, transit, work, or medical purpose.

People already exempt from a short-stay visa do not need an eVISA merely because the online system exists. Always use the current eligibility page and responsible Japanese mission.

## The granted period controls

Temporary Visitor periods are ordinarily fifteen, thirty, or ninety days, or another individually determined period within the status limit. Read the landing record rather than assuming every traveler receives ninety days.

Extensions are exceptional, not a routine method for remaining in Japan. Some bilateral exemption arrangements allow a qualifying extension beyond ninety days, and unexpected hospitalization has a separate evidence-based procedure. Neither exception creates a general right to extend tourism or use repeated short visits as residence.

## No ordinary residence card or resident onboarding

Temporary Visitors are excluded from the mid-to-long-term residence-management system and do not receive a Residence Card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), **zairyū kādo**). They do not follow the normal residence-card address-registration sequence merely because they rent accommodation or remain for several weeks.

This affects services that expect resident registration, a resident record, or a residence card. Travel insurance, accommodation, payment access, prescription planning, phone connectivity, and emergency funds should be arranged with visitor-compatible options.

## Before traveling

- Confirm nationality, passport type, passport conditions, and legal residence where applying.
- Match the visa or exemption to the real purpose rather than describing paid work as tourism.
- Use the responsible mission's current document checklist.
- Prepare itinerary, accommodation, funds, return or onward travel, inviter, relationship, and business records as applicable.
- Check the visa presentation window separately from the planned stay.
- Read the status and period actually granted at landing.
