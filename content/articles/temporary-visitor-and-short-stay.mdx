> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Temporary Visitor status covers qualifying short activities such as tourism, visiting relatives, conferences, and limited business meetings without remuneration in Japan. Some travelers are visa-exempt; others need a short-stay visa. Nationality, residence, passport, purpose, and intended duration determine the visa process.

## Who should examine it

- Tourists and family visitors
- Business visitors attending meetings or conferences without entering local employment

## Evidence to plan for

- Purpose, itinerary, accommodation, and return or onward plans
- Funds and relationship or inviter records where applicable
- The nationality-specific checklist from the responsible Japanese mission

## Important boundaries

Visa exemption is not work authorization, and a 90-day maximum is not guaranteed to every traveler. Do not plan to use repeated short stays as a substitute for a residence route.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
