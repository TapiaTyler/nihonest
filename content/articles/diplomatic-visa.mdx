## Who the category covers

Diplomat ([外交](/glossary/gaiko), **gaikō**) status applies to qualifying members of diplomatic missions or consular posts accepted by Japan, people accorded comparable privileges and immunities under treaties or international custom, and qualifying members of their household. The authorized period is described as the duration of the diplomatic mission.

Holding a diplomatic passport alone does not establish eligibility. The assignment, purpose, sending authority, Japan’s acceptance, household relationship, and any applicable bilateral arrangement determine the route. Travel for tourism, transit, or another private purpose does not become diplomatic activity merely because the traveler uses a diplomatic passport.

## Official requirements at a glance

- The activity must be a qualifying diplomatic or consular function, an activity accorded comparable privileges or immunities, or activity as a qualifying same-household family member.
- Japan must accept the mission, post, organization, or relevant protected role.
- The sending government or international organization must document the traveler’s identity and official purpose through a note verbale or equivalent official records.
- MOFA lists the passport and photograph; a visa application form is required in some cases and may be omitted in others.
- Diplomat status is granted for the period described as **during mission**, rather than from the ordinary fixed list used by many other statuses.

The sending authority must confirm whether a visa-exemption arrangement applies. Passport type, nationality, official purpose, and expected duration all matter.

## Coordinated application

This process should be managed through the sending foreign ministry, mission, consular post, or international organization and the responsible Japanese diplomatic channel. MOFA lists the passport, photograph, a note verbale or other government/international-organization documents establishing identity and purpose, and—in some cases—a visa application form.

> **Official distinction:** Visa-exemption arrangements for diplomatic passport holders differ by nationality, passport type, purpose, and duration. Entry under a short-stay exemption may result in Temporary Visitor status, not Diplomat status.

## During the assignment

Keep the accreditation or assignment office informed about arrival, family members, address, role changes, and departure. Follow its instructions for identity documents, vehicles, customs, taxes, social systems, household staff, and local registrations; special treatment cannot be inferred from ordinary resident guidance.

Privileges and immunities are legal arrangements tied to the qualifying role, not personal permissions that can be generalized. Private employment, side activity, changes of duty, and post-assignment residence require advance coordination with the sending organization and the competent Japanese authority.
