> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Japan's Digital Nomad Designated Activities route is for qualifying international remote work for a limited stay. It is restricted by nationality or region, income, insurance, activity, and duration conditions. A spouse or child may use the related family route when all requirements are met.

## Who should examine it

- Remote employees or independent professionals serving organizations or clients outside Japan
- Qualifying spouses and children accompanying the principal

## Evidence to plan for

- Contracts and a clear explanation of overseas work or clients
- Income evidence meeting the current threshold
- Required medical insurance and family relationship records

## Important boundaries

Confirm the current income threshold, eligible nationality or region, insurance amount, tax implications, and whether any Japanese client or local activity changes the analysis. It is not a general path to local employment.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
