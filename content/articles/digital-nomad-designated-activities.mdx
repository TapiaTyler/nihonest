## A temporary remote-work route

Japan's Digital Nomad Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**) route supports qualifying international remote work for a stay not exceeding six months. It is not a general relocation route and does not authorize ordinary employment by a Japanese public or private organization.

## Core requirements for the principal

Current ISA guidance requires the applicant to:

- be a national or citizen of a listed country or region covered by both Japan's Temporary Visitor visa-exemption arrangements and an applicable tax convention;
- perform international remote work, such as work through information-and-communications technology for an organization outside Japan or qualifying services sold to people outside Japan;
- have annual income of at least 10 million yen when applying; and
- hold private medical travel insurance covering the planned stay, including death, injury, and illness, with at least 10 million yen of treatment coverage for injury and illness.

The application needs a clear activity schedule, contracts or business records, income evidence, and complete policy terms. Credit-card insurance requires evidence that the actual coverage satisfies the route.

## Japanese employment and clients

The route does not permit work based on an employment or comparable contract with a Japanese public or private organization. A service that depends on entering Japan, a Japanese client relationship, local production, or mixed domestic and overseas work should be checked before relying on the Digital Nomad label.

Immigration permission and tax treatment are separate. A tax convention may affect which country can tax particular income, but nationality, length of stay, employer, client, permanent establishment, and work facts still matter.

## Six months, no extension, and no residence card

The granted period is six months. ISA states that no extension is available and that no residence card is issued. After using the maximum six-month period, a person must wait six months before applying for the same Digital Nomad status again.

Do not plan to convert the route into rolling six-month stays. A desired Japanese job, business, study program, or family basis needs its own qualifying status and procedure.

## Accompanying spouse or child

A supported spouse or child can apply for the related Digital Nomad family designation. The family member must satisfy the applicable nationality or citizenship and private-insurance conditions and provide relationship evidence. Family coverage under the principal's policy must be documented if used.

The family designation covers supported daily activities. It does not provide ordinary work authorization, and outside activity should not be assumed available.

## Evidence to organize

- passport and proof that the nationality or citizenship is currently listed;
- contracts, employer or client details, and an explanation of how the work remains foreign-facing;
- annual-income evidence under the current application instructions;
- the activity and stay schedule;
- full medical-insurance certificate, terms, period, and benefit limits; and
- marriage, birth, support, and policy-coverage records for accompanying family.

## Plan departure from the beginning

Because the route cannot be extended, arrange housing, contracts, insurance, accounts, tax advice, and return travel around a fixed departure date. If the activity changes, reassess before performing it—not when the six months are nearly over.
