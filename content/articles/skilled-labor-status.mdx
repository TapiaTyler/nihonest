> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Skilled Labor status applies only to occupational fields recognized by the immigration criteria, including examples such as foreign-cuisine chefs, pilots, sports trainers, animal trainers, and sommeliers. Each field has its own experience or qualification logic.

## Who should examine it

- Workers in a specifically covered skilled occupation
- Employers able to document a role requiring the qualifying specialized skill

## Evidence to plan for

- Detailed employment contract and duties
- Certificates and a traceable history proving the required skilled experience
- Employer, facility, menu, aircraft, sports, or other field-specific records

## Important boundaries

Do not confuse Skilled Labor with Specified Skilled Worker or general manual work. Identify the precise listed occupation and its current experience calculation before preparing the application.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
