> **Editorial review needed:** This comparison directs users to routes to examine. It does not determine whether an overseas qualification is recognized, authorize clinical practice, or replace advice from the relevant Japanese regulator and Immigration Services Agency.

## “Healthcare worker” is not one route

The workplace and English job title do not decide the status. A hospital can employ licensed clinicians, researchers, engineers, translators, administrators, and support staff under different rules. A care facility can receive Certified Care Workers, Specified Skilled Workers, EPA candidates, students, and other workers through distinct routes.

Start with the activity, the Japanese professional qualification, the exact program, and the contract.

## Licensed medical practice: Medical Services

Medical Services ([医療](/glossary/iryo), **iryō**) is the first route to examine when the person will perform medical work legally reserved to a covered Japanese-qualified professional. The current criteria include Physician ([医師](/glossary/ishi), **ishi**), dentist, pharmacist, public health nurse, midwife, Nurse ([看護師](/glossary/kangoshi), **kangoshi**), Assistant Nurse ([准看護師](/glossary/jun-kangoshi), **jun kangoshi**), and specified allied-health professions.

An overseas qualification does not itself authorize practice in Japan. Confirm the Japanese licensing path with the regulator before treating a hospital offer or immigration application as sufficient.

[Learn more about Medical Services status →](/articles/medical-services-status)

## Certified care work: Nursing Care

Nursing Care ([介護](/glossary/kaigo), **kaigo**) is the first route to examine when a registered Certified Care Worker ([介護福祉士](/glossary/kaigo-fukushishi), **kaigo fukushishi**) will provide care or care instruction under a contract with a Japanese public or private organization.

Caregiver, nursing assistant, home helper, and care worker are not interchangeable with the Certified Care Worker qualification. Confirm the person's registration and the actual care duties.

[Learn more about Nursing Care status →](/articles/nursing-care-status)

## Nursing-care field employment: Specified Skilled Worker

Specified Skilled Worker ([特定技能](/glossary/tokutei-gino), **tokutei ginō**) category (i) has a nursing-care field with its own skills, Japanese-language, employment, receiving-organization, and support rules. It can cover physical care and related support duties under the field definition.

Nursing care is not currently a category (ii) field because the professional Nursing Care status exists. Passing SSW tests does not grant Certified Care Worker qualification, and Certified Care Worker registration does not silently place someone in SSW.

[Learn more about Specified Skilled Worker →](/articles/specified-skilled-worker-status)

## EPA nurses, care workers, and candidates: Designated Activities

Economic Partnership Agreement programs use Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**) for covered nurses, care workers, and candidates. These are program-specific routes, not a general alternative available to any nationality or applicant.

Identify the exact EPA country program, whether the person is a candidate or qualified professional, the receiving arrangement, examination stage, and the individual designation. Do not apply conditions from an EPA route to an ordinary Medical Services or Nursing Care application.

[Learn more about Designated Activities →](/articles/designated-activities-status)

## Studying or waiting for qualification

Student status can support qualifying education but does not authorize full-time professional practice. A person who has not yet obtained the required Japanese qualification needs a lawful study, examination, training, or other route for the current activity.

ISA also publishes a narrow temporary Designated Activities measure for certain international students who pass the Certified Care Worker national examination through covered pathways but are waiting for the registration certificate needed to change to Nursing Care. This is not a general waiting status for every healthcare license.

[Learn more about Student status and the COE process →](/articles/student-visa-and-certificate-of-eligibility)

## Research, technology, administration, and translation

Medical research outside clinical practice may point to Researcher or Professor according to the host and duties. Medical-device engineering, health technology, hospital systems, translation, international coordination, finance, or other specialist office work may require Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services.

The healthcare setting does not convert those jobs into Medical Services. Conversely, a technical or research status does not authorize reserved clinical acts.

## Compare common situations

### Foreign-trained physician or dentist

First resolve the Japanese professional-license route and proposed clinical activity, then examine Medical Services. A foreign license, degree, or job offer alone is not enough.

### Foreign-trained nurse

Separate recognition to sit the Japanese national examination, passing the examination, obtaining the Japanese nursing license, and securing qualifying work. MHLW states that a foreign license alone does not authorize nursing work in Japan.

### Certified Care Worker

Examine Nursing Care when registration is complete and the contract is for care or care instruction. If registration is still pending after a qualifying examination route, check the specific temporary measure rather than assuming work may begin.

### Caregiver without Certified Care Worker registration

Do not label the job Nursing Care from its English title. Examine whether an SSW nursing-care route, EPA program, study pathway, or another lawful arrangement actually applies.

### Hospital researcher or laboratory worker

Determine whether the principal activity is research, technical work, or reserved clinical practice. Job location and access to patients do not decide the status.

### Medical interpreter or international coordinator

Examine the actual language, coordination, or specialist duties and the ordinary professional-work criteria. Medical Services does not cover a role merely because medical vocabulary is used.

## Questions for the applicant and organization

1. What exact acts and duties will the person perform?
2. Does Japanese law reserve any of them to a licensed profession?
3. Which Japanese license or registration is already effective?
4. If qualification is incomplete, what lawful activity and status apply now?
5. Is this ordinary licensed work, Certified Care Worker work, SSW nursing care, or an EPA designation?
6. Who is the contracting and receiving organization, and is employment direct or dispatched?
7. What remuneration, worksite, supervision, shifts, and mixed duties apply?
8. Which regulator, ISA procedure, and current checklist must be consulted?

Professional authorization, immigration permission, employment conditions, facility licensing, and employer reporting are separate checks. Complete all of them before the work begins.
