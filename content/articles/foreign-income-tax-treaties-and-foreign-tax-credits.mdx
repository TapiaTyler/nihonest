> **Editorial review needed:** Cross-border tax depends on the applicable treaty, tax residence, income source, timing, remittances, foreign tax, and procedure. This guide identifies questions; it does not calculate a treaty exemption or credit.

## Use a sequence, not one shortcut

For each foreign-connected payment, determine:

1. whether you are a tax resident ([居住者](/glossary/kyojusha), **kyojusha**), non-permanent resident ([非永住者](/glossary/hi-eijusha), **hi-eijūsha**), or non-resident ([非居住者](/glossary/hikyojusha), **hikyojusha**);
2. the income category and legal source;
3. whether Japan includes it in your taxable scope;
4. whether a tax treaty changes the domestic result;
5. whether a treaty form had to be submitted through the payer before payment;
6. which foreign tax was legally imposed and paid; and
7. whether a Japanese return and foreign-tax-credit statement are required.

The payer's country, currency, contract law, or receiving bank does not answer all seven questions. Work physically performed from Japan for a foreign employer or client can require Japanese analysis even when the money never enters a Japanese account.

## Treat tax treaties as jurisdiction-specific

A tax treaty ([租税条約](/glossary/sozei-joyaku), **sozei jōyaku**) can allocate taxing rights, reduce withholding, provide an exemption, resolve dual residence, or provide a dispute procedure. The actual article and conditions differ by treaty and income type.

Do not use “under 183 days” as a universal rule. Employment provisions can also depend on who pays or bears the remuneration, residence under the treaty, and other conditions. Student, pension, dividend, interest, royalty, business, and capital-gain provisions have different tests.

NTA guidance commonly requires an Application Form for Income Tax Convention and attachments through the payer before payment to obtain reduced withholding. When it was not submitted in time, a refund procedure may be available; relief is not necessarily automatic at the bank or payroll level.

## Foreign tax paid is not automatically deducted yen for yen

The foreign tax credit ([外国税額控除](/glossary/gaikoku-zeigaku-kojo), **gaikoku zeigaku kōjo**) can reduce double taxation for qualifying foreign income tax, but it uses Japanese credit limits. Some levies are excluded, and tax exceeding what a treaty allowed can be non-creditable.

The filing commonly requires a detailed credit statement, evidence of the foreign assessment or withholding and payment, and a calculation of foreign-source income. Carryovers require continuous statements across the relevant years. Non-permanent residents face an additional limit connected to foreign income paid in or remitted to Japan.

Preserve original returns, assessments, withholding certificates, payment receipts, translations, exchange rates, income-source calculations, treaty forms, and remittance records.

## Review common cross-border cases early

Seek individualized advice for foreign salary or remote work, stock compensation, pensions, overseas rent, investment accounts, cryptocurrency, companies or partnerships, trusts, treaty dual residence, significant foreign assets, or a move during the year. These cases can also create foreign reporting duties that are separate from the ordinary Final Tax Return ([確定申告](/glossary/kakutei-shinkoku), **kakutei shinkoku**).

Tax relief never grants immigration permission to perform the underlying activity. Confirm the residence status, outside-activity permission, employment contract, licensing, and local business rules separately.
