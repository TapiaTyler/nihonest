> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Permanent Resident status has no fixed period of stay and is not restricted to a listed work activity. It is normally obtained through a permanent-residence application from an existing status rather than through an ordinary first-entry visa. Eligibility depends on the applicable guideline and the applicant's residence, conduct, livelihood, public obligations, and route-specific circumstances.

## Who should examine it

- Established residents assessing the current permanent-residence guideline
- People who may qualify through a standard, spouse, highly skilled, or other recognized route

## Evidence to plan for

- Complete residence and immigration history
- Tax, pension, health-insurance, income, and employment or business records
- Relationship, guarantor, points, or other route-specific evidence

## Important boundaries

Long residence alone does not guarantee approval. Confirm the guideline version, required look-back periods, payment timing, absences, and any accelerated route before ordering documents.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
