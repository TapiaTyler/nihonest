## Choose the arrangement, then read the contract

Start with your school’s international office. Dormitories can simplify guarantor, furniture, and utility arrangements, but may have limited spaces and house rules. Private rentals offer more choice but often involve deposits, key money, agency fees, guarantor or guarantee-company fees, insurance, and advance rent.

Compare the total move-in cost, commute, contract term, renewal and cancellation terms, included furniture, internet, bicycle parking, and disaster risk—not rent alone. Never pay until you know who the counterparty is and have received understandable contract terms.

## On move-in day

Inspect every room and fixture. Photograph existing scratches, stains, mold, and damage with timestamps and submit the required condition report promptly. Confirm keys, emergency exits, locks, appliance instructions, and the property’s contact process.

Arrange electricity, gas, water, and internet as the property requires. Gas activation may require an appointment. Learn the municipality’s garbage categories, collection days, and designated bags; building rules may add requirements.

> **Practical advice:** Keep the lease, condition report, payment records, management-company contact, utility account details, and move-out notice deadline together.

Once the address is settled, complete municipal registration within the required period. Do not treat a postal forwarding request or notice to the landlord as a substitute.
