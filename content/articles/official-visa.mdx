## What Official status means

Official ([公用](/glossary/koyo), **kōyō**) status covers qualifying official business of foreign governments or international organizations recognized by Japan, plus qualifying family members in the same household. It excludes assignments that fall within Diplomat status. Official examples include embassy or consular employees and people dispatched on public duties by international organizations.

An official or service passport is not sufficient by itself. Eligibility depends on the traveler’s actual public duty, documentation from the sending body, Japan’s recognition of the assignment, and any applicable visa arrangement. A private holiday or transit remains a private purpose.

## Official requirements at a glance

- The activity must be qualifying official business of a foreign government or an international organization recognized by Japan, or activity as a qualifying same-household family member.
- Activity covered by Diplomat status is excluded from Official status.
- The foreign government or international organization must issue a note verbale or equivalent documents confirming identity and official purpose.
- MOFA lists a passport and photograph; the visa application form may be omitted in some cases.
- Possible periods are **five years, three years, one year, three months, 30 days, or 15 days**, with the actual period determined in the individual case.

The sending authority should verify any nationality-specific exemption before travel. An exemption for an official passport may lead to Temporary Visitor status for a qualifying short visit rather than Official status.

## Documents and route

The sending government or international organization should coordinate the process. MOFA identifies a passport, photograph, note verbale or other official documents confirming identity and purpose, and—in some cases—a visa application form. The authorized period can be five years, three years, one year, three months, 30 days, or 15 days, as granted in the individual case.

> **Official distinction:** Nationality-specific exemptions for official passport holders do not automatically produce Official status. Some exempt short visits are admitted as Temporary Visitor and remain subject to that status’s limits.

## Arrival, family, and changes

Follow the sending organization’s protocol instructions for entry, accreditation, household members, residence, and departure. Confirm separately whether each accompanying person qualifies as a same-household family member and what documentation is required.

Perform only the official activities covered by the assignment. Do not assume private employment or unrelated paid activity is allowed. A transfer, change of duties, end of assignment, family change, or plan to remain in Japan may require a new visa or residence procedure before the change occurs. Coordinate early with the sending organization and the competent Japanese authorities.
