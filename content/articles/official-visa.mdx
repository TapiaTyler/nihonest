## Who Official status covers

Official ([公用](/glossary/koyo), **kōyō**) covers:

- people performing official business of a foreign government recognized by Japan;
- people performing official business of an international organization recognized by Japan; and
- qualifying family members belonging to the same household.

Activity falling within Diplomat status is excluded. The difference rests on the recognized assignment, not seniority, prestige, employer branding, or which English title appears on a badge.

[Compare Diplomat status →](/articles/diplomatic-visa)

## Available periods

MOFA lists five years, three years, one year, three months, thirty days, or fifteen days. The individual assignment and decision determine the period actually granted.

Do not assume a short official trip and a multiyear posting use the same documentation, residence arrangements, or family process. Track the granted period and coordinate any continuing assignment before it expires.

## Official evidence and application channel

MOFA lists:

- the passport;
- a photograph;
- a note verbale or documents from the foreign government or international organization certifying identity and official purpose; and
- a visa application form, which can be omitted in some cases.

The sending organization should coordinate the application and any arrival, accreditation, assignment, or departure procedures. A host letter or government employment alone does not replace the required official recognition and documentation.

## Official passport does not always mean Official status

MOFA warns that not every official-passport holder receives an Official Visa or Official status. A person visiting privately for holiday, tourism, or transit can instead receive Temporary Visitor ([短期滞在](/glossary/tanki-taizai), **tanki taizai**) status for fifteen, thirty, or ninety days.

Diplomatic and official passport exemptions are nationality-, passport-, purpose-, and duration-specific. Some reciprocal arrangements concern official travel, while ordinary-passport exemption arrangements can admit a diplomatic or official passport holder as Temporary Visitor. The status granted at landing—not the passport cover—controls.

## Household family members are separate applicants

A spouse, child, or other claimed household member must fit the applicable same-household family definition and official documentation. Confirm identity, relationship, household connection, assignment duration, travel timing, and passport treatment for each person.

Do not assume every relative, partner, caregiver, or domestic worker shares the official's status. Other people can have separate designated or personal routes with different requirements.

## No ordinary residence card

Official status is excluded from the ordinary mid-to-long-term residence-management system. An Official-status holder does not receive the standard residence card used in ordinary resident onboarding.

Follow the sending organization and Japanese protocol instructions for identity, residence, municipal interaction, tax, healthcare, vehicles, household staff, and family arrangements. Do not apply the site's general residence-card checklist without confirmation.

## Activity changes and the end of assignment

Official status covers the recognized public duty and qualifying household activity. It does not create general private work permission. A change of employer, duty, sending body, assignment, household, or purpose can require coordinated immigration action.

Plan reassignment or departure early. If the person will remain for study, work, family, tourism, or another private purpose, identify and obtain the proper route instead of treating an official passport as continuing permission.
