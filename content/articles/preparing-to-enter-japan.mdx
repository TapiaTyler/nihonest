> **Applies to:** Students entering under Student status. Temporary Visitor students need their passport, any required short-stay visa, course evidence, accommodation, insurance, funds, and onward-travel plan, but they do not carry a Student COE or plan for residence-card registration.

## Keep these with you

Carry your passport and visa, COE information, school acceptance and contact details, Japanese accommodation address, onward instructions, and any records the school or embassy told you to present. Keep them in hand luggage, not checked baggage. Store a separate secure copy for recovery, but protect identity and financial information.

## Plan the first week

Confirm the airport, arrival time, route to your accommodation, check-in rules, and an emergency contact. Arrange access to money that will work before a Japanese bank account exists. Tell your school when you will arrive and ask about orientation, residence-card reporting, insurance, and any school-specific deadlines.

Prepare a short written address in Japanese if possible. Know whether your airport normally issues residence cards at landing or whether yours will be delivered after address registration. Do not assume every airport follows the same process.

## Final checks

- Recheck passport and visa validity and matching names.
- Confirm baggage, customs, medication, and import rules through the responsible official authority.
- Save offline copies of travel and school instructions.
- Keep enough time between arrival and mandatory school activities.
- Avoid carrying documents or items for someone else unless you know exactly what they are.

> **Practical advice:** Write a one-page arrival sheet containing the school’s phone number, accommodation address, route, and a contact who knows your plan. It remains useful if your phone has no service.
