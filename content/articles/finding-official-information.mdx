## Start with the responsible authority

For administrative questions, look first for the national agency, prefecture, municipality, or public institution responsible for the process. JapanGov provides an official directory for finding national ministries and agencies.

Procedures can differ by location. A national explanation may describe the rule while your prefecture or municipality explains the local process.

## Check the context

Before relying on a page, confirm which authority published it, what location it covers, and when it was updated. Important decisions may still require confirmation with the responsible authority or a qualified professional.
