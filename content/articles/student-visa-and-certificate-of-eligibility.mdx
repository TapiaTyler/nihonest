> **Applies to:** People using the long-term Student residence route. A person attending a qualifying short course as a Temporary Visitor does not use this COE and Student-visa sequence.

## The usual sequence

After admission, the school or another proxy in Japan commonly applies to a regional immigration services bureau for your Certificate of Eligibility (在留資格認定証明書, **zairyū shikaku nintei shōmeisho**). A COE shows that immigration has pre-examined whether the planned activity meets the relevant landing conditions; it is not a visa and does not guarantee entry.

When the COE is issued, apply for a Student visa at the Japanese embassy or consulate responsible for where you live. Use that office’s current checklist: nationality and local circumstances can change the required documents. Electronic COEs can generally be shown as specified by the authorities, but follow the embassy’s instructions.

## Check every detail

Confirm that names, passport details, school, status, and intended period are consistent across the admission record, COE, visa application, and passport. Tell the school immediately about an error or major change. Keep the COE information and school contact details accessible during travel.

> **Official requirement:** A visa permits travel to request entry; an immigration inspector makes the landing decision. Carry the documents requested for inspection and answer accurately.

Avoid non-refundable travel until the school’s schedule and your visa issuance make the timing reasonably certain. If the COE expires, enrollment changes, or you cannot arrive as planned, ask the school and the responsible embassy or consulate what must be done rather than assuming the old documents remain usable.
