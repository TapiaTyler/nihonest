> **Applies to:** People using the long-term Student residence route. A person attending a qualifying short course as a Temporary Visitor does not use this COE and Student-visa sequence.

## Four decisions, not one approval

1. The school decides admission.
2. ISA decides whether to issue a Certificate of Eligibility ([在留資格認定証明書](/glossary/certificate-of-eligibility), **zairyū shikaku nintei shōmeisho**).
3. A Japanese diplomatic mission decides the Student visa ([査証](/glossary/sasho), **sashō**) application abroad.
4. An immigration inspector decides landing permission ([上陸許可](/glossary/joriku-kyoka), **jōriku kyoka**) at entry.

After admission, you or an eligible representative in Japan applies for the COE. Many schools act as representative and provide institutional evidence. The school does not issue the COE and cannot guarantee the later visa or landing decisions.

When the COE is issued, apply for the Student visa at the embassy or consulate responsible for where you live. Use that office's current checklist: nationality and local circumstances can change the documents and method. Electronic COE information can generally be presented as the authorities specify, but follow the diplomatic mission's current instructions.

## Know who prepares which evidence

The applicant commonly provides identity, education, study-purpose, and personal-history evidence. The school provides admission, course, and institutional evidence. An expense supporter may need to document savings, income, relationship, and how funds were accumulated. The person submitting the case may be a representative without being the expense supporter.

There is no safe universal savings amount. Build a budget for tuition and living costs over the intended period, then use the current school and ISA requirements. A balance certificate without a credible source or continuity of funds may not answer the underlying question.

For affected Japanese-language institution cases, new evidence treatment applies in 2026. A prior history of 150 study hours alone no longer substitutes for the specified examination certificate or interview confirmation for the covered application dates. The institution also prepares current confirmation records. Use the current ISA notice because application type, intake, and stated exceptions matter.

## Check every detail

Confirm that names, passport details, school, course, education and employment dates, expense supporter, status, and intended period are consistent across the admission record, COE application, visa application, and passport. Explain discrepancies truthfully. Tell the school immediately about an error or material change.

> **Official requirement:** A visa permits travel to request entry; an immigration inspector makes the landing decision. Carry the documents requested for inspection and answer accurately.

Avoid non-refundable travel until the school's schedule and visa issuance make the timing reasonably certain. If the COE expires, enrollment is deferred, the course or school changes, the expense supporter changes, or you cannot arrive as planned, ask the school, ISA, and responsible diplomatic mission which records must be updated rather than assuming the old case remains usable.
