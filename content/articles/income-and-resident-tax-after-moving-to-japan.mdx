> **Draft tax orientation:** Tax residence is not the same as immigration status or nationality. Cross-border income, treaties, and departure timing need fact-specific advice from the tax office or a qualified tax professional.

## Learn the two tax timelines

National income tax ([所得税](/glossary/shotoku-zei), **shotokuzei**) applies under national rules to income within the relevant taxable scope. An employer may deduct tax through withholding ([源泉徴収](/glossary/gensen-choshu), **gensen chōshū**) and may complete year-end adjustment ([年末調整](/glossary/nenmatsu-chosei), **nenmatsu chōsei**) for eligible salary income.

Individual resident tax ([住民税](/glossary/jumin-zei), **jūminzei**) is administered locally and commonly reflects prior-year income. That delay surprises newcomers and people leaving a job: low or absent first-year billing does not mean the later bill will be low. Municipality, January 1 residence, income, and collection method matter.

## Immigration labels do not determine tax residence

Tax law separately evaluates domicile, residence duration, and income source. A Temporary Visitor can still have a tax question, while a resident-card holder's worldwide-income treatment can change over time. Do not infer “nonresident for tax” from a visa label.

## Records to keep from arrival

- employment contracts, payslips, and withholding slips;
- overseas and Japanese income records with payment dates and currencies;
- invoices and evidence of business expenses for side work;
- social-insurance, pension, donation, dependent, and other deduction records;
- entry, address, and departure dates; and
- tax-office and municipal notices.

Ask early how side income, overseas accounts or assets, remote work, stock compensation, cryptocurrency, rental income, dependents abroad, or a tax treaty affects you. Year-end adjustment is not a universal substitute for a Final Tax Return ([確定申告](/glossary/kakutei-shinkoku), **kakutei shinkoku**).
