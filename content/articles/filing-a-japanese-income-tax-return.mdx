> **Draft filing guide:** Filing duties and deadlines depend on the tax year, residence classification, income, withholding, deductions, and departure. Use the current National Tax Agency instructions.

## When to investigate a return

A Final Tax Return ([確定申告](/glossary/kakutei-shinkoku), **kakutei shinkoku**) reconciles national Income Tax ([所得税](/glossary/shotoku-zei), **shotokuzei**) for the year. Investigate the requirement if you have freelance or multiple-employer income, income not fully handled through payroll, qualifying deductions or losses, overseas income or assets, or a mid-year move or departure.

Do not use a generic “side income under a certain amount never needs reporting” shortcut. National filing exceptions and municipal resident-tax reporting are not identical, and the income calculation is not simply the cash received.

## Prepare before the filing window

Reconcile income by category, withholding, allowable expenses, social-insurance and other deductions, and payments already made. Preserve source documents and the exchange-rate method used for foreign-currency amounts. Confirm whether electronic filing, a tax-office appointment, or professional assistance is appropriate.

A licensed tax accountant ([税理士](/glossary/zei-rishi), **zeirishi**) can give individualized tax advice. Immigration professionals and employers do not automatically fill that role.

## If you will leave Japan

Resolve filing and payment before departure or determine whether to appoint a tax agent ([納税管理人](/glossary/nozei-kanrinin), **nōzei kanrinin**) under the applicable procedure. Departure can also leave later resident-tax, pension, insurance, business, and refund matters, so coordinate the tax plan with the broader departure checklist.
