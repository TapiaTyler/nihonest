> **Editorial review needed:** Entry instructions and requested documents can change. Recheck the responsible Japanese mission, airline, and ISA shortly before departure.

## First confirm that the immigration plan still matches reality

Before buying a nonrefundable ticket, compare the approved route with the current plan. The employer, school, family relationship, business, activity, entry date, and accompanying family details should remain consistent with the Certificate of Eligibility ([在留資格認定証明書](/glossary/certificate-of-eligibility), *zairyū shikaku nintei shōmeisho*) and Visa ([査証](/glossary/sasho), *sashō*).

Check the COE issue date: ordinary validity is currently three months. If the planned entry falls outside it or material facts changed, ask the sponsor and relevant authority what procedure is needed. Do not rely on expired pandemic-era extensions.

## Carry an entry file you can reach during travel

Keep originals where required and secure copies in a separate location. The useful file commonly includes:

- passport and issued visa;
- emailed COE or printed copy and its issue date;
- employer, school, host, or family contact information;
- offer, enrollment, assignment, relationship, or accommodation records appropriate to the route;
- address or temporary-accommodation details in Japan;
- arrival itinerary and onward instructions;
- medication documentation and essential health information; and
- emergency contact and secure copies of important documents.

The exact required evidence comes from the current consular and ISA instructions. An airline may also check whether documents support boarding, which is separate from the landing decision.

## Plan money and communication without circular assumptions

Carry accessible payment methods and enough funds for transport, temporary accommodation, deposits, food, and setup costs. Do not assume a Japanese bank account can be opened before registering an address, or that every rental or mobile provider accepts the same alternative payment method.

Arrange a reachable phone or data option for arrival and know how to contact the sponsor or accommodation. Use the housing, phone, and banking guides to plan a sequence rather than applying everywhere at once.

## At the port of entry

Present the requested documents and answer consistently about the intended activity and stay. A visa and COE support examination but do not guarantee Landing Permission ([上陸許可](/glossary/joriku-kyoka), *jōriku kyoka*).

After permission is granted, check the Status of Residence ([在留資格](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku), *zairyū shikaku*) and Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), *zairyū kikan*) actually recorded. Do not rely on the period requested or expected.

Eligible mid- to long-term residents receive a Residence Card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), *zairyū kādo*) at designated ports or after the later address-registration process. Before leaving the airport, check names, dates, status, work notation, and other visible details and ask about an apparent error immediately.

## Hand off to the first weeks

After establishing a residence, a new mid- to long-term resident generally reports it to the municipality within 14 days. This starts the shared settlement sequence for residence registration, My Number, applicable insurance and pension procedures, and later banking or service setup.

Keep route-specific onboarding separate: a school, employer, business, or family sponsor may have its own deadlines. Family members can receive different statuses and periods, so check every person's records individually.

## Before departure checklist

- [ ] Passport, visa, and COE remain usable for the planned date.
- [ ] Material facts still match the application record.
- [ ] Current mission, airline, and ISA instructions have been checked.
- [ ] Essential records are accessible offline and backed up securely.
- [ ] Arrival address, contact, transport, and emergency plan are recorded.
- [ ] Funds and payment alternatives do not depend on an unopened Japanese account.
- [ ] Every accompanying applicant's documents and dates were checked separately.
- [ ] The first-week municipal and route-specific appointments are mapped.
