> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

After a long-term visa is issued, preparation shifts from eligibility to a clean entry and settlement handoff. Keep the visa, COE information, offer or relationship records, and arrival instructions available during travel. Plan an address that can receive mail, enough accessible funds for deposits and setup costs, and a first-week sequence that does not assume a Japanese bank account or phone number already exists.

## Who should examine it

- Long-term entrants after the relevant visa or entry permission is ready
- Families coordinating different documents for principal and accompanying applicants

## Evidence to plan for

- Passport, visa, COE copy or electronic record, and supporting contact details
- Temporary or permanent accommodation information
- Accessible payment methods, medication records, and copies of important documents

## Important boundaries

Check the conditions printed on the visa and confirm the latest entry instructions close to departure. After landing, rely on the status and period granted at immigration—not an expected period from an earlier plan.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
