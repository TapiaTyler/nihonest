## Who this status covers

Spouse or Child of Permanent Resident ([永住者の配偶者等](/glossary/eijusha-no-haigusha-to), **eijūsha no haigūsha tō**) can cover:

- a qualifying spouse of a Permanent Resident or Special Permanent Resident; or
- their biological child who was **born in Japan and has continued to reside in Japan**.

The child definition is narrower than its English title suggests. A child born abroad or whose residence was interrupted may need Dependent, Long-Term Resident, or another analysis.

## Spouse evidence

Prepare records showing the principal person's Permanent Resident or Special Permanent Resident position, the valid and genuine marriage, shared life, residence, finances, and any unusual separation or chronology. Relevant records can come from Japanese and foreign authorities and generally require Japanese translations when written in another language.

## Child evidence and timing

For a child born in Japan, first distinguish nationality and immigration status. A foreign child who will remain beyond the initial statutory period generally needs a timely application to acquire a status after birth. Birth registration at the municipality and immigration status acquisition are separate procedures.

Document the birth in Japan, parent-child relationship, parent's status, and continued residence. Do not assume a parent's later Permanent Residence retroactively satisfies the route.

## Work, period, and renewal

This is status-based residence without an occupation restriction. Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or six months**. Renewal remains an individual review of the qualifying relationship and residence circumstances.

A spouse holder must notify ISA within **fourteen days** after divorce or bereavement. Separation, domestic violence, custody, or other urgent facts deserve early qualified help; the notification does not itself decide the next status.

## Permanent residence

Holding this status is not the same as being a Permanent Resident. A qualifying spouse or child may have an exception to the ordinary residence-history rule, but must apply separately and meet the applicable Permanent Residence guideline.

## Questions to resolve

1. Is the principal person a Permanent Resident or Special Permanent Resident?
2. Is the applicant a spouse or biological child?
3. If a child, were they born in Japan and have they continued residing here?
4. Are nationality and municipal birth procedures also involved?
5. Has the relationship or household changed?
