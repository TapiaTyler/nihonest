> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

This status can cover the spouse of a permanent resident or special permanent resident, and a qualifying biological child born in Japan who has continued to reside in Japan. The child category is narrower than a general route for every child of a permanent resident.

## Who should examine it

- Spouses of permanent or special permanent residents
- Children who satisfy the specific birth and continued-residence conditions

## Evidence to plan for

- Marriage or birth records and the principal resident's status records
- Household, residence, relationship, and financial-support evidence
- Documents explaining any separation or unusual timeline

## Important boundaries

Do not infer child eligibility from parentage alone. Confirm the birth-location and continued-residence requirements and distinguish this status from Dependent or Long-Term Resident.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
