> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Medical Services status covers qualifying healthcare work by professionals who hold the required Japanese legal qualification. Immigration permission does not replace professional licensing, language, registration, or employer requirements.

## Who should examine it

- Physicians, dentists, pharmacists, nurses, and other covered licensed professionals
- Healthcare workers with a qualifying Japanese role and authorization

## Evidence to plan for

- Japanese license and registration records
- Employment terms, facility information, and duties
- Education, professional history, and any required approvals

## Important boundaries

Confirm the exact profession is included and the Japanese qualification is effective before treating a job offer as sufficient. Nursing Care is a separate status for certified care-worker activity.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
