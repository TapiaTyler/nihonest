## What Medical Services authorizes

Medical Services ([医療](/glossary/iryo), **iryō**) covers medical work that Japanese law assigns to appropriately qualified professionals. Immigration permission does not create the professional license, and a professional license does not by itself grant a residence status.

The proposed duties must be within the Japanese qualification actually held. A hospital, clinic, or pharmacy job offer cannot replace that requirement.

## Every profession named in the current criteria

The current landing criteria name:

- Physician ([医師](/glossary/ishi), **ishi**)
- Dentist
- Pharmacist
- Public health nurse
- Midwife
- Nurse ([看護師](/glossary/kangoshi), **kangoshi**)
- Assistant Nurse ([准看護師](/glossary/jun-kangoshi), **jun kangoshi**)
- Dental hygienist
- Radiologic technologist
- Physical therapist
- Occupational therapist
- Orthoptist
- Clinical engineer
- Prosthetist/orthotist

This is a qualification-controlled list. Do not infer that an unlisted healthcare, wellness, therapy, laboratory, or support title belongs to Medical Services. Confirm the Japanese profession name, governing law, license or registration, and proposed scope of practice.

## A foreign qualification is not the Japanese license

Foreign education, experience, and licensing can be part of a Japanese recognition or examination path, but they do not independently authorize practice. Each profession has its own regulator and process.

For example, MHLW states that a person licensed as a nurse abroad must first receive recognition of eligibility to sit the Japanese national examination, pass that examination, and obtain the Japanese nursing license before working as a nurse in Japan. Current recognition criteria include a Japanese-language requirement for applicants who did not graduate from Japanese junior and senior high school. Use the current profession-specific page rather than applying the nursing process to physicians, pharmacists, or allied-health professions.

Licensing can take substantial preparation. Resolve translations, credential verification, curriculum review, examination eligibility, examination dates, Japanese-language requirements, registration, and the lawful status for any time spent preparing in Japan.

## Remuneration parity

The current Medical Services criteria require remuneration at least equivalent to that received by a Japanese worker performing comparable work. Record base pay, allowances, deductions, hours, overtime, duties, and employment conditions. Immigration parity and broader employment-law compliance are related but separate assessments.

## Two profession-specific conditions

### Assistant Nurse

For Assistant Nurse activity, the current immigration criterion limits the work to training performed within four years after the Japanese assistant-nurse license is obtained. This condition belongs to the specific qualification and must not be generalized to Nurse.

### Pharmacist and specified allied-health professions

For pharmacists, dental hygienists, radiologic technologists, physical therapists, occupational therapists, orthoptists, clinical engineers, and prosthetists/orthotists, the current criteria require invitation by a Japanese medical institution or pharmacy.

Verify the current wording and evidence for the applicant's profession. Do not assume that a commercial wellness business, remote-care platform, research company, or unlicensed facility is equivalent to the required institution.

## Evidence and organization responsibilities

Current Medical Services checklists separate physicians and dentists from the other covered professions. Evidence commonly includes:

- the applicable Japanese license or qualification certificate;
- employment conditions showing duties, hours, term, and remuneration;
- the medical institution, pharmacy, or other receiving organization;
- organization details, including the permission date where the hospital or clinic requires establishment permission; and
- procedure-specific identity, residence, tax, and continued-employment records.

The organization should describe the licensed position accurately and avoid mixing clinical duties with unlicensed work. It may support the COE or status application through an eligible representative, but ISA decides the immigration application and the professional regulator controls the license.

## Nearby roles use other analyses

Nursing Care ([介護](/glossary/kaigo), **kaigo**) is a separate status for registered Certified Care Workers providing care or care instruction. SSW nursing care and EPA candidate routes also remain separate.

Medical research outside Professor activity may point to Researcher. Medical-device engineering, health technology, translation, international coordination, or hospital administration may point to another professional status. A role does not become Medical Services simply because it occurs in a healthcare facility.

Use [Which status fits medical or care work in Japan?](/articles/choosing-a-status-for-medical-and-care-work-in-japan) when the professional or program boundary is unclear.

## Period and renewal

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. The granted period is an individual decision.

For renewal, maintain the Japanese professional authorization, qualifying duties, organization relationship, remuneration, and residence record. Use the current checklist; extensions can require employment and tax evidence. A changed profession, expired or restricted license, different institution, new duties, or move into research, administration, or care work can require a new status analysis.

## Side work and multiple facilities

Medical Services is activity-limited, not unrestricted employment. Additional clinical work must fall within both the professional license and immigration authorization. Teaching, research, consulting, telehealth, writing, business operation, or non-clinical work can require outside-activity permission or another status analysis. Professional ethics, facility rules, employment contracts, confidentiality, insurance, and tax obligations remain separate.

## Questions to resolve before applying

1. Which exact Japanese profession and legal qualification apply?
2. Is the Japanese license already effective and unrestricted for the proposed work?
3. What clinical acts, supervision, worksite, and mixed duties are planned?
4. Does the profession have a special immigration condition?
5. Is the receiving body a qualifying medical institution or pharmacy where required?
6. Is remuneration equivalent to comparable Japanese work?
7. What lawful route covers any examination or licensing period before practice?
8. Which current procedure and checklist apply?
