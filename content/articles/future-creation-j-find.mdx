## What J-Find is for

J-Find is the Future Creation Individual ([未来創造人材](/glossary/mirai-sozo-jinzai), **mirai sōzō jinzai**) Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**) route. It lets a qualifying recent graduate of a highly ranked overseas university stay in Japan for job hunting, entrepreneurial preparation, or another activity permitted by the individual designation for up to two years.

It is different from continued job hunting after graduating from a Japanese institution. It is also preparation rather than permission to begin any future job or operate any future business without the status that activity requires.

## Core eligibility questions

Confirm all of the following against the current official program:

- the applicant is at least eighteen;
- the applicant graduated from an eligible overseas university or graduate school within the preceding five years;
- the credential is at least a bachelor's degree or equivalent;
- the institution qualifies under the current methodology, generally by appearing within the top one hundred in at least two of the three named world-ranking systems; and
- the applicant can show at least 200,000 yen for initial living expenses.

The institution—not only a department, affiliated college, exchange destination, or similarly named campus—must satisfy the published method. A high ranking in one list alone does not meet a two-ranking rule.

## Activity and work boundaries

The principal activity must remain the job search, entrepreneurial preparation, or other activity described in the designation. Do not assume J-Find grants unrestricted employment or an unrestricted freelance business. Read the designation and confirm whether any income-producing activity is included or needs separate permission.

Before starting ordinary employment, compare the actual duties, qualifications, contract, remuneration, and organization with the appropriate work status.

Before establishing and operating a business, distinguish preparation from the requirements for Business Manager or another appropriate status.

## Duration is capped and can be cumulative

J-Find can permit up to two years, commonly through shorter periods that require extension while the conditions and activity continue. Do not treat every extension as automatic.

The maximum is not necessarily a fresh two-year clock. ISA guidance identifies time under certain similar job-hunting or entrepreneurial-preparation Designated Activities arrangements as relevant to the cumulative calculation. Document prior statuses and designations before planning the end date.

## Accompanying spouse or child

A qualifying spouse or child can seek the related accompanying Designated Activities route. The family member needs a separate application and relationship, support, and insurance or other program evidence as required. Their permitted activity is not the same as the principal's future-creation activity, and ordinary paid work should not be assumed.

## Evidence to organize

- degree, graduation date, and institution records;
- evidence connecting the institution to the current ranking methodology;
- age, passport, and identity records;
- proof of initial living funds;
- a concrete job-search or entrepreneurial-preparation plan;
- records of earlier related Designated Activities periods; and
- relationship and support evidence for each accompanying family member.

## Plan the exit or transition

Track both the current period and the cumulative maximum. A job offer, company registration, investment, or family application does not itself extend J-Find or authorize the new principal activity. File the correct change in time, or prepare to leave before permission ends.
