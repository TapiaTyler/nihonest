## What this route covers

The Future Creation Individual route, often called J-Find, is a Designated Activities pathway for recent graduates of eligible highly ranked overseas universities to conduct job hunting, entrepreneurial preparation, or other permitted future-creation activity in Japan. Eligibility is tied to current school-list, graduation-timing, funds, and activity conditions.

## Who should examine it

- Recent graduates whose institution appears under the current program methodology
- Eligible applicants with a concrete job-search or entrepreneurship plan

## Evidence to plan for

- Degree and graduation-date records
- Proof the institution qualifies under the current list or methodology
- Activity plan, funds, insurance, accommodation, and accompanying-family records where applicable

## Important boundaries

The eligible-university list and financial conditions can change. Confirm the exact activity and whether any proposed paid work is authorized before beginning it.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
