> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Artist status is for qualifying income-producing artistic activity such as composing, visual art, writing, or photography. Cultural Activities may fit unpaid study or research, while Entertainer may fit performance and show-business activity. The planned work, income, record, and Japan-based arrangements need to support the selected category.

## Who should examine it

- Established creators carrying out covered artistic work
- Artists with concrete commissions, contracts, or a sustainable activity plan in Japan

## Evidence to plan for

- Portfolio, professional history, awards, publications, or exhibitions
- Contracts, commissions, schedules, and expected remuneration
- A realistic financial and activity plan

## Important boundaries

Confirm whether the work is creative production, performance, instruction, or unpaid cultural study. Freelance work is not automatically outside immigration restrictions.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
