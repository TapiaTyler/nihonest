> **Check before every driving period:** A valid document from abroad is not automatically authority to drive in Japan. Confirm the document type, original validity, issue date, landing history, and current NPA rules. Rental-company acceptance is an additional commercial check, not proof of legality.

## Identify which legal route you hold

Japan recognizes three basic forms of driving authority:

1. a valid Japanese driver's license;
2. an original International Driving Permit ([国際運転免許証](/glossary/kokusai-unten-menkyo-sho), **kokusai unten menkyoshō**) issued under the 1949 Geneva Convention in the prescribed form; or
3. a valid license from a country or region specifically recognized by Japanese rules, carried with a Japanese translation prepared by an authorized issuer.

A document advertised as an “international driver's license” is not enough. A permit based on a different convention, an unofficial translation, a photograph, or a digital copy cannot be treated as the required original. The NPA's April 2026 guidance lists the currently recognized foreign-license jurisdictions and translation conditions; check the live list rather than copying an old country list.

## Calculate the shorter permitted period

For a qualifying international permit or recognized foreign license, driving authority generally ends at the earlier of:

- one year from the relevant landing date in Japan; or
- the document's own expiration.

The permit itself is generally valid for one year from issuance, but that printed validity does not override Japan's landing-based limit. Keep the passport or other records needed to establish the relevant landing date.

For a person recorded in Japan's Basic Resident Register, leaving and returning in less than three months generally does not create a new landing date for this calculation. Do not make a short trip abroad to “reset” the year. [Re-entry permission](/articles/reentry-permission-and-temporary-travel) preserves immigration status under its own rules; it does not independently renew driving authority.

## Carry the complete set

Before driving, carry the original license or permit and every required companion document, such as the original foreign license, authorized Japanese translation, and passport. Check the exact NPA combination for your route.

Also verify:

- the vehicle category you may drive;
- restrictions printed on the license or permit;
- rental, employer, or insurer requirements;
- whether your address and travel history make Japanese conversion the appropriate next step; and
- [Japan's traffic and accident rules](/articles/driving-rules-and-traffic-accidents-in-japan).

An insurer or rental company may impose a stricter document requirement. Conversely, a company handing over keys does not establish that driving is lawful.

## When temporary authority will not work

Do not drive if the issuing route, format, translation, originals, category, validity, or timing cannot be confirmed. Public transport, a properly licensed driver, or another safe option is preferable to testing an uncertain interpretation on the road.

Residents who need to drive beyond the temporary period should investigate Foreign-license Conversion ([外免切替](/glossary/gaimen-kirikae), **gaimen kirikae**) early. Appointments and evidence can take time, and applying does not extend temporary driving authority.
