## A job change can create more than one immigration question

For covered employment- and institution-based statuses, a change involving the affiliated or contracting organization may require a Notification Concerning an Affiliated Organization ([所属機関に関する届出](/glossary/shozoku-kikan-todokede), **shozoku kikan ni kansuru todokede**). Reportable events can include the organization changing its name or location, ceasing to exist, a contract ending, or a new contract beginning.

ISA's procedure sets a 14-day period for covered events. File through an accepted channel and retain the submission record with the contract, separation documents, and residence-card records.

## Notification is not permission

Submitting the notice does not certify that the new duties fit the current Status of Residence ([在留資格](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku), **zairyū shikaku**). Before starting, separately determine whether the work is within scope, needs outside-activity permission, or requires a change of status. A certificate of authorized employment may help in some cases, but it is a separate procedure.

Also distinguish an employer's required notification from your own resident notification. One party's filing does not necessarily replace the other's obligation.

## Build a safe changeover file

Keep the old and new contracts, detailed duty descriptions, work locations, start and end dates, organization registration information, pay terms, and proof of each filing. Check the expiry date on your Residence Card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), **zairyū kādo**) because a contract change does not extend the period of stay.
