> **Editorial review needed:** Journalist depends on journalistic activity and the required foreign-news-organization relationship. A press card, portfolio, publication, or self-description does not decide eligibility.

## The two central requirements

Journalist ([報道](/glossary/hodo), **hōdō**) covers reporting and other journalistic activity performed under a contract with a foreign news organization. ISA gives reporters and camera personnel as examples.

Analyze both parts:

1. Is the organization a foreign news organization for this purpose?
2. Is the proposed work reporting or another journalistic activity under a contract with it?

The subject matter can be newsworthy without the relationship satisfying the status. Likewise, working for a foreign organization does not help if the actual output is advertising, corporate communications, entertainment production, or unrelated content.

## Employment, dispatch, and freelance contracts

The current ISA procedure recognizes dispatch by a foreign news organization, employment in Japan by a foreign news organization, and activity under a non-employment contract with a foreign news organization, including qualifying freelance work.

Freelancer is therefore not an automatic rejection or approval. The contract should establish the organization, activity, period, position or role, and remuneration. If a contract omits those facts, current materials call for a document from the foreign news organization supplying them.

A freelancer with several outlets should identify each contract, assignment, payment arrangement, and expected body of work. Independent content made without the required foreign-news relationship needs comparison with other routes.

## Evidence to prepare

Current procedures distinguish a category involving a foreign news organization that employs a person holding a foreign-press registration card issued through the Ministry of Foreign Affairs from other organizations and individuals. The evidence route differs by category.

Depending on the facts, prepare:

- dispatch instructions, employment conditions, or non-employment contracts;
- activity, assignment, duration, role, and remuneration details;
- an overview of the foreign news organization, including its history, organization, facilities, staff, and reporting record;
- professional history and representative published or broadcast work; and
- work locations, expected coverage, and relationships with any Japanese bureau or receiving organization.

Press credentials can support the record but should not replace the contract and activity evidence required for the application category.

## Common roles and boundary questions

Reporter, correspondent, news editor, broadcast journalist, photojournalist, and news photographer are useful search terms. For editors, camera personnel, producers, researchers, and documentary workers, explain how the actual duties contribute to reporting and how the foreign news organization commissions and uses the work.

### Photography

A foreign-news photojournalist can point to Journalist. Fine-art photography can point to Artist, commercial photography is expressly addressed in Entertainer materials, and in-house studio work may require another employment analysis. Camera equipment and visual style do not decide the category.

### Film, broadcast, and documentary work

News reporting under the required foreign-media contract differs from commercial film or broadcast production under Entertainer. Independent documentary work can require individual analysis of the commissioning organization, editorial purpose, contract, income model, and production duties.

### Corporate and promotional content

Public relations, advertising, brand content, corporate communications, and marketing do not become journalism merely because they use interviewing, writing, editing, or video skills. Employed specialist work may instead require comparison with Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services.

Use [Which residence status fits creative and media work?](/articles/choosing-a-residence-status-for-creative-and-media-work) before selecting a route.

## Period and renewal

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. The granted period is an individual decision.

For an extension, retain current contracts or continued-dispatch evidence, activity and remuneration records, published output, organization information where requested, and tax and residence records. A change from foreign-news reporting to domestic-media employment, advertising, production, or independent content can require a different status rather than renewal as Journalist.

## Side assignments and changes

Do not assume every writing, photography, speaking, teaching, documentary, or commercial commission is included. Compare each paid activity with the authorized reporting activity and foreign-news contract. Check outside-activity permission, status-change, contract-notification, tax, and professional rules separately before beginning work.

## Questions to resolve

1. Which foreign news organization is the contractual counterparty?
2. Is the arrangement dispatch, employment, or a non-employment contract?
3. What reporting or journalistic work will be produced?
4. What are the period, role, remuneration, and work locations?
5. How will the organization publish, broadcast, or otherwise use the work?
6. Are any assignments advertising, corporate, artistic, or commercial production instead?
7. Which current application category and checklist apply?
