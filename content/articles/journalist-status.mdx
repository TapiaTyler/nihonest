> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Journalist status covers qualifying journalistic activity based on a contract with a foreign news organization. Reporting, editing, news photography, and broadcasting may fit, but corporate communications, domestic-media employment, and independent content creation can raise different category questions.

## Who should examine it

- Correspondents and news staff assigned by foreign media
- Qualifying freelance journalists with an appropriate contractual basis

## Evidence to plan for

- Contract or assignment records from the foreign news organization
- Professional history and published work
- Japan coverage plan, remuneration, and expected duration

## Important boundaries

Confirm that the contracting organization and actual output fit the Journalist category. Do not rely only on a press credential or the applicant's description as a journalist.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
