> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Japan does not issue one unrestricted working visa for every job. Most work-authorized statuses are tied to defined activities, and the correct route depends on actual duties, qualifications, the contracting organization, and sometimes a regulated license or industry. The employer or another eligible proxy commonly prepares the Japan-side Certificate of Eligibility application before the overseas visa application.

## Who should examine it

- People with a Japanese job offer
- Employers comparing work-status categories before preparing a COE

## Evidence to plan for

- A detailed job description rather than only a job title
- Employment terms, employer records, and work location
- Degrees, work history, licenses, or skills evidence relevant to the proposed status

## Important boundaries

Ask the employer which status it intends to support and compare the duties with ISA's current category description. A COE supports the process but does not guarantee visa issuance or landing.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
