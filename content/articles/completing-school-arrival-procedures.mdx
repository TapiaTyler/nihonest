> **Applies to both routes:** All students should complete their program's check-in and academic procedures. Only students registered as residents should submit residence-card, address, insurance, or My Number information when the school legitimately requires it.

## Use your school’s instructions as the authority

Arrival procedures vary by institution and program. Attend the required orientation and confirm enrollment, tuition status, student ID, course registration, placement tests, academic calendar, attendance rules, campus systems, emergency contacts, and where to seek immigration, disability, wellbeing, harassment, or academic support.

Tell the school your current address and contact details through its secure process. If you are a Temporary Visitor, make that route clear and ask which visitor documents the program needs. If you are a registered resident, ask what evidence it needs after municipal registration and whether it offers help with health insurance, pension, housing, a phone, or banking. Do not email sensitive identity numbers unless the school explicitly provides a secure method and explains why they are needed.

## Build a working semester checklist

- Record add/drop, tuition, scholarship, examination, and vacation dates.
- Learn how attendance is recorded and whom to contact when sick.
- Ask what happens after extended absence, leave, transfer, withdrawal, dismissal, or graduation.
- Confirm health-clinic, counseling, disability, harassment, and emergency support.
- Register for disaster alerts and learn the campus evacuation point.
- Keep immigration and school expiration dates visible in a private calendar.

> **Practical advice:** Ask questions early. A missed school procedure can affect enrollment, scholarships, housing, or the records used in later immigration applications.

If your program, address, contact details, financial situation, or arrival date changes, notify the responsible school office promptly. If you transfer, withdraw, are dismissed, graduate, or cannot continue studying, ask both the school and ISA which separate student and institutional actions apply. A school report does not automatically change or extend your status.

School advice is valuable, but immigration, municipal, pension, insurance, banking, and employment decisions remain governed by their respective authorities. If you travel outside Japan during a school vacation, use the temporary-travel and re-entry guide; a school calendar does not extend your period of stay.
