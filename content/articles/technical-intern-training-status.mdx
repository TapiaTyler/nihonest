> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Technical Intern Training is a regulated status tied to an approved training plan, implementing organization, supervising arrangements where applicable, occupation, and stage. Japan has legislated a successor employment-for-skill-development system, so applicants and current participants need to verify which rules and transition dates apply when they act.

## Who should examine it

- People entering or continuing an approved technical intern plan
- Participants comparing a lawful transfer or transition to another status

## Evidence to plan for

- Approved training plan and program-stage records
- Contracts, compensation, workplace, supervision, and housing information
- Test results, completion records, and any transfer or transition documentation

## Important boundaries

Treat recruitment fees, withheld documents, coercion, unpaid wages, or work outside the plan as serious warning signs. Verify current protections and transition rules with ISA, the responsible program bodies, or an independent support service.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
