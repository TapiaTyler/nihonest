## What the Start-up pathway actually is

Japan's Start-up Visa is a supported preparation program implemented through Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**) No. 44. It can give an eligible foreign entrepreneur time in Japan to prepare a business with an approved organization before applying separately for Business Manager ([経営・管理](/glossary/keiei-kanri), **keiei kanri**).

It is not a separate residence-status category called Start-up, and it does not authorize an indefinite preparation period. It also does not guarantee that the business will later meet Business Manager requirements.

## The two decision makers

The entrepreneur first applies to an approved Foreign Entrepreneurship Promotion Organization—currently an eligible municipality or private organization. That organization reviews the entrepreneurial preparation plan under its program. If satisfied, it issues the confirmation needed for the immigration process.

The applicant then makes a separate application to the Immigration Services Agency. ISA decides whether to grant the applicable Designated Activities permission. Local plan confirmation is important evidence, but it is not an immigration approval, visa, landing permission, or later Business Manager decision.

## Programs vary by location and organization

Start by checking METI's current list of approved programs. Each organization may define:

- the geographic area in which the business must be prepared or established;
- accepted industries or policy priorities;
- application windows, interviews, language, and required plan format;
- evidence of financing, professional background, market demand, residence, or local connection;
- support services, milestones, progress reports, and meeting schedules; and
- conditions for continued confirmation or a later renewal.

Do not rely on another city's checklist. Confirm whether the program can accept the business, whether relocation is required, and what happens if milestones change before committing funds.

## Length of the preparation period

The unified national framework introduced in January 2025 allows supported preparation for **up to two years**. This is a maximum pathway length, not a guaranteed two-year grant at entry. The actual period appears in the person's immigration permission, and continued stay depends on the applicable extension reviews and satisfactory program progress.

MOFA's overseas visa materials may describe a six-month visa or residence period within that process. Read the program, COE or immigration permission, and consular visa as separate documents rather than assuming each promises the full two years.

## What to prepare

A strong plan normally connects:

- the product or service, customer, market, revenue, and competitive evidence;
- the founder's experience and actual role;
- ownership, funding source, financing, and traceable movement of money;
- incorporation, premises, licensing, hiring, and operating milestones;
- living expenses during preparation;
- the program's local or industry conditions; and
- a credible path to every current Business Manager requirement.

Expect continuing monitoring. METI's framework requires the promotion organization to provide support, check progress regularly, and report under the program. Preserve meeting records, revised plans, incorporation and banking documents, contracts, expenses, premises work, hiring efforts, and professional advice.

## Planning for Business Manager

The Start-up pathway gives preparation time; it does not waive the requirements in force when the later application is filed. Since October 16, 2025, the Business Manager framework includes an appropriate business place, a qualifying full-time employee, at least ¥30 million in qualifying business assets or capital, Japanese capacity, applicant education or management experience, a professionally evaluated business plan, and wider legal compliance.

Read [Business Manager visa and status of residence](/articles/business-manager-status) early and build the program milestones backward from the intended filing date. Incorporation alone does not establish Business Manager activity, and spending money is not a substitute for documenting the source, use, and qualifying treatment of the investment.

## If the plan changes or cannot reach the target

Tell the promotion organization promptly about changes to the business, location, funding, founder team, premises, or timeline. Those facts may affect program support, progress confirmation, and immigration filings.

Before the authorized preparation period ends, either file the appropriate application supported by current evidence or make another lawful departure or status plan. Do not assume that a pending local discussion extends the period of stay. A different activity—such as employment for another company—requires its own residence-status analysis.

Company, tax, employment, licensing, and immigration rules remain separate. Use the approved organization for its defined support and qualified professionals for issues outside that role.
