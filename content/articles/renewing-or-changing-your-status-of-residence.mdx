## Extension and change answer different questions

An Extension of Period of Stay ([在留期間更新許可](/glossary/zairyu-kikan-koshin-kyoka), *zairyū kikan kōshin kyoka*) asks to remain longer under the current Status of Residence ([在留資格](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku), *zairyū shikaku*).

A Change of Status of Residence ([在留資格変更許可](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku-henko-kyoka), *zairyū shikaku henkō kyoka*) asks to move to a different authorized activity or personal position without first leaving Japan.

Use an extension when the underlying basis continues. Examine a change when the future activity or position is materially different—for example, moving from study to professional work or from dependent residence to a principal work route. A new employer alone does not always require a status change, and a similar job title does not prove that the new duties remain inside the current status.

## Start from the card, then work backward

Read the current Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), *zairyū kikan*) and expiry date on the Residence Card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), *zairyū kādo*). For a period of six months or more, ISA generally accepts an extension application from three months before expiry. Shorter periods use a different opening rule.

Build earlier internal deadlines for employer, school, family, translation, tax, or social-insurance records. Use the current route-specific evidence list and fee rather than an old saved checklist.

## A timely pending application has a limited special period

If a qualifying extension or change application was filed by the old expiry date and remains undecided, the Special Period ([特例期間](/glossary/tokurei-kikan), *tokurei kikan*) can preserve the old status until:

- ISA makes the decision; or
- two months pass after the old expiry date,

whichever happens first.

This is not an automatic two-month renewal. Keep the application receipt or online record, monitor notices, remain reachable, and collect the decision promptly.

> **Travel while the application is pending:** Do not rely on the ordinary “return within one year” summary. Travel requires valid Re-entry Permission ([再入国許可](/glossary/sainyukoku-kyoka), *sainyūkoku kyoka*) or Special Re-entry Permission ([みなし再入国許可](/glossary/minashi-sainyukoku-kyoka), *minashi sainyūkoku kyoka*). The return deadline may instead be tied to the old expiry and special period. ISA advises applicants to remain reachable, return before the earliest applicable deadline, and receive the decision before the special period ends.

## Filing a change does not authorize the new activity

Until a change is granted, the existing status and any separate permissions continue to control. Do not begin work or another activity outside that scope merely because an application was accepted. If timing is unavoidable, obtain case-specific guidance before acting.

## Approval remains discretionary

ISA examines whether the activity or position fits the requested status and whether there are sufficient grounds to approve the extension or change. Residence history, compliance, evidence, livelihood, and other circumstances can matter. Meeting a published example or submitting every listed document does not guarantee approval.

## Maintain a reusable application file

- passport and residence card;
- current status, period, and prior application copies;
- contract, enrollment, relationship, business, or other basis records;
- detailed future activity and organization evidence;
- tax, pension, insurance, address, and compliance records where relevant;
- Japanese translations of foreign-language documents; and
- submission receipt, requests for additional material, decision notice, and updated card.

If a job, school, contract, spouse, or address changed, check whether a separate notification was or remains due. Extension or change does not silently replace those duties.
