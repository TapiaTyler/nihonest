## Extension and change answer different questions

An Extension of Period of Stay ([在留期間更新許可](/glossary/zairyu-kikan-koshin-kyoka), **zairyū kikan kōshin kyoka**) asks to continue under the current status beyond the present Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), **zairyū kikan**). A Change of Status of Residence ([在留資格変更許可](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku-henko-kyoka), **zairyū shikaku henkō kyoka**) asks to move to a different authorized activity or personal position.

A new employer, graduation, marriage, divorce, business launch, or major duty change does not automatically reveal which procedure applies. Compare the actual future activity with the current status and obtain case-specific guidance before starting something outside its scope.

## Maintain a renewal file throughout the year

Keep the Residence Card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), **zairyū kādo**), passport, contracts, enrollment or family evidence, tax and social-insurance records, address history, and copies of prior filings organized. Check the card's expiry date independently of passport, contract, or school dates.

Use the current application window, form, evidence list, fee, and collection instructions. Calendar earlier internal deadlines for employer, school, family, translation, or municipal documents so missing evidence does not become a last-week emergency.
