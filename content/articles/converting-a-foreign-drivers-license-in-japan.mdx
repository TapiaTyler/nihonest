> **Prefecture-specific procedure:** National law creates the conversion route, but you apply through the prefectural police authority for your registered Japanese address. Its appointments, document instructions, supported languages, checks, and fees control the actual filing.

## Confirm the foundation before booking

Foreign-license Conversion ([外免切替](/glossary/gaimen-kirikae), **gaimen kirikae**) can allow a person holding a valid foreign license to obtain the corresponding Japanese Driver's License ([運転免許証](/glossary/unten-menkyo-sho), **unten menkyoshō**) with part of the ordinary examination exempted.

The applicant must generally show that, after receiving the foreign license, they stayed in its issuing country or region for a total of at least three months. Passport stamps alone may be incomplete because automated gates and residence histories do not always produce stamps. Start gathering old passports, entry and exit records, residence permits, school or employment records, tax or utility records, and an issuing-authority license history where relevant. The licensing center decides what establishes the period.

The foreign license must remain valid. Renewal, replacement, category-addition, or an issue date that hides the original licensing date may require a full history from the issuing authority.

## Use the licensing center for your Japanese address

Check your prefectural Driver's License Center ([運転免許センター](/glossary/unten-menkyo-center), **unten menkyō sentā**) before translating or booking travel. Confirm:

- whether advance screening and an appointment are required;
- acceptable proof of Japanese address and residence;
- original passport and travel-history requirements;
- complete original foreign-license history;
- which authorized organizations may translate your particular license;
- photograph, fee, and application-form rules;
- interpreter or language arrangements; and
- which knowledge and practical checks apply to the issuing jurisdiction and license category.

NPA guidance recognizes translations prepared through specified public authorities, consular or equivalent bodies, and designated organizations under the applicable rules. A translation accepted for one jurisdiction is not automatically valid for another.

## Expect document review before tests

The center first needs to establish identity, address jurisdiction, license validity, categories, and the required post-licensing residence. Inconsistent names, dates, romanization, categories, or passports can delay the application.

Exemption from a knowledge or practical check depends on current treatment of the issuing jurisdiction and the person's evidence; it is not a nationality benefit. Where checks apply, use the current prefectural format and safety criteria. Passing document review or one check does not authorize driving until the Japanese license is actually issued.

## Check the license you receive

Before leaving, verify the name, birth date, address, vehicle categories, automatic-transmission or other restrictions, expiration, and instructions for renewal or changes. Ask immediately about an error.

Conversion also does not settle vehicle access or insurance. If buying a vehicle, separately review [registration, parking, inspection, tax, and insurance](/articles/buying-registering-and-insuring-a-vehicle-in-japan). Before driving, review [traffic rules and collision duties](/articles/driving-rules-and-traffic-accidents-in-japan).
