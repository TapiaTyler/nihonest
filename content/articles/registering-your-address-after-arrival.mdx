## Report where you live

After deciding your residence, a mid- to long-term resident must notify the municipal office for that address within 14 days. This is commonly called a moving-in notification (転入届, **tennyū todoke**). Bring your residence card. If your passport says the residence card will be issued later, bring that passport instead. Ask the municipality about any additional documents, especially for family members.

The office records you in the Basic Resident Register and updates the address on the residence card when applicable. Check the result before leaving. Your school may also require a copy of updated information; follow its secure submission process.

## Use the municipal visit well

Procedures for National Health Insurance, National Pension, and other local services may be handled during the same visit or at another counter. Counter locations, forms, translations, appointments, and accepted supporting records vary, so consult the municipality’s official site.

> **Official requirement:** The 14-day period runs from establishing your residence, not simply from the day you entered Japan.

Keep any receipt or guidance the office provides. When you later move, there are separate move-out or change-of-address procedures. Updating only the school, landlord, bank, or postal service does not replace the required municipal and immigration-related notifications.
