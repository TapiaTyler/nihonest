## What this route covers

Highly Skilled Professional status covers advanced academic research, specialized technical or professional work, and business management through category-specific pathways. The conventional route uses a points calculation; the special highly skilled professional pathway commonly called J-Skip uses separate education, career, and income thresholds. Benefits and permitted accompanying-family arrangements depend on the approved route and conditions.

## Who should examine it

- Advanced researchers, specialists, executives, and entrepreneurs who may satisfy the relevant points route
- Applicants examining the separate J-Skip thresholds

## Evidence to plan for

- Points calculation with proof for every claimed item
- Degree, career, income, role, employer, and activity records
- Documents for any accompanying family or household arrangements

## Important boundaries

Do not combine criteria from different HSP activity categories or assume a points estimate guarantees approval. MOFA requires a COE for the Highly Skilled Professional visa, and the authorized activity remains important after entry.

Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
