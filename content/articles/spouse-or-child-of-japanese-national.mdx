> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

This status is based on a qualifying relationship to a Japanese national rather than a particular work activity. It can cover a spouse, biological child, or child specially adopted under the relevant Civil Code provisions. Immigration examines the legal relationship and, for spouses, whether the marriage is genuine and continuing.

## Who should examine it

- Spouses of Japanese nationals
- Qualifying biological or specially adopted children

## Evidence to plan for

- Japanese family register and foreign civil-status records
- Relationship history, shared-life evidence, and clear explanations where needed
- Residence, support, guarantor, and household information

## Important boundaries

Marriage to a Japanese national does not itself grant status or guarantee a particular period. Report material changes and use the current checklist for the exact relationship and application procedure.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
