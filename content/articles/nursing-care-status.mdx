> **Editorial review needed:** Caregiver and care worker are broad English labels. Nursing Care status specifically requires Certified Care Worker qualification and registration, a qualifying contract, and covered care activity.

## The status and qualification have similar names

Nursing Care ([介護](/glossary/kaigo), **kaigo**) authorizes a person who holds Certified Care Worker ([介護福祉士](/glossary/kaigo-fukushishi), **kaigo fukushishi**) qualification to provide care or teach care under a contract with a Japanese public or private organization.

The occupation, Japanese qualification, and residence status are connected but distinct. A facility's willingness to hire, passing an examination, receiving a registration certificate, and obtaining immigration permission are separate events.

## Registration is essential

The current application materials request the Certified Care Worker registration certificate. Passing the national examination does not by itself mean the registration certificate has been issued. Confirm registration timing before setting a start date or filing a change to Nursing Care.

Since April 1, 2020, Nursing Care status has been available regardless of the route through which the person obtained Certified Care Worker qualification. Older explanations limiting it to one education route are outdated.

## A narrow bridge while registration is pending

ISA publishes a temporary measure for certain international students who pass the Certified Care Worker national examination through the practical-experience or welfare-high-school routes but cannot obtain the registration certificate before the new work period begins. A change to the specified Designated Activities route can allow care work while the certificate is pending.

This is not automatic and is not a general bridge for every licensing delay. The person must use the described application route and provide the required examination and employment evidence. Check the current measure with ISA before work begins.

## Contract, duties, and organization evidence

Prepare:

- the Certified Care Worker registration certificate;
- written employment conditions and a detailed duty description;
- the employing and receiving organization, facility, and worksite details;
- remuneration, hours, shifts, supervision, and contract duration;
- any dispatch contract and receiving-site information where applicable; and
- current procedure-specific identity, residence, tax, and employment evidence.

For applications from April 15, 2026, the Nursing Care page identifies an additional declaration concerning the representative of the affiliated organization. Use the current COE, change, or extension checklist rather than an older saved copy.

The organization should not advertise a generic “caregiver visa.” It should name the proposed status, required qualification, actual duties, worksite, and any program under which the person is being accepted.

## Nursing Care is not Medical Services

Nurse ([看護師](/glossary/kangoshi), **kangoshi**) and Certified Care Worker are different Japanese qualifications. Licensed nursing and other regulated clinical work point to Medical Services ([医療](/glossary/iryo), **iryō**), while Certified Care Worker activity points to Nursing Care.

Care work does not become nursing merely because it occurs in a hospital or includes health-related support. Reserved clinical acts, care duties, supervision, and the person's actual qualification must be identified precisely.

## Nursing Care is not SSW nursing care

Specified Skilled Worker ([特定技能](/glossary/tokutei-gino), **tokutei ginō**) category (i) includes a nursing-care field. Its current field rules use a care skills evaluation, Japanese-language evidence, direct employment, accepting-organization requirements, and the category (i) support framework. Nursing care is not a category (ii) field because the professional Nursing Care status exists.

SSW field eligibility does not equal Certified Care Worker registration. A person who later obtains the professional qualification should examine whether and when to change to Nursing Care rather than assuming the status changes automatically.

## EPA programs remain Designated Activities

EPA nurses, care workers, and candidates use program-specific Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**). Eligibility depends on the applicable agreement, candidate or qualified stage, receiving program, and individual designation. It is not a route open to every caregiver and should not be merged with ordinary Nursing Care or SSW guidance.

Use [Which status fits medical or care work in Japan?](/articles/choosing-a-status-for-medical-and-care-work-in-japan) to compare these routes.

## Period and renewal

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. The status is renewable while the qualifying professional activity continues, but no particular period or extension is guaranteed.

Maintain registration, contracts, employment and payment records, duties, facility information, taxes, residence records, and required notifications. A facility change, dispatch change, end of employment, different duties, or move to another program requires fresh review even if the public-facing job title stays the same.

## Side work and additional facilities

Nursing Care is activity-limited. Additional care work, teaching, clinical work, translation, unrelated part-time work, or self-employment is not automatically covered. Check the professional scope, contracts, immigration authorization, outside-activity permission, and notifications before beginning another activity.

## Questions to resolve before applying

1. Is Certified Care Worker registration complete?
2. Which qualification route and registration evidence apply?
3. Are the duties care, care instruction, licensed nursing, or another activity?
4. Who employs the person and where will the work occur?
5. Is this Nursing Care, SSW nursing care, an EPA designation, or a temporary registration bridge?
6. What remuneration, hours, shifts, supervision, and contract conditions apply?
7. Does the April 15, 2026 organization declaration apply to the filing?
8. Which current ISA checklist applies?
