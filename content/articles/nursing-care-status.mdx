> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Nursing Care status is for nursing-care services or instruction performed by a person holding the relevant Japanese certified care-worker qualification. It differs from Medical Services, Specified Skilled Worker care roles, and EPA-related Designated Activities.

## Who should examine it

- Certified care workers with qualifying Japanese employment
- Professionals teaching covered nursing-care practice

## Evidence to plan for

- Japanese certified care-worker qualification
- Employment contract, facility details, and duties
- Professional registration and employment conditions

## Important boundaries

Identify whether the route is Nursing Care, Specified Skilled Worker, or an EPA Designated Activity. Similar workplaces do not mean the immigration categories are interchangeable.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
