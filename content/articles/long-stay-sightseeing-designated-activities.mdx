> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

This named Designated Activities route permits qualifying nationals of visa-exempt countries or regions to stay for sightseeing and recreation for an extended period. It is savings-funded and does not create general work permission. A qualifying spouse may accompany the principal under the program conditions.

## Who should examine it

- Eligible applicants meeting the nationality, age, savings, insurance, and purpose conditions
- Qualifying accompanying spouses applying under the linked route

## Evidence to plan for

- Bank records showing the required level and ownership of savings
- Private medical insurance, itinerary, and accommodation plans
- Marriage records for an accompanying spouse and mission-specific forms

## Important boundaries

Confirm the current savings threshold, currency conversion treatment, insurance coverage period, and whether the spouse applies together or separately. This route is not a substitute for remote-work permission.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
