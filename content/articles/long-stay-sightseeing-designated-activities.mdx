> **Editorial review needed:** This is a purpose-limited, savings-funded route—not a general retirement, residence, or remote-work visa.

## Who can examine this route

Long Stay for sightseeing and recreation is a named Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**) route. The principal applicant must currently:

- be a national or citizen of a country or region covered by Japan's visa-waiver arrangements;
- be at least eighteen;
- intend sightseeing and recreation rather than work; and
- have savings exceeding 30 million yen, owned by the applicant and spouse, together with qualifying private medical travel insurance.

This is not a general retirement category. Age alone, pension income, home ownership, or a willingness to support oneself does not replace the published nationality, savings, insurance, and purpose conditions.

## Savings evidence

MOFA's application guidance asks for evidence such as a bankbook showing a balance exceeding 30 million yen and deposits and withdrawals over the preceding six months. Confirm accepted ownership, account, currency-conversion, and document rules with the Japanese mission or the COE procedure being used.

Borrowed money or a last-minute unexplained transfer can raise questions because the record must show the qualifying savings and ownership. Keep source-of-funds and account-history explanations available.

## Six months with a possible extension

The initial period is six months. An eligible person can apply in Japan before expiry for an extension producing a maximum stay of one year under the scheme. The extension is an application, not an automatic second six months.

Track the Period of Stay ([在留期間](/glossary/zairyu-kikan), **zairyū kikan**) and complete any residence-card and municipal procedures that apply to the granted route. Do not treat a six-month visa or status label as permission to remain for one year without the extension decision.

## Accompanying spouse—and the child boundary

A qualifying spouse can accompany the principal when they share the same residence and travel together in Japan. The spouse does not have to enter on the same day, but cannot enter before the principal under the published rule.

Dependent children cannot accompany through this scheme. If spouses stay separately under the scheme rather than one accompanying the other, their combined qualifying savings must exceed 60 million yen. Each person still needs the correct application and insurance evidence.

## No employment or remote work

This route is for sightseeing and recreation and does not authorize remunerative activity. Do not use it as a substitute for Digital Nomad, employment, freelance, or business permission merely because the work is online or paid abroad.

## Evidence to organize

- passport and current visa-waiver-country or region eligibility;
- proof of age and planned sightseeing or recreation schedule;
- qualifying account ownership, current balance, and six-month transaction history;
- private medical travel insurance covering death, injury, and illness for the stay;
- marriage and shared-travel evidence for an accompanying spouse; and
- a COE or the alternative mission-specific evidence path.

## Plan around the one-year ceiling

Arrange insurance, housing, accounts, travel, and departure around the maximum. A wish to remain, work, study, retire, or join family does not extend this designation. Any later route requires its own eligibility and application.
