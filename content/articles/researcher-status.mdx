> **Editorial review needed:** This guide explains the national route and current published criteria. A research title alone does not decide between Researcher, Professor, Engineer/Specialist, or a designated program.

## Start with the host and principal activity

Researcher ([研究](/glossary/kenkyu), **kenkyū**) covers contracted research work at a Japanese public or private organization, excluding activity covered by Professor. Official examples include researchers at government-related organizations and private companies.

Common roles to examine include research scientist, laboratory researcher, research fellow, government or institute researcher, corporate or industrial researcher, R&D scientist, materials scientist, and researchers in scientific, technical, humanities, or social-science fields.

The title is not enough. A university researcher performing research, research guidance, or education may point first to Professor. Product design, engineering, data analysis, or development whose principal activity is specialized technical work may require comparison with Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services. Clinical work may also depend on a Japanese professional license and Medical status. Some government, fellowship, or nationally designated programs use other routes.

## Ordinary qualification routes

Under the published landing criteria, an ordinary applicant must fit one of the research-background routes and receive remuneration at least equivalent to a Japanese person performing comparable work.

- After qualifying higher education, hold a master's degree or have at least three years of research experience in the proposed field. Graduate-school research time can count toward the research-experience period.
- Alternatively, have at least ten years of research experience in the proposed field. University research time can count.

The official criteria include exceptions for research at specified public or publicly funded organizations. Do not assume an organization qualifies from its English name; identify its legal category and use the current official checklist.

## Transfer-related research route

The Researcher criteria also address a person transferred for a defined period from a qualifying overseas office to a Japanese office to conduct research. The published exception examines at least one continuous year of relevant research immediately before the transfer, with certain prior Researcher-status time in Japan capable of being combined. Corporate relationship, offices, prior work, transfer order, duties, period, and remuneration all need evidence.

This is not the same as Intra-company Transferee, whose permitted duties are the Engineer/Specialist activity field rather than research assigned to Researcher.

## Build an evidence record

Prepare a consistent account of:

- the research question, field, methods, outputs, schedule, and work locations;
- why the work is research rather than mainly product development, teaching, clinical practice, or routine testing;
- the host organization's legal identity and research activity;
- the contract, position, period, working conditions, and remuneration;
- degrees, transcripts, research history, publications, projects, or experience certificates; and
- transfer and corporate-relationship records where using the transfer-related exception.

Current checklists vary by application and organization category. ISA announced additional organization-representative information for some Researcher applications filed from April 15, 2026. Use the checklist for the application being filed rather than copying an older employer's packet.

## Period, renewal, and changes

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. The individual period granted is discretionary, and an extension is not automatic.

Renewal can examine whether the research continues, the current host and contract, remuneration, organization stability, notifications, taxes, and residence history. A move to a university, product-development role, clinical position, unrelated employer, or materially different activity needs a fresh status comparison. Filing an organization notification does not prove that the new work is authorized.

Before accepting teaching, consulting, writing, or other paid side work, compare it with the authorized research activity and check whether outside-activity permission or a change of status is needed.
