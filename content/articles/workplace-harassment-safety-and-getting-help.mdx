> **Editorial review needed:** Harassment, discrimination, safety, crime, civil claims, and labor enforcement use different legal tests and help routes. This guide provides orientation, not a verdict about an individual incident.

## Address immediate danger first

If there is immediate physical danger, serious injury, violence, confinement, threats, or another emergency, move to safety when possible and contact the appropriate emergency service. Workplace reporting can follow; it should not delay urgent medical or police help.

For a work or commuting injury, tell the medical provider it may be occupational and preserve the facts for Industrial Accident Compensation Insurance. [Use the work-injury and insurance guide](/articles/employment-insurance-social-insurance-and-work-injury) rather than treating every injury as ordinary health insurance.

## Identify the problem without forcing one label

Workplace Power Harassment ([パワーハラスメント](/glossary/pawahara), **pawā harasumento**) has a defined three-part framework involving a power relationship, conduct beyond what is necessary and reasonable for work, and harm to the working environment. Appropriate instruction is not harassment merely because it is unwelcome, while severe conduct can matter even when it happens once.

Sexual Harassment ([セクシュアルハラスメント](/glossary/sekuhara), **sekushuaru harasumento**) and harassment concerning pregnancy, childbirth, or childcare and family-care leave—often called Maternity Harassment ([マタニティハラスメント](/glossary/matahara), **mataniti harasumento**)—use related but distinct rules. Nationality-related treatment, unpaid wages, unsafe instructions, excessive hours, assault, stalking, discrimination, and retaliation can require other legal routes even when they occur alongside harassment.

Foreign nationality or residence status does not make workplace harassment acceptable. Employers must establish measures for covered workplace harassment and cannot disadvantage a worker merely for consulting or cooperating with the employer's response under the relevant rules.

## Preserve useful evidence safely

Create a private chronology with dates, times, locations, people, exact words or conduct, witnesses, work consequences, reports, and responses. Preserve relevant messages, emails, schedules, evaluations, pay records, medical records, photographs, and policies outside employer-controlled systems.

Do not take unrelated personal or confidential business data. Recording rules, privacy, confidentiality, and evidence use can be fact-specific; ask qualified help before publishing accusations or covert material. If language is a barrier, retain the original Japanese and prepare a clearly labeled translation.

## Choose the route that matches the problem

Possible routes include:

- a supervisor outside the reporting line, HR, compliance, occupational health, or the employer's harassment contact;
- a labor union, including a union that accepts individual workers;
- a Prefectural Labour Bureau ([労働局](/glossary/rodo-kyoku), **rōdō kyoku**) or Comprehensive Labour Consultation Corner ([総合労働相談コーナー](/glossary/sogo-rodo-sodan-corner), **sōgō rōdō sōdan kōnā**) for workplace consultation and dispute-resolution orientation;
- a Labour Standards Inspection Office ([労働基準監督署](/glossary/rodo-kijun-kantokusho), **rōdō kijun kantokusho**) for matters within labor-standards and workers’ compensation authority;
- Hello Work for employment-insurance and job-search processes;
- a bar association, attorney, legal-support service, or court procedure for individualized legal remedies; and
- police, medical, mental-health, or domestic-violence services where the facts require them.

No single office handles every issue. The MHLW publishes foreign-language consultation services and an after-hours Labour Standards Advice Hotline; languages and operating times can change, so check the current official page before calling.

## Immigration threats need separate verification

An employer does not personally cancel a residence status. Employment ending or inactivity can create notifications and later immigration consequences, but those are decided under immigration law. Do not assume a threat such as “complain and your visa disappears” is an authoritative explanation.

Preserve the threat, verify the current residence permission and deadlines with ISA or qualified immigration counsel, and address the workplace issue through the appropriate labor or safety route. Avoid starting a new job until its duties are authorized.

## Before making a report or leaving

1. Protect immediate health and safety.
2. Preserve a focused chronology and original records.
3. Identify the outcome needed: danger stopped, wages paid, schedule corrected, accommodation, investigation, injury claim, separation records, or legal remedy.
4. Use the internal route when safe and appropriate, but do not assume it is the only route.
5. Contact the authority or adviser whose jurisdiction matches the issue.
6. Calendar employment, immigration, insurance, tax, and claim deadlines separately.
