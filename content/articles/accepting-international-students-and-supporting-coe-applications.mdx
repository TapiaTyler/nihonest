> **For education providers:** This is an operational orientation, not a substitute for the current ISA or MEXT instructions or advice from a qualified professional.

## Be precise about the school's role

The Immigration Services Agency issues a Certificate of Eligibility ([在留資格認定証明書](/glossary/certificate-of-eligibility), **zairyū shikaku nintei shōmeisho**). A school does not issue, grant, sponsor, or guarantee it. The student is the subject of the application; a qualifying school employee or another eligible person may act as a representative and the school supplies institutional evidence.

Visa issuance by a diplomatic mission and landing permission are later, separate decisions. Explain those boundaries in admissions material and staff scripts so “we help with the COE” is not heard as “we guarantee entry.”

## Before admitting a student

Create a documented process to:

1. Verify that the institution and exact course can accept the student under the proposed immigration route.
2. Publish accurate course, language, attendance, tuition, refund, housing, and support conditions.
3. Assess prior education, study purpose, ability to complete the course, language ability, and the funding plan under the current rules.
4. Reconcile names, dates, school history, employment history, expense support, and translations across the application.
5. Investigate inconsistencies instead of coaching the applicant to hide or invent facts.
6. Identify the applicant, expense supporter, school contact, translator, and authorized representative as different roles.
7. Collect only necessary personal records and define secure access, retention, return, and destruction practices.

Admission is the school's decision. Whether the proposed study supports Student status ([留学](/glossary/ryugaku), **ryūgaku**) is an immigration decision. Preserve both records without presenting one as the other.

## Support the COE application

Use the current ISA page for the institution type, application context, and institutional classification. Give the applicant a dated checklist that distinguishes:

- records the applicant prepares;
- records the expense supporter prepares;
- records the school prepares;
- originals, translations, and issue-date limits;
- the school submission deadline and the government process; and
- how changed facts or additional-document requests will be handled.

Many schools act as representative in Student COE cases, but staff authority and online-system eligibility have conditions. Do not assume any employee or outside agent may submit or charge for immigration representation. Escalate uncertainty to ISA, an attorney, or an administrative scrivener with the appropriate scope of practice.

Do not submit an application the institution knows is false or internally inconsistent. A clean document packet cannot cure a course, institution, funding plan, or study purpose that does not meet the governing requirements.

## Apply current Japanese-language-school rules

ISA changed the evidence treatment for affected Japanese-language institution cases in 2026. For COE applications involving entrants scheduled from October 2026, and change or extension applications filed from July 1, 2026, a history of 150 Japanese-study hours alone no longer serves as the language proof. The announced process requires the specified examination certificate or interview confirmation and institution-prepared confirmation records, subject to stated exceptions.

Treat this as effective-dated guidance. Use the current notice and forms for each application, record how the assessment occurred, and do not copy an older intake's checklist forward without review.

Japanese-language institutions are also moving into the MEXT certification framework. Existing qualifying institutions have a transition period through March 31, 2029. Confirm the institution's present status and any reporting framework that still applies rather than relying on an old list or promotional claim.

## Prepare for entry and enrollment

Give the student one named contact and clear instructions for:

- visa and travel updates after COE issuance;
- delayed entry, changed course, changed expense supporter, or inability to enroll;
- arrival time, accommodation, transport, emergency contact, and orientation;
- records to carry rather than place in checked baggage;
- enrollment, student ID, course registration, placement, tuition, and scholarship steps; and
- safe submission of the residence card ([在留カード](/glossary/zairyu-card), **zairyū kādo**) or municipal evidence when legitimately required.

At arrival, check that the student's granted status and period, school, and work notation appear consistent. Staff should not alter government records or promise that an error is harmless. Direct discrepancies to the responsible authority promptly.

## Support study without becoming the decision-maker

Orientation should explain attendance recording, absence reporting, academic progress, vacations, wellbeing and disability support, harassment reporting, disasters, emergencies, and who handles immigration questions. State the difference between a school rule, a legal requirement, and practical advice.

Student status does not itself authorize paid work. Permission to Engage in Activity Outside the Status Scope ([資格外活動許可](/glossary/shikakugai-katsudo-kyoka), **shikakugai katsudō kyoka**) is a separate immigration permission. Schools should explain the ordinary limits, prohibited business categories, combined-hours risk, and study priority, then refer unusual internships, freelance work, or individual activities for authoritative review.

## Keep resident and institution actions separate

For education providers accepting Student-status mid- to long-term residents, ISA requests institutional notifications when acceptance starts or ends and reports of the acceptance position as of May 1 and November 1, generally within fourteen days. Japanese-language institutions can have additional attendance, withdrawal, completion, and outcome reporting under their applicable framework.

The student's own address, school-change, status-maintenance, extension, work-permission, re-entry, and departure actions remain separate. Filing an institutional report neither completes the student's notification nor changes or extends the student's status.

Create an escalation checklist for transfer, leave, withdrawal, expulsion, graduation, prolonged absence, disappearance, loss of contact, and institution closure or recognition changes. Record the known facts, contact the student where appropriate, protect sensitive information, meet the institution's applicable reporting schedule, and direct the student to the authority responsible for their personal next step.

> **Operational principle:** The school provides accurate education, evidence, records, support, and required reporting. ISA decides the immigration application; the diplomatic mission decides the visa; and the immigration inspector decides landing.
