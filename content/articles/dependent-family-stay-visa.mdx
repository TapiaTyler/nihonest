## Who this route is for

Dependent, commonly described as Family Stay (家族滞在, **kazoku taizai**), covers ordinary daily activities of a qualifying spouse or child supported by a foreign resident who holds one of the eligible statuses. It is not a general route for parents, adult relatives, unmarried partners, or every family member. Eligibility depends on the sponsor’s status, the family relationship, actual support, and the applicant’s circumstances.

Check the sponsor’s exact status of residence before planning. Some statuses can support Dependent applications and others cannot. A family member may instead need a different status based on their own work, study, or personal circumstances.

## Official requirements at a glance

- The applicant must be a qualifying spouse or child who is actually supported by the principal resident.
- The principal resident must hold an eligible status. The official list includes specified professional and work statuses, Cultural Activities, and Student; it does not include every status of residence.
- The application must establish the family relationship and the sponsor’s ability and intention to support the dependent.
- A sponsor or another eligible proxy in Japan commonly applies for the COE.
- MOFA lists a passport, application form, photograph, and COE as the core visa-stage documents, with additions depending on nationality and the responsible mission.
- The period of stay is individually designated and cannot exceed **five years**.

Marriage or parentage documents issued outside Japan may need a Japanese translation for immigration use. Confirm document age, authentication, translation, and original/copy rules with the current ISA and mission checklists.

## Evidence and application

A sponsor or proxy in Japan commonly applies for a COE. Expect the immigration application to address the genuine family relationship, the sponsor’s residence and activity, and the ability to support the household. The embassy or consulate’s visa stage generally requires a passport, form, photograph, and COE, with additional documents depending on nationality and location.

> **Official distinction:** The visa permits travel to request entry. Landing permission establishes the status and period of stay. Neither the sponsor’s visa length nor a family relationship guarantees a particular result.

## Work is not automatic

Dependent status does not generally authorize employment. Before paid work or an income-producing activity, obtain the applicable Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted and follow its conditions. Do not assume freelance, remote, or cash work is exempt.

After arrival, complete address, insurance, pension where applicable, school, and other municipal procedures for each family member. Track the dependent’s period of stay separately from the sponsor’s. Report material changes and seek advice if support ends, the relationship changes, the sponsor changes status, or the dependent plans full-time study or work.
