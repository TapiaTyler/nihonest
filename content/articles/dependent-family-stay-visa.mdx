## Who Dependent covers

Dependent, or Family Stay ([家族滞在](/glossary/kazoku-taizai), **kazoku taizai**), authorizes ordinary daily activities as the supported spouse or child of a foreign resident with an eligible status. It is not a general route for parents, siblings, unmarried partners, or every adult relative.

## Check the sponsor's exact status

The current list includes Professor, Artist, Religious Activities, Journalist, Highly Skilled Professional, Business Manager, Legal/Accounting Services, Medical Services, Researcher, Instructor, Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, Intra-company Transferee, Nursing Care, Entertainer, Skilled Labor, Specified Skilled Worker (ii), Cultural Activities, and Student.

The list is closed. Specified Skilled Worker (i) does not ordinarily support Dependent accompaniment. Highly Skilled Professional and some Designated Activities programs have separate family provisions.

## Evidence and application

A Certificate of Eligibility ([在留資格認定証明書](/glossary/certificate-of-eligibility), **zairyū shikaku nintei shōmeisho**) is commonly obtained before the overseas visa application. Prepare current evidence of the relationship, sponsor's status and activity, income or scholarship, support, and household. Explain separation, delayed arrival, or inconsistent records. Foreign-language documents generally need Japanese translations.

A COE supports later review but does not guarantee a visa, landing, or a particular period.

## Period and renewal

The period is individually designated up to **five years** and does not automatically match the sponsor's period. Renewal can be requested while the eligible sponsor status, relationship, support, and residence circumstances continue. Track both expiry dates.

## Work requires separate permission

Dependent does not authorize paid work by default. Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted ([資格外活動許可](/glossary/shikakugai-katsudo-kyoka), **shikakugai katsudō kyoka**) may allow blanket part-time work up to **twenty-eight hours per week**, subject to its conditions and prohibited-business rules.

Multiple jobs share the limit. Freelance, remote, platform, and cash work are not automatically exempt. Qualifying full-time work generally requires an independent work status.

## Family and sponsor changes

A holder whose authorized activity is as a spouse must notify ISA within **fourteen days** after divorce or bereavement. Seek advice promptly when support or cohabitation ends, the sponsor leaves Japan or changes status, or a child becomes independent. Do not delay safety measures because of immigration appearances.

## Questions to resolve

1. Is the sponsor's exact status currently eligible?
2. Is the applicant a legally recognized spouse or qualifying child?
3. How will continuing support be documented?
4. Does the family member plan study, part-time work, or full-time work?
5. Are both expiry dates covered?
