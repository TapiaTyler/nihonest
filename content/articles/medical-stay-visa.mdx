> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

The Visa for Medical Stay supports foreign patients visiting Japan for treatment, medical examinations, or related services, with an accompanying person where needed. The visa form and status used can depend on the planned duration and treatment arrangement. A registered guarantor or coordinating organization is typically central to the process.

## Who should examine it

- Patients with an accepted treatment plan from a Japanese medical institution
- Accompanying persons whose presence is medically or practically necessary

## Evidence to plan for

- Medical institution acceptance and treatment plan
- Guarantor records, schedule, costs, and ability to pay
- Passport, visa forms, and accompanying-person explanation

## Important boundaries

Confirm whether the treatment duration requires a short-stay or longer Designated Activities arrangement and whether a multiple-entry request needs an additional physician's plan. This visa does not itself resolve payment or insurance coverage.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
