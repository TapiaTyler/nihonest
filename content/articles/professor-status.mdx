> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Professor status covers qualifying research, research guidance, or education at a university, equivalent institution, or college of technology. It is institution- and activity-specific; the word professor in an employment title does not by itself settle the category.

## Who should examine it

- University faculty and qualifying academic researchers
- People teaching or supervising research at covered higher-education institutions

## Evidence to plan for

- Appointment or employment terms and a detailed activity description
- Institution records and the department or program
- Academic qualifications, career history, and remuneration

## Important boundaries

Distinguish Professor from Researcher and Instructor by checking the institution and actual duties. Confirm how separately paid consulting, lectures, or work for another organization would be treated.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
