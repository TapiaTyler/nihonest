> **Editorial review needed:** Professor is defined by the institution and activity, not by academic rank.

## What Professor covers

Professor ([教授](/glossary/kyoju), **kyōju**) covers research, research guidance, or education at a Japanese university, an equivalent institution, or a college of technology. Common roles to examine include professor, associate or assistant professor, lecturer, visiting faculty, university language instructor, academic or postdoctoral researcher, and research supervisor.

The appointment title is evidence, not the test. A lecturer at a private language company may instead need Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services, while a researcher at a corporation or government institute may point to Researcher.

## English teaching at a university

An English teacher at a qualifying university should examine Professor first. This differs from Instructor at covered schools and private-company language instruction. Use [Which residence status fits teaching in Japan?](/articles/choosing-a-residence-status-for-teaching-in-japan) for the comparison.

## Full-time, part-time, and multiple appointments

Professor is not limited to tenured or full-time faculty. Current extension materials distinguish full-time and part-time cases and may require additional activity, position, remuneration, income, and tax evidence for part-time appointments.

For several universities or paid activities, document each appointment and identify the principal basis of residence. ISA specifically uses a university Professor who also teaches language for a private company as an example requiring an outside-activity analysis.

## Evidence, period, and renewal

Use the current application checklist. Common evidence covers the institution and department, appointment terms, teaching and research duties, period, position, schedule, remuneration, academic or research history, other appointments, and applicable organization and tax records.

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. The period actually granted is an individual decision. Extension is possible but discretionary. Changes from full-time to part-time work, several short contracts, lower or irregular income, institution changes, notifications, taxes, and other residence facts can require additional explanation.

A move to covered school teaching can point to Instructor; private-company language teaching can point to Engineer/Specialist; and corporate or government research can point to Researcher. Consulting, private-company teaching, or freelance work may require Permission to Engage in Activity Other Than That Permitted ([資格外活動許可](/glossary/shikakugai-katsudo-kyoka), **shikakugai katsudō kyoka**) or a status change.
