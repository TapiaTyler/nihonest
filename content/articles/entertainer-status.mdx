## What this route covers

Entertainer status covers specified performance, show-business, modeling, and professional-sports activities. Requirements differ by activity and venue, and immigration may examine the performer, contracting organization, facility, schedule, compensation, and past compliance.

## Who should examine it

- Musicians, actors, singers, dancers, models, and professional athletes in covered engagements
- Production companies and promoters arranging qualifying appearances

## Evidence to plan for

- Contracts, itinerary, venues, roles, and remuneration
- Professional history and evidence of prior entertainment work
- Promoter, production, and facility records required for the activity type

## Important boundaries

A creative occupation may instead fit Artist, while ordinary employment surrounding an event may fit neither. MOFA lists additional contract, résumé, and show-business-history evidence in some applications.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
