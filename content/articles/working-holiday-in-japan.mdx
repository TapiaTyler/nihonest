## Holiday first, employment second

Japan's Working Holiday programs let eligible young people spend an extended holiday in Japan while undertaking employment incidentally to supplement travel funds. The Designated Activities ([特定活動](/glossary/tokutei-katsudo), **tokutei katsudō**) designation is not an ordinary labor-migration route.

As of April 1, 2026, MOFA lists thirty-two partners: Australia, New Zealand, Canada, South Korea, France, Germany, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Denmark, Taiwan, Hong Kong, Norway, Portugal, Poland, Slovakia, Austria, Hungary, Spain, Argentina, Chile, Iceland, the Czech Republic, Lithuania, Sweden, Estonia, the Netherlands, Uruguay, Finland, Latvia, Luxembourg, Malta, and Italy.

The list, quotas, and rules can change. Being a citizen of a country that sends tourists visa-free does not itself create Working Holiday eligibility.

## General conditions—and country-specific exceptions

MOFA's general outline asks whether the applicant:

- has the required nationality, regional passport, and—where applicable—current residence in that country or region;
- primarily intends a holiday for the permitted period;
- falls within the applicable age range when applying;
- is not accompanied by dependants or children;
- holds a valid passport and return ticket or funds to buy one;
- has reasonable initial maintenance funds and is in good health; and
- has not previously participated, unless a current bilateral exception permits another participation.

The general age description is eighteen through thirty, but MOFA identifies narrower default wording for Australia, Canada, South Korea, and Ireland unless the competent authorities allow extension to thirty. Use the responsible mission's rule, not another nationality's checklist.

## Repeat participation changed for some partners

Current MOFA material permits two lifetime participations for specified partners, with different structures and effective dates:

- from December 1, 2024: Canada and the United Kingdom can use two consecutive years or two non-consecutive one-year stays; New Zealand, Denmark, and Austria can use two non-consecutive one-year stays;
- from January 1, 2025: Germany, Ireland, and Slovakia can use two non-consecutive one-year stays;
- from October 1, 2025: South Korea can use two non-consecutive one-year stays; and
- from February 1, 2026: Taiwan can use two non-consecutive one-year stays.

Do not infer a second participation for a partner absent from the current exception or assume the Canadian and British consecutive format applies to everyone.

## Apply through the responsible mission

Applications are made through the Japanese embassy or consulate responsible for the applicant's country or region, or the applicable Japan–Taiwan Exchange Association office. The mission controls the operative document list, location rule, funds, insurance, itinerary, quota, and local procedure.

No private association is authorized by MOFA to operate Japan's Working Holiday program on its behalf. Treat promises of guaranteed visas or special access cautiously.

## Work boundaries

Employment is allowed only as an incidental way to supplement holiday funds and within the individual designation. Work at bars, cabarets, nightclubs, gambling establishments, and other premises affecting public morals is strictly prohibited. A job should not replace the holiday as the principal purpose.

Employment can create Japanese tax, labor, payroll, and insurance questions even though it is incidental for immigration purposes.

## Register after establishing a place to live

MOFA directs participants to visit the municipality and report their address within fourteen days after finding a place to settle. Confirm residence-card handling at entry, then complete Resident Registration ([住民登録](/glossary/jumin-toroku), **jūmin tōroku**) and the municipal procedures that apply.

Housing, phone, banking, health insurance, pension, and other onboarding rules depend on the person's records, work, age, and coverage. Do not assume the Working Holiday label removes ordinary resident obligations.

## Prepare for the end date

Record the granted period, any country-specific participation structure, employment records, tax documents, and departure tasks. A continuing job or relationship does not automatically extend Working Holiday; a different basis requires its own status and timely application.
