> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Specified Skilled Worker authorizes qualifying employment in designated industrial fields. Category (i) and category (ii) differ in skill level, available fields, support framework, length and renewal structure, and family accompaniment. The worker, employer, contract, field, tests or exempting pathway, and support arrangements must all fit the program.

## Who should examine it

- Workers who pass the applicable skills and Japanese tests or qualify through an accepted transition
- Employers approved to receive workers in a designated field

## Evidence to plan for

- Skills and Japanese-language test results or transition evidence
- Employment contract, compensation comparison, field, and workplace details
- Category (i) support plan and registered support organization records where used

## Important boundaries

Check the currently designated fields and whether the occupation belongs to category (i), category (ii), or neither. Do not assume Technical Intern Training automatically converts or that rules from one field apply to another.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
