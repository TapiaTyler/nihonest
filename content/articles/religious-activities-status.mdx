> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Religious Activities status generally concerns religious activity carried out by a worker dispatched by a foreign religious organization. The sending relationship, Japanese receiving organization, duties, support, and duration should be documented clearly.

## Who should examine it

- Missionaries and other religious workers sent by an overseas religious body
- Religious personnel with defined duties at a Japanese host

## Evidence to plan for

- Dispatch letter and records for the sending organization
- Host details, duties, locations, duration, and financial support
- The applicant's religious role, training, and history

## Important boundaries

Separate religious duties from unrelated paid work, teaching, or general volunteer activity. Confirm whether any secondary activity requires separate permission.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
