> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Instructor status covers language education and other education at qualifying elementary, secondary, vocational, and comparable institutions. University teaching generally points to Professor; some private language-school or company instruction may point to Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services.

## Who should examine it

- Teachers employed by covered primary or secondary schools
- Language instructors whose institution falls within the Instructor category

## Evidence to plan for

- School type, employment contract, duties, and timetable
- Degree, teaching credentials, and relevant experience
- Employer records and remuneration

## Important boundaries

The school type matters as much as the subject taught. Confirm the legal category of the institution and how work at multiple locations is structured.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
