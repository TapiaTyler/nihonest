> **Editorial review needed:** The validity and timing of an employment ending depend on the contract, workplace rules, conduct, communications, and applicable law. Get individualized advice before accepting a disputed classification or deadline.

## First identify what happened

“Leaving a company” can describe several different events:

- a worker chooses to resign;
- both sides agree to end the contract;
- the employer dismisses the worker;
- a fixed term expires and is not renewed;
- the business closes or becomes insolvent; or
- the parties dispute whether the worker resigned or was dismissed.

Do not sign a resignation letter, settlement, admission, blank form, or Japanese document you do not understand merely because you are told it is administrative. Save the proposal and ask whether it changes pay, notice, unemployment records, references, claims, confidentiality, immigration reporting, or the stated reason for separation.

## Voluntary and agreed separation are different

Resignation or separation ([退職](/glossary/taishoku), **taishoku**) is often used broadly. A worker's unilateral resignation and a mutually agreed termination do not necessarily use the same timing or consequences.

For an indefinite contract, Civil Code and workplace-rule questions can affect the effective date. A fixed-term worker generally commits through the term, subject to legal exceptions and the specific facts. The familiar statement that everyone can always quit with two weeks' notice is therefore too broad for every case. Review the contract, rules of employment, collective agreement, remaining term, reason, and current official guidance.

Give important communications in a durable form, state what you are and are not agreeing to, and retain proof of delivery. If there is coercion, unpaid wages, danger, harassment, illness, family emergency, or a disputed fixed term, seek advice rather than disappearing or signing immediately.

## Dismissal requires more than a notice calculation

Dismissal ([解雇](/glossary/kaiko), **kaiko**) initiated by the employer can be invalid when it lacks objectively reasonable grounds and is not considered socially appropriate. Dismissing a fixed-term worker before the term ends generally requires unavoidable circumstances, a stricter question than ordinary expiration.

Separately, an employer ordinarily must provide at least thirty days' advance notice or pay average wages for the missing notice days. Defined exceptions and exclusions exist. Notice or payment is a procedural requirement; it does **not** by itself prove that the reason for dismissal is valid.

Ask in writing whether the employer is dismissing you, the effective date, reason, notice calculation, work and pay arrangements during notice, and treatment of unused leave. A worker given dismissal notice can request a certificate stating the dismissal reason.

## Fixed-term nonrenewal is not always automatic

Nonrenewal ([雇止め](/glossary/yatoi-dome), **yatoi-dome**) occurs when an employer does not renew a fixed-term contract. Start with the stated term, renewal criteria and limits, renewal history, employer statements, actual practice, and whether the worker requested renewal.

Where contracts have been repeatedly renewed or there are facts supporting a reasonable expectation of renewal, refusal can be restricted when it lacks objectively reasonable grounds and social appropriateness. Separate advance-notice standards can apply to defined repeatedly renewed or longer-running arrangements. Do not assume either that every expiry guarantees renewal or that the printed end date resolves every case.

## Indefinite conversion requires a worker's application

Indefinite conversion ([無期転換](/glossary/muki-tenkan), **muki tenkan**) can become available when fixed-term contracts with the same employer are renewed beyond five years. The worker must exercise the right during an eligible contract; conversion is not simply automatic on the fifth anniversary. The indefinite contract ordinarily begins after the current fixed term ends.

Breaks between contracts, statutory exceptions, research or project exceptions, retirement-age rules, and which entity is the same employer can affect the calculation. Indefinite duration also does not automatically copy the title, duties, salary, or benefits of another employee category. Check the disclosed post-conversion conditions.

## Request and preserve the closing records

Depending on the situation, request:

- a Certificate on Retirement ([退職証明書](/glossary/taishoku-shomeisho), **taishoku shōmeisho**) limited to the items you request;
- written dismissal reasons, if applicable;
- the employment-insurance Separation Notice ([離職票](/glossary/rishokuhyo), **rishokuhyō**) when required for the unemployment process;
- the Salary Income Withholding Tax Slip ([給与所得の源泉徴収票](/glossary/kyuyo-shotoku-no-gensen-choshu-hyo), **kyūyo shotoku no gensen chōshūhyō**);
- final payslips, time and leave records, expense reimbursement, and bonus or retirement-pay explanations;
- health-insurance, pension, and employment-insurance loss-of-coverage records;
- return receipts for equipment and proof that your own property and documents were returned; and
- a personal copy of work products only when law, confidentiality, privacy, and intellectual-property rules allow it.

When requested after employment ends, unpaid wages and other money or property owed to the worker are subject to a seven-day rule under the Labor Standards Act, but disputes over whether an amount is owed can require resolution. Make the request clearly and retain it.

## Employment ending does not instantly answer immigration

Job loss does not by itself rewrite the residence card at the moment employment ends. However, a covered affiliated-organization or contract notification may be due within fourteen days, and prolonged failure to perform the authorized principal activity can create immigration risk subject to the statute and individual circumstances.

[Read the immigration notification guide](/articles/notifying-immigration-about-work-contract-changes) and compare the next job's actual duties with the current status before starting. Extension, change, unemployment, job-search, and departure decisions use separate procedures.

## Complete the wider transition

1. Identify the type, reason, and effective date of ending.
2. Preserve documents and obtain qualified help before signing a disputed agreement.
3. Reconcile final wages, leave, expenses, property, and certificates.
4. Confirm employee health insurance, pension, employment insurance, and any municipal replacement coverage.
5. Complete personal and employer immigration notifications separately.
6. Keep the withholding slip for a new employer, year-end adjustment, or tax return.
7. Update housing, banking, guarantor, school, family, and departure plans when income or residence changes.
