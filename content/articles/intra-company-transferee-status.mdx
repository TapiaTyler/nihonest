> **Editorial review needed:** This guide describes a transfer-specific route. A multinational employer, foreign payroll, or internal job title does not by itself establish eligibility.

## What makes this a transfer route

Intra-company Transferee ([企業内転勤](/glossary/kigyonai-tenkin), **kigyōnai tenkin**) covers an employee of an overseas office of an organization that has a head office, branch, or other office in Japan who transfers for a defined period to the Japanese office and performs work within the Engineer/Specialist in Humanities/International Services activity field.

This can include transferred software or systems engineers, technical consultants, analysts, designers, marketing or planning professionals, translators, and other qualifying technical, humanities, or international-services workers. The occupation must still fit that professional activity field.

## The main conditions

- **Related offices:** establish the legally qualifying relationship between the overseas and Japanese entities and the existence of the relevant offices.
- **Immediately preceding service:** the employee generally must have performed covered Engineer/Specialist-type duties continuously for at least one year at the overseas office immediately before the transfer. Certain prior time in Japan under Intra-company Transferee can be combined under the published rule.
- **Defined transfer:** document the transfer period, Japanese office, position, and assignment. An indefinite move is not made temporary by calling it an assignment.
- **Covered work:** the principal duties in Japan must fit the technical, humanities, or international-services activity field.
- **Remuneration:** pay must be at least equivalent to that received by a Japanese worker performing comparable work.

Unlike the ordinary Engineer/Specialist qualification routes, Intra-company Transferee is built around qualifying prior internal service and a genuine transfer. It should not be described as simply avoiding a degree requirement; every transfer-specific fact still needs proof.

## Situations needing another comparison

- A new external hire by a multinational company is not an intra-company transfer.
- An employee without the qualifying immediately preceding overseas service may need to examine Engineer/Specialist if its separate criteria can be met.
- A move between unrelated companies does not preserve this status merely because both belong to the same industry.
- Work mainly for an unrelated client, at an unestablished location, or outside the covered professional field needs careful review.
- Research assigned to Researcher, teaching assigned to Professor or Instructor, business management, regulated practice, entertainment, and other separately listed activities do not become covered through a corporate transfer.

ISA's published Q&A distinguishes this route by the limited transfer period and work at the particular transferred-to office. It also explains that some workers who miss the one-year transfer criterion may instead qualify for Engineer/Specialist if they independently meet that route's criteria.

## Evidence to organize

Prepare corporate registrations, ownership or relationship records, office evidence, an employment-history certificate showing dates and duties, the transfer order, Japan assignment description, worksite, period, organization records, and remuneration evidence. The foreign and Japanese records should use consistent entity names, dates, reporting lines, and duties.

Identify who remains the contractual employer and who directs and pays for the work. A same-company branch transfer may not require an entirely new employment contract, but the continuing contract and Japanese-office relationship still need to be established.

## Period, renewal, and the end of an assignment

Available periods are **five years, three years, one year, or three months**. These are possible grants, not a promised assignment length.

An extension can examine whether the defined intra-group assignment, qualifying office, duties, remuneration, and compliance continue. A permanent local transfer, move to an unrelated employer, change of office, or material duty change may require Engineer/Specialist or another status. Notify required organization changes separately; a notification does not authorize a changed assignment.

Before taking consulting, freelance, teaching, or other side work, compare it with the current authorized activity and the transfer arrangement. Outside-activity permission, employment approval, tax, and notification questions are separate.
