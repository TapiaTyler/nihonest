> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Intra-company Transferee status supports a qualifying transfer within the same corporate group to perform covered technical, humanities, or international-services duties in Japan. Corporate affiliation, prior overseas employment, transfer duration, duties, and remuneration are central.

## Who should examine it

- Employees moving from an overseas office to a Japanese branch or qualifying affiliate
- International assignments that retain a documented intra-group relationship

## Evidence to plan for

- Corporate relationship charts and registration records
- Overseas employment history and transfer order
- Japan duties, period, location, and remuneration

## Important boundaries

Confirm the entities have the required relationship and the work itself is covered. A new external hire is not an intra-company transfer merely because the employer is multinational.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
