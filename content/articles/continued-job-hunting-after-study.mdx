> **Draft coverage:** This article maps the route and the questions to verify. It is not yet a review-ready eligibility or document checklist.

## What this route covers

Some graduates of eligible Japanese institutions may change from Student to Designated Activities to continue job hunting after graduation. Institution type, completion, recommendation, prior job-search activity, financial support, and ongoing reporting can matter. Separate arrangements exist for people waiting to start an already secured job.

## Who should examine it

- Eligible graduates who began job hunting while enrolled and need time to continue
- Graduates with an offer who need an appropriate status until the start date

## Evidence to plan for

- Graduation records and school recommendation
- Job-search history, applications, interviews, and activity plan
- Funds, residence records, and offer documents where applicable

## Important boundaries

Apply before Student status expires and distinguish continued job hunting from waiting for employment or a broader J-Find route. Work permission is not automatic.

## Before relying on this draft

Open the official sources linked below and use the current application page for the exact procedure you will file: Certificate of Eligibility, overseas visa, change of status, or extension. Requirements can differ by nationality, employer or host category, family circumstances, and the Japanese mission handling the application. Keep the planned activity consistent across contracts, forms, supporting records, and explanations.
