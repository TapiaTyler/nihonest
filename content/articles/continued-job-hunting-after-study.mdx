## This route continues an existing search

Continued Job Hunting ([継続就職活動](/glossary/keizoku-shushoku-katsudo), **keizoku shūshoku katsudō**) is a Designated Activities route for defined graduates who started job hunting before graduation and want to continue after completing a qualifying Japanese program. It is not an automatic extension of Student ([留学](/glossary/ryugaku), **ryūgaku**) status and is different from J-Find or permission to wait after securing a job.

Apply for Change of Status of Residence ([在留資格変更許可](/glossary/zairyu-shikaku-henko-kyoka), **zairyū shikaku henkō kyoka**) before the current period expires. Graduating does not allow a person to keep using a Student outside-activity permission as though they remained enrolled.

## Who can examine the ordinary route

Current ISA guidance identifies several categories:

- graduates of a Japanese university, junior college, graduate school, or college of technology who held Student status, excluding categories such as non-degree, auditing, subject-only, and research students;
- graduates of a qualifying advanced course at a specialized training college when the acquired learning relates to activity under a prospective work status;
- graduates of a specialized course who obtained the title of diploma and whose course content relates to prospective work-status activity; and
- graduates of certain qualifying Japanese-language institutions who previously graduated from an overseas university or graduate school and meet the additional institutional conditions.

The person must normally show that job hunting began before graduation and continues genuinely. A school recommendation, favorable residence record, funds, graduation evidence, and documented applications or interviews can be central.

## Ordinary duration and the supported second year

The ordinary continued-search route is generally granted for six months and can generally be extended once, allowing up to one year after graduation when the requirements continue.

A different supported route can allow an additional year when the person participates in a qualifying employment-support project operated by a local government and receives the required certification. Do not assume every municipal career program qualifies.

## Waiting after receiving an offer is separate

If employment is already secured but begins later, examine the pre-employment waiting designation rather than describing the person as still searching. Current guidance generally examines whether:

- the employment begins within one year after the offer and within eighteen months after graduation;
- the planned duties are expected to qualify for a work-authorized status;
- the person's residence history is compliant and living expenses are supported; and
- the employer undertakes to maintain contact and report if the offer is cancelled.

## Paid work and internships

Continued job hunting does not itself grant unrestricted work. A person may apply for Permission to Engage in Activity Outside the Status ([資格外活動許可](/glossary/shikakugai-katsudo-kyoka), **shikakugai katsudō kyoka**).

ISA distinguishes blanket permission for qualifying paid activity up to twenty-eight hours per week from individual permission for activity outside that format, including some paid internships. Contract work or self-employment whose hours are difficult to verify can require individual permission. Read the permission actually granted; do not reuse the Student-status assumptions from before graduation.

## Evidence to organize

- graduation or expected-graduation evidence and the qualifying credential;
- the school's current recommendation and any course-to-work explanation;
- job-search records such as applications, interviews, employer communications, and a continuing plan;
- funds and continuing residence records;
- the offer, duties, start date, and employer undertaking when waiting to begin work; and
- any outside-activity application and detailed internship or contract evidence.

## Plan the transition before the deadline

A job offer does not automatically change the authorized activity. Compare the actual duties with a work status and file the appropriate change before beginning work. If the search ends, the offer is cancelled, the school withdraws support, or the maximum period approaches, reassess the lawful next step promptly.
